Overview

Primer

Start here: the whole setting in one read — what is actually true, how it got this way, and who the player characters work for. Everything after this page is detail.

Signal to Noise is a modern-day cosmic-horror procedural: GURPS, TL8, fully lethal, set in present-day Europe. The player characters are a fresh response squad of ANSCOM — a covert agency that handles psionic incidents — working a continental circuit from a hub office in Vienna. The tone is creeping cosmic dread first, procedural casework second. This page is the whole picture, including the things nobody in the setting knows.

What is actually true

Reality is dualistic: the material world, and a second plane called the Undertow — shaped by human feeling, occasionally seeping through into the world. One particle exists in both planes at once: the liminon, the interface between them. The Undertow is inhabited — by thoughtforms congealed from collective feeling and the echoes of the dead, and, exceedingly rarely, by alien Visitors passing through. Its inner layer, the Depths, is non-linear and barely comprehensible; time dilates there.

In deep time, an entity was cast to Earth. The notes call it The Gardener — a GM-doc name; nothing in-world has a word for it, because nothing in-world knows it exists. It engineered an alien plasmid into the hominid line and has been cultivating psionic lineages ever since, the way a breeder improves stock. Its purpose: to grow receivers potent enough to serve as the neurons of its fully instantiated mind — and as vessels for its kind. Its instruments are seeds, fragments of itself buried worldwide, which nudge the pairings, dreams, and migrations of the lineages within their reach, and through which it senses the corrupted at range.

The Gardener is primal — half-awake, its cultivation instinctual, its attention a flicker. But its mind grows with its thralls: nearly every corrupted psi adds cognition. Its current register is anger, so the corrupted trend violent; the long arc runs from instinctive flicker toward cunning, strategy, and — unchecked — open mastery, the rage cooling as it grows.

The biology: psionic aptitude rides a modified mitochondrion, the LAM, which carries a small alien DNA circle coupling the cell's energy machinery to liminons. Every human nervous system carries a feeble baseline form; the enhanced variant rides down maternal lineages, and whether it ever docks is gated by a rare nuclear allele needed from both parents — so carrier lines can run dark for generations. Roughly one person in a hundred thousand manifests: about 80,000 receivers alive today, and rising.

One more thing is true of everyone: every human broadcasts their thoughts psychically, louder with emotion — the hum of a crowd. Human chatter rides a short wavelength; corruption tunes the deep band. The waking crowd never reaches the Gardener — but sleep carries a person's emission deeper, and dreaming sharpens reception. The whispers land there.

How it got this way

The cultivation is visible across millennia: real oracles, witches, and saints; folk belief is garbled truth; haunted places and relic objects are residue of the old work. The séance craze of c. 1850–1910 was the first mass contact with the Undertow — some circles touched it, and things looked back.

In 1892, Hans Berger survived an accident and his sister, far away, knew — he transmitted, she received. His diary account of that event became the foundation of The Berger Programme, the German psi-research effort of the 1930s–40s: breeding registries, a forced-breakthrough doctrine, and the first taxonomy of psis. Its theory crossed to the Americans with the Paperclip paperwork.

The Cold War split the field in two. PALE LANTERN, the US programme, judged the German inheritance foundation enough and went straight to weaponizing — operations, tradecraft, elite operatives. NII-19, the Soviet programme, inherited nothing and had to build a science from scratch — the biology and clinical work, including the discovery of the anomalous neurons and their mitochondria. Both programmes were real and both were productive. Meanwhile, from about 1900, psionic incidents were quietly climbing all century — the GM notes call it the bloom, and nobody in-world has yet recognized it as a phenomenon.

In 1986, Chernobyl: the tumors of Subject 357, NII-19's incurable patient, secreted a psionically reactive substance (Subject 357 Case). In 1987, the Baltic Incident — publicly an oil spill, in truth a self-replicating psionically reactive bio-substance, its hotspot now mostly defused — forced the blocs to the table. JDIT was founded as a UN treaty body, and both Cold War programmes became it (Founding of the JDIT). But each side kept its best back as leverage: the US its elite operatives and advanced techniques, the USSR its prototypes and old-guard personnel. Both sent their juniors — JDIT was founded decapitated, and the 1987 intake, never having had a generation above it, is today's senior leadership.

In 1991 the USSR dissolved and the withheld Soviet material went loose (Dissolution of the USSR); JDIT swept the successor states and recovered most of it, the rest privatized. That recovered material fed JDIT's science until about 2007, then the well ran dry. The research arm's one earned triumph of its own is the liminon, discovered c. 1993–94 (Discovery of the Liminon), which unified the theory and renamed the entire technical vocabulary. And for the last ten years the caseload has climbed, year over year — the bloom's visible face. Offices are understaffed, veterans mutter that something changed, and nobody has connected the curve to a cause.

The official world

Publicly, psi does not exist, and JDIT is a boring international defense initiative that does nothing. The truth is compartmented as CANOPY: all JDIT employees know psi is real. Inside CANOPY, the working theory is that psis pick up an external signal — which is wrong (the powers are cultivated biology), but it named the word "receiver" and the agency itself: Anomalous Signals Command. The irony the org cannot see: the corruption channel genuinely is a transmission — the Gardener broadcasts and the corrupted receive — so ANSCOM named itself for a wrong theory that happens to describe exactly what is eating its agents.

JDIT keeps the field clear: any organization that gets big enough to notice is eliminated or driven back underground. It holds every known hot zone — guarded, not cleaned. ANSCOM, its operations arm, does not publicly exist; it detects, contains, and closes noises, and manages receivers before they go loud.

ANSCOM is genuinely grey, and never a bastion against evil. It collects its fallen operatives' bodies and feeds a brain-salvage amplifier that corrupts its operators. Its psis may only retire by decommissioning — a mandatory injection, Soviet-built and inherited whole in '87, that kills the docked LAMs and takes part of the mind with them; recruits know the policy, not the price. It runs PRUNE, a city-agent program that gets suspected latents addicted to methamphetamine so street chemistry degrades them quietly; it funds itself through TAPROOT, drug-trade logistics facilitation; it keeps DEADWOOD, a secret internal squad that brings down rogue agents; and it wipes the memories of people who brush against CANOPY. It invented neither instrument it turns on its own psis: the monitoring regime is American, the needle Soviet.

And it is caught in a double bind it cannot see. Its rogue-hunting doctrine is correct — every contained corrupted psi genuinely slows the Gardener — but every receiver it recruits and strengthens, and every psi-tech experiment it runs, feeds the enemy. The institution fighting the colonization is its supply line. Worse: corruption is already inside. Independent sleepers — uncoordinated, some of them receive-only and thus invisible to emission monitoring — converge on the entity's wants without any conspiracy existing. That is the first planned arc, The Blight.

Life as a receiver

JDIT rates receivers on the Berger scale: manifest power over ceiling, per power — "a three over five." Telepathy manifests first; that is universal biology. Many receivers lean on crutches — verbal or gestural routines that make manifestation reliable — and are sneered at for it. Psi runs hot: the active neurons are strongly dopaminergic, so using a power feels good and pulls mildly like an addiction, and carrying working LAMs roughly doubles a body's caloric baseline.

The cost is Corruption, and Corruption measures alienation from the human world — which is the same thing as openness to the Undertow. The Gardener is the loudest broadcaster there, so the corrupted usually tune to it. Loudness sets a floor: the stronger the receiver, the more Corruption they carry that can never be cleansed. The door opens from inside: any psi can voluntarily open to the Undertow for a boost, at a price, and every receiver instinctively knows how. The involuntary roads in are hot zones, the Undertow itself, vile encounters, and cursed artifacts. Alienation can recede — above the floor only, through months of deliberately mundane humanity, and any psi use resets the cycle: retire or rot. Past the point of no return lie catatonia, mutation — and, if the body keeps enough energy, a new seed. The network propagates itself.

Every agent is a tragic character by the nature of their powers alone. There is no good way out; the ones who take the needle come out broken. ANSCOM monitors its operatives constantly — psychological evaluation, liminon-signature baselining, LER drift — and careers end at thresholds, quietly.

Where play begins

It is 2026. The PCs are a new ANSCOM response squad covering Europe from the Vienna hub — a new city each case, a recurring home base — joining an understaffed organization during the strain, with veterans muttering and the caseload still climbing. The campaign's deliberate open questions and ambiguities are inventoried in Open Threads; they are to be spent in play, not tidied away.

Table Agreements

The campaign's table contract, set at genesis before any material was written — Signal to Noise is a modern-world (TL8, edges of TL9) GURPS psionics campaign whose premise can be played anywhere from pulp action to grinding horror, so these dials come first.

The contract

  • Tone: creeping cosmic dread (primary); procedural casework (secondary). Cases open mundane and curdle; the Flowering's source bleeds wrongness into everything. Humor happens between operatives, not in the fiction's framing.
  • Every agent is a tragic character by the nature of their powers alone, before whatever personal trouble they bring; there is no good way out, and the ones who take the exit come out broken, depressed, and killing themselves far above the general rate (The LAM, The Needle).
  • Competence and lethality: professional operatives; the world is fully lethal — GURPS rules as written. TL8 firearms and psi both kill; no GM softening of death rolls. Death is always on the table and caution is a survival skill.
  • Lines and veils: none declared. The table is comfortable with dark content on-screen; issues are handled ad hoc if they arise.
  • Co-authoring level: (not yet discussed — carry to a future sitting; Open Threads §Session-0 outputs)
  • Cadence and shape: biweekly sessions, open-ended campaign. No planned endpoint; the metaplot escalates at the pace play sets.

What the contract implies

  • Full lethality plus cosmic dread means PC turnover is expected — the program frame makes replacements natural (a new operative is assigned), and prep should keep that pipeline cheap.
  • Dread needs room to build, but combat is a recurring part of casework, not a rare punctuation: fights can break out during any phase of a case (the group under investigation may come after the squad mid-investigation), and some missions are outright assaults — storming and fighting through a rogue receiver's compound. Combat, whenever it comes, is decisive and dangerous.
  • Open-ended shape means the source's Flowering must be pace-flexible — escalation beats keyed to play, not to a calendar (Doctrine).

Doctrine

GM-craft doctrine for running Signal to Noise — pacing devices, authoring rules, and process rules. Not setting content: nothing here is a fact about the world.

The escalation spine

The Gardener's competence curve — instinctive flicker → tactical cunning → strategy → (unchecked) open mastery — is the campaign's era pacing. The corrupted turning from violent to eerily calm is the players' visible dread beat. Run early-campaign opposition angry and crude; let cold competence arrive gradually and unannounced — players noticing "it's getting smarter" is the intended dread beat. Late-era conserved bodies run as discreet agents arrive only if things get worse (Seeds). The open-ended campaign shape means escalation beats key to play, not to a calendar (Table Agreements).

Authoring rules

  • Fiction attaches to fiction. No atrocities are fabricated onto documented sites or people — Kaltenroda was invented as a Buchenwald-system satellite precisely so the camp's horrors attach to a fictional place. Follow the same rule for any new historical material.
  • The Gardener's fingerprints on history are used sparingly — the horror is that you can never be sure which events were its (The Gardener).

Presentation rules

  • Powers should usually feel embodied. Each psi feels the Undertow's influence differently, set by where their liminon-sensitive neurons concentrate: one smells emotions, another sees them, another knows them abstractly (Corruption §Detection). Give every psi NPC a sensory signature worth one line in their note.
  • Bamf tone mandate and kin: displaced-air effects should unsettle, not amuse (The Closed List).

Process rules

  • Player-safe summaries get copied to Campaign/Handouts/; every other campaign note stays behind the GM screen (gm-only by default).
  • Sitting notes are minutes, not canon storage; rulings need promoting into the owning article (Open Threads §Revision pending).
Canon glossary

Signal to Noise — canon glossary

Modern-world secret-psionics campaign. The vault's vocabulary splits along two axes. The first is in-world versus GM-side: ANSCOM's clipped signals-bureaucracy jargon (what gets said aloud) versus the truth vocabulary (what the notes mean, words nothing in-world possesses) — keep them apart. The second axis runs through the in-world language itself, and it is setting lore in its own right.

How the program talks — the two registers

Two registers run through the program's language. The formal/technical register is what the scientists and the higher-ups write and say; the field register is what agents actually use. The field register is not a stratum of its own — it is a living second register eroding all the others. Field forms arise two ways: by corruption of a formal term (shortening, chewing down — "a B6" becomes "a six") or by native replacement (fresh imagery with no formal parent, the way "the Undertow" displaced "astral plane"). The older a term, the more worn it is; a field coinage dates from when agents started chewing on the term.

Every formal term carries two coordinates — tradition × minting era — from the canonical stratigraphy of the program's history: Occult inheritance (pre-program, up to the 1940s), Berger-German (1930s–40s loanwords), the US program (Cold War, mystical flavor), the Soviet program (Cold War, dry-descriptive), and JDIT bureaucratese (post-1987). Provenance is domain-keyed: the US program was results-focused — practical application in warfare and espionage — so it yielded the ops, tradecraft, and doctrine vocabulary; the Soviet program was scientific, after the whys and hows, so it yielded the physics, biology, and clinical vocabulary; compartments and administration are JDIT's own. Berger-German owns the two things Berger's programme actually theorised: the basic concepts of psi — what the faculty is, what it does, what kinds of people have it — and the race-science vocabulary of heredity and grading that a breeding programme could not do without. A term's subject matter therefore predicts its stratum. The split has a cause: the US inherited Berger's theory through Paperclip and judged it foundation enough to start weaponizing, while the Soviets inherited nothing and had to understand the phenomenon before they could use it (The Berger Scale).

German terms reach the lexicon through the Americans, never directly. PALE LANTERN carried the Jena science largely as it received it — that was the part of the corpus it expanded least, being busy weaponizing rather than theorizing — so the German layer is thickest exactly where American science is thinnest. Most of that layer is nonetheless dead: retired, replaced, or superseded, first by Soviet theory at the '87 merger and then by JDIT's own science. What survives, survives because the field kept saying it — or, in one case, because nobody ever needed to replace it (lineage). (This mirrors real history: Project Stargate's remote-viewing mysticism against the Eastern bloc's dry "psychotronics" and "biocommunication.")

Since JDIT absorbed both Cold War archives in 1987 (Founding of the JDIT), the working lexicon has been a fossil bed — deliberately so, as a design principle: origin × wear locates every term in the program's history, and attentive players can read that history straight out of the slang.

Terms and documentation stay English; the game is played in Portuguese, so every term also carries a settled spoken form for the table — the "PT (table speech)" line in each entry.

Program and clearance

CANOPY

The compartment designation for the psionics secret — a container for information, never a name for any organization. "Read into CANOPY" = knows psi is real; all JDIT employees are CANOPY-read. Written in caps, always.

A post-'87 JDIT compartment codeword, kept English everywhere (NATO-style, caps on paper). JDIT's compartment codewords draw from a themed arboreal series — CANOPY, DEADWOOD, and any future compartments fit the scheme. Real tradecraft forbids indicative codewords; the half-indicative themed series is JDIT's institutional vanity — and a standing GM wink the org cannot see. PT (table speech): "Canópi"; the read-in idiom is the formal "credenciado" — "ela é credenciada?" There is no slang layer: clearance talk stays stiff even in field mouths.

DEADWOOD

Compartment designation for ANSCOM's secret internal investigation and elimination squad — the unit that brings down dangerous or rogue agents (ANSCOM, The Closed List). Most of JDIT does not know the compartment or the squad exists; JDIT's advisory board does. Written in caps, always. Where the field knows of it at all — as rumor — it whispers "the Janitors."

A post-'87 JDIT compartment codeword of the arboreal series (see CANOPY): dead wood is what a healthy forest sheds — and corporate idiom for personnel to be cut. The whisper extends "a Casa": every house has its cleaners. PT (table speech): the codeword stays English; the whisper is "os Faxineiros" — "faxina" already carries the cleanup-detail sense.

PRUNE

Compartment designation for ANSCOM's city-agent addiction program: one dedicated non-psi agent in every major city, working to engender methamphetamine addiction in suspected latent receivers — hooked early and degraded through mundane means, which is usually less detectable and less of a hassle than an ops squad and a cleanup team (The LAM, ANSCOM). Clearance is slightly more relaxed than TAPROOT's. Written in caps, always.

A post-'87 JDIT compartment codeword of the arboreal series (see CANOPY): pruning is the gardener's seasonal cutting-back — scheduled-maintenance vocabulary for standing harm. The program is an open secret: most agents above recruit level know ANSCOM facilitates and encourages addiction by latents, and the knowledge is shared in discreet meetings — cafés and the like — never on paper. The field whisper is the lawn idiom: an agent working a city is "mowing the lawn"; a latent "got mowed"; the agents in charge are "mowers" — "John's been designated as Rome's new mower." PT (table speech): the codeword stays English; the idiom "cortando a grama" / "aparar a grama"; a mower = "o cortador de grama", worn to "cortador".

TAPROOT

Compartment designation for ANSCOM's drug-money machine — the facilitation of smuggling logistics for methamphetamine and other drugs, and the financing it generates: the bulk of ANSCOM's budget (ANSCOM). Clearance is tighter than PRUNE's. Written in caps, always.

A post-'87 JDIT compartment codeword of the arboreal series (see CANOPY): the taproot is the deep root the whole tree feeds through. No field register exists, by ruling. Suspicion runs by age, not rank: veterans who see the logistics up close do the arithmetic eventually, each generation working it out individually and shutting up. Anyone asking too many questions is called into a room by their supervisor and told to drop it; non-compliance leads to decommissioning or flat-out execution. PT (table speech): the codeword stays English.

Sector squad

A station-attached ANSCOM field team working the minor and more protracted investigations of its station's sector — mixed teams, a minor psi or two alongside elite mundane investigators. PT (table speech): "esquadrão de setor".

Response squad

A mobile, psi-heavy ANSCOM field team working a circuit rather than a station, dispatched for the more urgent and/or dangerous cases — the PCs' unit type: a Europe circuit from the Vienna hub. PT (table speech): "esquadrão de resposta".

Squad callsigns

On paper a squad is hub + tier + number: Vienna Response Squad One. On the radio it answers only to its fixed nickname callsign — each squad carries one — with members numbered off the nickname, the senior agent in person speaking as "Actual", and any member addressable as squad callsign + their appointment title. Traffic between stations, or up to The Hague, runs instead on rotating SOI-style callsigns issued per period. Personal nicknames exist but are strictly informal — bestowed, never chosen — and orthogonal to both callsign and title. PT (table speech): callsigns stay English.

Appointment titles

A codeword designation each squad member carries for their specialization — psi specialty or mundane trade — spoken with the squad callsign to raise the function without naming the person or the capability. The title lexicon is unminted.

Restricted handling

A mission classification: the case runs as a regional bypass — the squad answers only to regional command through its handler, and the local station owes support but holds no authority over the operation and may not be fully briefed. Classification keys to severity and urgency and is a discretionary power of command (ANSCOM). PT (table speech): "RH" (the initialism, said in English).

Rank and office

Chief, ANSCOM

The formal designation of the head of ANSCOM — the top of the organization's single title ladder (Chief of Station → Chief of European Operations → Chief, Anomalous Signals Command). Usually addressed as "Commander," even by his superiors. PT (table speech): "o Comandante". Avoid: Director — that title belongs to JDIT's executive director alone.

Field jargon (ANSCOM register)

Decommissioning

The mandatory exit injection — the only retirement ANSCOM allows its psis (The Needle). Non-psi and anti-psi personnel are exempt. Disclosed at signing as a routine, classified, safe-sounding medical procedure; the price is not.

The formal term is post-'87 JDIT bureaucratese — equipment language for people. The field says "getting dimmed" — lifted from the org's own assets-too-valuable-to-dim idiom — and "the needle", the injection standing for the whole exit. PT (table speech): formal "descomissionamento" — the oil-rig/power-plant word; field "ser apagado" — "apagar" doubles as BR slang for killing, and the field word refuses to distinguish — and "a agulha".

Psi

The field's word for the whole business — the faculty, the phenomenon, the people ("a psi"). Occult-inheritance stratum: in agents' mouths since the 1940s, when PALE LANTERN's field said it while the papers said far-sense — and it has outlived every formal term stacked on top of it; today the papers say liminic function and the field still says psi. PT (table speech): "psi" — invariant.

Receiver

ANSCOM's term for a psionically active person. Reflects the (incorrect) working theory that psis pick up an external signal.

Standard vocabulary in both registers, and the great survivor of the Berger-German stratum. Hans Berger coined the pair in his own diary account of the 1892 event — he transmitted, his sister acted as the receiver — and his programme built its taxonomy on it: psis come in two kinds, Sender and Empfänger, emitters and receivers. PALE LANTERN took the theory through Paperclip, translated Empfänger straight, and kept only that half of the pair, because a person who picks things up is the half an intelligence service can use. JDIT inherited the word already worn smooth. It stuck where the rest of the German lexicon died for a plain reason: agents say it fifty times a day, and daily words are not replaced by committee.

Old and heavily worn, and wrong twice over — the external-signal theory is incorrect, and everyone emits, all the time (Psi in the World): nobody was ever only a receiver, so the discarded half of Berger's pair turns out to have been the more nearly right one. Datable evidence for attentive players. PT (table speech): "receptor".

Noise

Field term for any psionic incident, from a bent spoon to a burning precinct — the unit of ANSCOM casework. "We've got a noise in Graz." Strictly field: no document says "noise."

A native replacement in the field register — signals imagery, something audible where there should be silence — minted in bloom-era ANSCOM and still young. Its formal parents are a three-layer stack: liminic event (CANOPY-compartmented — coined from the liminon; internal memos) and anomalous event (bland any-clearance bureaucratese — external-facing documents, plus some internal use, ANSCOM more than other JDIT divisions). PT (table speech): field "um barulho" — "deu barulho em Graz"; formal "evento limínico" / "evento anômalo".

Hot

Field slang: a site is "hot" / "running hot" when it reads elevated liminic flux — or when it is corrupted; the field cannot tell and does not care (formal splits, field lumps). A corrupted site is a "hot zone" in agent speech, and "hot" serves all cases at all levels — no official JDIT technical term exists yet; the report behind the slang says liminic flux, elevated.

A native replacement, field register: Geiger-counter culture riding the Soviet instrument lineage; bloom-era. PT (table speech): "quente" — "o lugar tá quente", "área quente".

The hum

Field lingo for the ambient civilian thought-broadcast — the psychic murmur of a crowd (Psi in the World): "the hum in Shinjuku is unbearable." Alternate: "the babble." PT (table speech): "o zumbido"; alternate "o burburinho".

Going loud

Informal term for a receiver — or a whole group — risking CANOPY reveal, voluntarily or involuntarily: public manifestation, a case boiling toward witnesses, an agent losing control. "Manage receivers before they go loud" (ANSCOM). Distinct from loudness (Corruption) — Undertow audibility; same imagery, different channel. PT (table speech): "estourar".

Classified science (CANOPY-compartmented vocabulary)

Liminon

The subatomic particle existing simultaneously in the material and astral planes; the interface between them. Neutrino-like and near-non-interactive except when organized by psionic operation. Discovered by JDIT's research effort c. 1993–94 — the coincidence experiment that unified the theory and seeded the whole limin- vocabulary (Discovery of the Liminon).

Formal register only — post-'87 JDIT science wing, coined in the Soviet-descended clinical idiom. The particle name has no field erosion: agents never handle the noun. Field contact is via readings — formal liminic flux ("elevated flux at the site"); the field names the result, not the particle (see Hot). PT (table speech): "limínon" (penult stress, elétron pattern); "fluxo limínico".

Liminic function

The capacity itself — the faculty of psionic operation, named for the liminons it organizes (Liminon). It is a quantity, not a yes/no: it is spoken of as high or low, and it tracks the manifest half of a rating, so a receiver with a strong B-number is said to have high liminic function (The Berger Scale).

Formal register only — a coinage of the c. 1994–95 renaming cascade, in the Soviet-descended clinical idiom (Discovery of the Liminon). It renamed the bioplasmic function, the last of the faculty's dead formal names (Far-sense, Fernsinn). No field form: agents say psi. PT (table speech): "função limínica" — "função alta".

LFI

Liminically functional individual — a person with measurable liminic function (Liminic function). The science wing's term, not the agency's: it is what the research and clinical staff write and say, and it is one of the cleanest tells of which building someone works in. An agent who says "LFI" out loud has either come from the labs or is being sarcastic about someone who did. Ops says receiver; the field says psi.

Formal register, post-'87 JDIT bureaucratese in the science wing's idiom; sibling to LER and CLM. Spoken as its letters, "el-eff-eye" — no field erosion, because the field does not use it.

The same apparatus gives the screening idiom: an assay that finds liminic function comes back liminic-positive, and the phrasing has spread past the labs because dates attach to it — "she tested positive at fourteen" (Assay). That one does erode: a positive.

PT (table speech): the initialism spoken as letters, "éle-éfe-i"; screening "limínico-positivo", ordinarily just "deu positivo" ("testou positivo aos catorze"); field "um positivo".

Berger scale

JDIT's rating for receivers: manifest power over ceiling (potential), per power — "a Berger 3, ceiling 5 telepath" — with an overall figure in dossier headlines. Written B3/C5, spoken "a three over five." Named for Hans Berger. Assays estimate ceiling imperfectly.

Formal register, used by both — a composite term, and the two halves carry different stratum coordinates. The name and the manifest band are US-program stratum, science domain: inherited from Berger's own Jena proto-scale through Operation Paperclip. The ceiling half is Soviet-program stratum: the neuron-count assay (Liminically active neurons, Assay). The two were welded at the '87 standardization. Before the weld the Soviet program rated on the bioplasmic index, dead since the standardization (Bioplasmic index). Field erosion: spoken "a three over five"; bare "a six" for B6. PT (table speech): "escala Berger"; spoken "um três sobre cinco"; ceiling = "teto" ("um telepata Berger 3, teto 5"); field "um seis".

Assay

Concept file

Corruption

Alienation from the human world, measured — the openness to the Undertow that power creates, the Gardener exploits, and JDIT monitors without understanding

Corruption measures alienation — the degree to which a psyche has become alien to the mundane world and to human experience. It is not inherently Gardener colonization: colonization is one road in, but any door — power growth, Undertow exposure, weird events — deposits the same substance. All mechanics: Rules/house/corruption.md; every number there is provisional pending the calibration sitting (Open Threads §Chores).

What is true

The openness model

Alienation opens the mind to The Undertow. Corruption is the opening; what comes through depends on who is broadcasting. The Gardener is by far the strongest and closest broadcaster, so the corrupted usually pick up its proto-mind and tune to its primal desires — usually, but not necessarily. Other presences (the Undertow's residents, the Visitors) can capture a corrupted mind that strays into their reach. A corrupted psi is not automatically an enemy asset — "whose signal is he picking up?" is a live question per case, and the rare non-Gardener wavelength is premium plot inventory.

The tuning evolves with the Gardener: today the corrupted are merely tuned to its erratic primal desires; as it grows more sophisticated, its influence over corrupted psis becomes stronger and more calculated, trending toward direct control. Its flickering attention has a mechanical face: the tuning waxes and wanes with its focus, not with the victim's total alone. The blessing's trap is a loop: the Gardener's whispering is a source of Corruption, and Corruption is what makes a mind exploitable. Contact corrupts; corruption opens.

Reception and emission are coupled — the more corrupted a psi, the more sensitive they become to Undertow signals, and, almost always, the more they emit on the same wavelength. This is the antenna reciprocity of the coherence-gated emission model (The LAM §The emission axiom).

Corruption converts into mental disadvantages drawn from a curated, weighted list aligned with the Gardener's ethos: disadvantages matching its current register are more probable (e.g. Bloodlust while it is angry), misaligned ones less. The weights are era-dependent — as the entity's emotional register cools, the table drains from hot violence (Bloodlust, Bad Temper, Berserk) toward cold calculation — so the "eerily calm late-campaign corrupted" tell emerges from the dice. Conversion is permanent: the substance stabilizes by becoming you (The Needle, "you're this, now").

Power estranges

Growing very powerful changes the receiver: the higher the power, the more alien the person becomes, mentally and even physically. This is the cultivation showing (The Gardener) — potency is progress toward being usable substrate, and it does not come free. Estrangement drift is the Corruption total: one substance through many doors. PC advancement carries a horror tax — buying up potency is buying toward alienation, and the GM narrates drift (sleep, empathy, appetite, anatomy — scaled to points in psi) as powers grow. High-power NPC receivers are presumptively strange — rogue cases at the top end are body-horror cases by default.

JDIT knows the pattern (empirically — not its cause) and monitors its psionic operatives closely and frequently: psychological evaluation, physical examination, liminon-signature baselining. ANSCOM careers end at thresholds, quietly — and the monitoring thresholds of The Berger Scale (clinically notable ~B6, career review ~B8) get their endpoint at The Needle. JDIT cannot reliably distinguish power-driven estrangement from breaks caused by weird experiences (Undertow contact, trauma from a noise) — the monitoring regime treats strong receivers and weird-case survivors with the same nervous scrutiny, because the etiology split was never real: all roads feed the same Corruption. The jargon: one clinical state — liminic overexposure syndrome (its proper name still unminted, Open Threads); field says "deaf" (Liminic overexposure syndrome). JDIT's diagnostic blindness is structural — there is genuinely nothing to split.

Loudness sets a floor

Every psi has a Corruption floor — a minimum total that cannot be bought off or cleansed below. Power and potential make a mind loud in the Undertow, and the loud are whispered-at whether or not they ever act: alienation is the standing cost of being audible. (Physically this is antenna reciprocity.)

Potential counts — but only Undertow-linked potential. Talent in powers that work through the Undertow contributes to loudness even if never manifested; Talent in powers that merely organize liminons locally does not (full taxonomy: The Closed List §Undertow linkage) — an unmanifested kinetic is silent; an unmanifested telepath is already an open ear.

  • Since every receiver has Telepathy Talent ≥ 1, every receiver has a real floor — there are no clean receivers, only quiet ones.
  • A never-manifested high-Talent latent carries a real floor and rolls against it: civilians who go quietly mad, hearing voices, without ever manifesting — a canonical case-genesis JDIT will misfile, since there is no manifest power to flag. The latent-madness genesis quietly seeds cases for JDIT files and ties the civilian world to the metaplot without any manifest event.
  • The rolls machinery grinds on the floor forever: estrangement is a machine, not a narration. Conversion clamps at the floor — the rule that converting Corruption reduces the running total applies only to the above-floor margin. A psi who keeps using their powers is never done being subject to corruption.
  • Advancement prices alienation in: raising your band raises your floor, permanently. No discipline keeps a strong psi clean.

The door opens from inside

Any psi can open themselves to the Undertow for power — and almost everyone instinctively knows how. The maneuver takes one extra second, then the power simply arrives: no FP cost, no extra-effort roll — a dark variant of psi extra effort, deliberately far more effective than the legitimate version; that is what makes it tempting. Two modes, both priced in dice of Corruption, not fixed points — the gamble is the point; deterministic costs would read as a fee, not a risk: skill boost and level-borrowing. Borrowed power ignores the Berger ceiling — a documented C3 can perform C6 work, for a price. Door-opened (and borrowed/stolen) power is what reaches the impossible tier no unaided human touches (The Closed List §The impossible tier). Every desperate moment is a devil's bargain the player strikes with themselves — the oppression is interactive, not just ambient.

Critical failures on any psi use (all powers, Undertow-linked or not) are the involuntary version: the channel flaps open with nobody holding the door. Corruption gain is the default critical-failure special effect — strange manifestations and other punishments may stack on top, but Corruption always accrues.

JDIT tracks above-ceiling manifestation as a standing red flag for all agents: receivers repeatedly performing above assay are presumed to be going bad. Assay error is bounded at one band, so mismeasurement innocently explains only modest overshoots — repeated multi-band performances have no innocent explanation JDIT knows of. The dossier ambiguity lives in the one-band margin (and, unknown to everyone, in The Astral Corpus).

Alienation can recede

Above-floor Corruption recedes only through sustained, deliberate, mundane humanity — ordinary life, human connection, no exposure. The penance-shadow, secularized. Cleansing runs in cycles, and any use of psionics resets the current cycle's progress to zero. Not psi failure — psi use. The door must stay shut.

  • Retire or rot is the standing operative's dilemma: the job is psi use, and psi use is the reset trap. Working operatives effectively cannot cleanse — hope exists, and its price is the campaign.
  • JDIT-mandated leave is a real institution: pulled off rotation, forbidden to manifest, sent somewhere quiet. Whether an operative complies off the books is a story every time. A PC who wants to come back from the edge must genuinely stop being a psi for months of play-time — the mechanic enforces the theme.
  • Exotic mercies exist but are unkeyed — probably certain less-malignant intelligences in The Undertow can lift Corruption. What they are, what they want, and what they cost is a deliberate ambiguity — plot inventory, do not resolve casually (Open Threads §Deliberate ambiguities).
  • Mental Surgery cannot touch corruption-converted disadvantages. Excising them is a dedicated psi technique in godlike-feat territory — theoretically possible, in practice impossible — and any failure on the attempt is a critical failure. "You're this, now" stands; the asterisk exists so that somewhere in the deep files, someone has tried.

("Openness" is GM-side shorthand; the in-world clinical term is the LER — liminon-evoked response (LER), and drift from baseline is "LER drift.")

(In theory you could just ask the psi "did you feel that?" — in practice JDIT and ANSCOM do not trust their agents enough to run it on self-report.) This is the concrete procedure behind liminon-signature baselining.

JDIT knows openness can indicate growing corruption — especially marked increases. But the LER is an indicator, never a diagnosis. The corruption signal is a marked change in profile — especially toward certain wavelengths — and it is confounded: growing skill as a telepath or astral projector also yields better LER results. A drifting profile is one data point among several (psych evals, physicals, time in service) that ANSCOM weighs when deciding between mandatory leave and permanent decommissioning. A naturally sensitive psi and a corrupting one look identical in absolute terms — loudness is baseline openness; etiology never shows; totals stay GM-secret. The diagnostic fog survives measurement. Monitoring scenes get a physical procedure: the tube, the emitter, the tech reading the bloom on the scan — an operative hiding growth is hiding it from a machine.

Living psis almost never perceive Corruption. Very sensitive, very skilled telepaths or astral projectors MIGHT read a specific receiver as unusually open to signals that feel wrong — rare and unformalized: the felt read is not institutional knowledge anywhere in JDIT. Deep-corruption veterans are walking sensor arrays: they hear more than clean psis, which makes the corrupted genuinely better at parts of the job. The blessing again. Each psi feels the Undertow's influence differently, set by where their liminon-sensitive neurons concentrate: one smells emotions, another sees them, another knows them abstractly — presentation guidance in Doctrine.

The Undertow side: the emission is how the Gardener senses the corrupted — its Seeds are very powerful sensors spread around the world. It reads wavelength alignment easily; for a human psi the same read takes extraordinary capability. What JDIT knows on this channel: certain broadcasts echo through the Undertow; it suspects some of them are malign; and it can sometimes detect a psi emitting on those wavelengths — unreliably, and more easily the more powerful and less controlled the psi. This instrumental capability is as far as institutional knowledge goes.

Some very rare psis only receive — corrupted, tuned to it, and leaving no Undertow footprint. The emission channel cannot find them; their entire risk surface is behavioral. These are the Enemy's natural sleeper agents (The Blight) — not a coordinated cell (the Gardener cannot yet run one): its will simply becomes theirs, unconsciously, and their actions converge without any of them knowing the others exist. The sleepers are not caught, they are churned: an erratic sleeper gets removed as a misbehaver or an overexposure-syndrome case, never identified as enemy influence. The Gardener needs no roster — it made them, and its broadcast is one-way. From outside, convergence is indistinguishable from conspiracy.

The end of the line

Past the point of no return the trajectory is terminal: catatonia, mutation, and — if the body retains enough energy — a new seed. The whole terminal stage, JDIT's clinical picture of it, and the era-gated conservation rule live in Seeds.

Detection shows in their own monitoring sessions, a veteran's "something's wrong about him" that turns out true, the case where a screen came back clean and shouldn't have. And one day a PC's own disadvantage roll lands and the GM describes what it felt like to receive.

Psi in the World

How psi sits in humanity — one receiver in a hundred thousand, a suspecting public, a broadcasting crowd, and millennia of cultivation blooming in the 20th century

Psi is real, alien in origin, and officially nonexistent. Receiver frequency is Uncommon: about 1 per 100,000 — at the 2026 population, roughly 80,000 worldwide, a few hundred in the largest metros, at least one in every significant nation, and rising since the 20th-century bloom.

What is true

Ancient cultivation, modern bloom

The Gardener has been on Earth since deep time (Fall); its cultivation of human lineages spans millennia of recorded and oral history. Real receivers are scattered through history — some oracles, witches, saints, and "hauntings" of legend were genuine; folk beliefs (second sight, the evil eye, blessed bloodlines) are garbled, accidentally-true memories of the selected lineages (Lineages). The breeding program reached critical mass in the 20th century: psionic phenomena have been getting significantly more common since then, which is why containment institutions arose when they did (Founding of the JDIT).

Psi is the only living supernatural power, but its history left residue: haunted places, relic objects, echoes of dead receivers — astral seepage (The Undertow). Cases can feel occult on the surface while the cause stays single: everything traces back to psi and its cultivation — one living force in a two-plane world. Historical research is a live investigative avenue: archives, hagiography, witch-trial records, and parish genealogies contain real signal, and every residue case is, ultimately, a receiver's echo.

The nature of psi

Psi is scientific (detectable, measurable, ultimately blockable) and not strictly mental — the body is fair game. Advanced receivers can manifest physical changes; body horror is a signature of late-stage cultivation, and body-horror manifestations telegraph advanced cultivation — a visual escalation ladder for the metaplot. Psi runs at standard Psionic Powers strength (Rules/house/character-generation.md) — strong enough that rogue receivers are genuinely dangerous under full lethality.

A suspecting world

Public awareness is Suspected: the world officially agrees psi does not exist — no conclusive proof, no clean media capture — but roughly 1 in 10 people wholeheartedly believes, and a quarter of those say so openly. Charlatans abound, some quietly sponsored by JDIT specifically to discredit the idea. Receivers need to keep what they are secret (Secret (Psi) [-30] — ANSCOM).

  • Uncommon frequency naturally degrades secrecy toward "suspected" and defeats all-encompassing factions — which is exactly the world as chosen: JDIT's monopoly is policy, not omniscience. Free receivers, believer networks, and small underground groups exist everywhere ANSCOM isn't looking.
  • The disbelief shield: most people write off psi effects as coincidence, so discreet receivers get away with a great deal — but a true believer may recognize the real thing.
  • Word-of-mouth makes receivers organize for self-protection — the minor organizations JDIT mows down are a permanent crop, not a finite list.
  • Debunker NPCs (Quester / Skeptic / Gainsayer) are a standing cast resource; some are JDIT assets.

Public reaction, if the truth broke: fascinated dread. Not uniform persecution — real fear laced with celebrity worship, cults, and recruitment frenzies. Exposure outcomes are unpredictable, which is precisely what JDIT dreads: chaos, not pogroms. An outed receiver faces a lottery — mob, media circus, cult adoption, or a foreign recruiter's van, sometimes all in one week. (And a public revelation would seed The Undertow with new thoughtforms — a hidden cost nobody has priced.)

Potential is inborn (Lineages) — with one secret exception: an undefined fringe actor holds a crude, dangerous psi-induction method (Open Threads §Deliberate ambiguities).

Everyone is broadcasting

Every civilian broadcasts their thoughts, all the time — more intensely the stronger the emotion. (Mechanically: Easy to Read on the standard human template — Rules/house/character-generation.md.)

The buy-off is ANSCOM training, granted free. Part of the training ANSCOM gives prospective agents is learning to shut off the noise of the crowds — and non-psi ANSCOM personnel get the same training: in an agency whose enemies include telepaths, being unreadable is basic opsec. Civilians at large are stuck with the trait — they don't know there is anything to train. The mental-discipline curriculum is one thing in fiction and two Unusual Backgrounds on the sheet; the UBs are a class, not an ANSCOM monopoly — rival psi-agency training grants either under other names, and an analyst can hold trained silence without the combat UB, so the desk/field seam falls out mechanically.

Screens slip. Keeping the screen up is an acquired habit, not a switch: temporary degradation of cognitive capacity — especially prefrontal function, from any source (exhaustion, alcohol, sedation, concussion, terror) — lowers the screen's effectiveness or drops it entirely. Some very experienced people have the habit so ingrained it cannot drop. A slipped screen is a security breach: whatever degrades cognition makes an operative readable again — and an adversary who knows that can arrange the degradation. Hospitals, bars, and interrogation rooms are all soft points.

Trained silence is itself a signature: to a psi who knows what to listen for, an ANSCOM-trained mind in a civilian crowd is a hole in the noise — and very experienced rogue psis know it, using the silence as a tell to spot and avoid ANSCOM and its agents. The hunters' opsec is the prey's tripwire.

Wavelength separation. Human minds broadcast on a much "shorter wavelength" (metaphorically) than the deep-Undertow signals that openness tunes to:

  • Corruption does not amplify crowd noise — the corrupted are more sensitive to signals from deeper in the Undertow, not to the surface chatter of minds.
  • This is why mundane minds are dim to The Gardener: it listens on the deep band, and the crowd's waking noise never carries there. Receivers are loud where it lives; waking civilians are loud only in the room.

Sleep is the exception, by phase. Dreams touch the Undertow — the dream sea is the Undertow — and the sleep state changes the coupling (The LAM §Transduction). In slow-wave sleep, the brain's global synchrony phases the baseline machinery and the sleeper's emission carries deeper: sleeping civilians are marginally louder toward the Depths, and so slightly more visible to the Gardener than waking ones. In REM, reception sharpens instead: sleepers are more susceptible to emissions from the Depths — which is where the Gardener's broadcast originates; its whispering lands here. Flavor, no mechanical rider.

The relief is part of the hook. Recruits whose telepathy manifested before recruitment lived immersed in the murmur; ANSCOM's training is the first silence of their adult lives. The org knows this — the gift of quiet helps it sell itself as genuinely concerned with its operatives' well-being, and the gratitude is a loyalty instrument it doesn't have to fake. Untrained and rogue telepaths are people drowning: crowds are torment, riots and panics are deafening — sympathy and horror in the same case file, and a texture for why self-taught receivers (The Berger Scale's crutch class) are strange before they are dangerous. Emotion scales volume: a telepath reads a poker table poorly and a panicking mob overwhelmingly — crowd scenes have a psychic weather report.

How it is known in-world

Genealogy work on case families reaches absurdly far back; a residue case's echo behaves like a receiver; ANSCOM's own statistics show the 20th-century curve when someone finally plots them. The broadcast, the players lived: the training montage, the first quiet crowd — and every PC remembers the day the noise stopped. The other side arrives with the first rogue telepath who never got the gift, or the first time a target's panic gets loud enough to hurt.

The Berger Scale

JDIT's B/C power ladder — a US-carried capability band welded by treaty to a wholly independent Soviet biological assay, wrong by a band either way

JDIT rates every receiver on the Berger scale: manifest rating and ceiling, written B3/C7, spoken "a three over seven" (Berger scale). The rating is properly per power — "a Berger 3, ceiling 5 telepath" — with an overall figure quoted in dossier headlines. Band table and build mechanics: Rules/house/berger-scale.md.

What is true

Ceiling is estimated by assay — the percentage of liminically active neurons — but the correlation is imperfect: more organelle usually means more potential, not always. A measured ceiling can be wrong by a band either way. The assay reads docked, working complexes, not carriage (The LAM §Universal carriage): a gate-closed carrier and baseline humanity read baseline.

Telepathy comes first. Every receiver manifests some telepathic aptitude before anything else: minds are liminon-active matter (even baseline minds couple feebly), so affecting them is cheap. Manifesting kinetic effects means organizing vastly more liminons against their native non-interactivity (The Undertow) — it is far easier to control someone's mind than to move a coin.

Converting potential into manifest power is training the nervous system to fire in specific patterns — muscle memory for the mind. During training, some receivers develop crutches: verbal and/or gestural routines (mudras, mantras, sketched signs) that make manifestation deliberate and reliably repeatable (Crutch). ANSCOM trains its agents to push past crutches; it doesn't always succeed, especially with receivers who self-trained before recruitment. Crutch-use carries real stigma inside the psionic community — the crutch economy creates visible class lines inside ANSCOM (polished academy graduates vs. field-recruited self-taught talents).

The two parents

The scale is named for Hans Berger — the inventor of the EEG, who built it trying to prove telepathy existed, and who spent his last three years running a Reich programme at Jena to breed and force psionic supermen out of captured subjects (The Berger Programme, The Jena Lab).

The scale has two parents, and that is why it is a slash-pair. Berger built a proto-scale in the Jena programme (1938–41): bands of demonstrated capability, graded by what a subject could actually be made to do, because capability was the only thing his instruments could reach. US forces stripped the lab in 1945 and recruited surviving programme scientists through Operation Paperclip; that lineage — PALE LANTERN — carried the proto-scale, the banding logic, and the name. It became the manifest rating.

The Soviets took the site that July (Jena Inheritance), and what they recovered from it was not data. It was a fact: that the Germans had believed they had proof, and had been experimenting on that belief. No results, no method, no theory — an existence proof, and a second-hand one. That bought a small programme and no more (NII-19). Soviet leadership was pragmatic and the proposition was speculative, so the work started minor, stayed minor, and produced little for years. The break was intelligence, not science. The Soviets learned of the US programme and then infiltrated it — at relatively low clearance, the same trade they had worked against Manhattan. Modest access was enough for the only question that mattered: the Americans had succeeded. From that moment it was another arms race, and the Party funded it like one.

What the money bought was still entirely Soviet work. Having inherited no theory, they had to build one — biologically rather than behaviourally — and arrived pre-'87 at the anomalous neuron type (Discovery of the Psi Neuron) and at the assay: ceiling estimated from the percentage of those neurons — now formally the liminically active neurons (Liminically active neurons). That became the ceiling.

The notation is a fossil of the two doctrines. The Americans, handed a working theory, judged it foundation enough and went straight to application — a programme organized around warfare and espionage, and a rating that measures what an asset can be made to do. The Soviets, handed nothing, had to understand the phenomenon before they could use it — the physics, the biology, the clinical picture, and a rating that measures what a person is. Each bloc contributed the half its doctrine made it good at, which is why the domain-keyed stratigraphy in GLOSSARY works at all: ops vocabulary is American because the Americans skipped the theory, and the science vocabulary is Soviet because the Soviets had no choice but to do it.

The 1987 standardization welded them together. B/C notation is a US-carried name and capability band bolted to a wholly independent Soviet biological assay — two research traditions with no common ancestor past a rumour, forced into one notation by a treaty. That is why the halves disagree often enough that "a measured ceiling can be wrong by a band either way" had to become a standing rule. They are not measuring the same thing, they never were, and nobody at the merger was in a position to say so out loud.

Case files, NPC dossiers, and threat assessments all get a shared shorthand — and assay error means a "C3" rogue can turn out to be a C6. Berger himself is a deep-history thread tying the psychical-research era (Psi in the World) to JDIT's compartmented science.

The LAM

The liminically active mitochondrion — the modified organelle every human carries, whose docked, enhanced form is the seat of psi

The seat of psi is a modified mitochondrial subtype — formally the liminically active mitochondrion, "the LAM" in JDIT's own shorthand. Morphologically it is an ordinary mitochondrion — nothing to see under any microscope. Every human nervous system carries it; what separates a receiver from the rest of humanity is not carriage but what its complex docks to.

What is true

The organelle

Beside the standard, fully ordinary human mitochondrial genome the LAM carries one or more copies of a small, separate, circular DNA element — the plasmid (GM-doc name; its in-world formal name is unminted, Open Threads) — whose gene sequences match no known life: The Gardener's engineering signature (Fall).

The plasmid brings no machinery of its own. Its genes sit behind mimics of the human mitochondrial promoter sequences and are transcribed, translated, and replicated entirely by the host mitochondrion's ordinary equipment. Two consequences:

  • The genes are necessarily written in the mitochondrial genetic code, which differs from the nuclear code — so any fragment that migrated to the nucleus would be untranslatable there. The trait cannot escape the organelle, and stray alien reads in sequencing data look exactly like the routine artifacts every real pipeline discards.
  • The whole anomaly reduces to one small extra DNA circle and the handful of proteins it encodes. Everything else is standard-issue human mitochondrion.

The proteins assemble into a novel complex built on the photoreceptor template: an alien cofactor clamped in the protein scaffold does the actual liminon coupling (the plasmid also encodes its synthesis enzymes); the protein never touches the physics, it only tunes the cofactor and transduces its state changes.

Universal carriage, graded coupling

Every human nervous system carries the plasmid. It is the Gardener's base engineering, fixed in the species — the breeding program predates Homo sapiens (Fall). What separates a receiver from the rest of humanity is what the complex docks to:

  • Baseline humanity — the undocked complex. The baseline plasmid encodes a complex that assembles but does not dock onto the oxidative-phosphorylation chain: it floats free of the proton-motive force, scraping by on ambient ATP. Underpowered, leaky, untuned — enough for a feeble short-band murmur (the thought-broadcast telepaths read, the feelings that shape The Undertow, the sleep-window drift), nowhere near enough for reception as perception or for directed work.
  • Psi lineages — the docked variant. The Gardener's cultivation (The Gardener) is not handing out the plasmid — everyone has that. It is upgrading it in selected matrilines: enhanced plasmid variants whose complex carries the docking module, seating it on the respiratory chain as effectively a sixth respiratory complex tapping the proton-motive force. Full power, deep-band tuning, real gain.
  • The cliff is bandwidth, not a switch. Baseline coupling is feeble but two-way — subthreshold in both directions. Docked coupling is perceptual and operative. Nobody is at zero.

A neuron rich in docked, working LAMs is a Tarusov–Zemtsova neuron — the anomalous cell type the Soviets identified c. 1970. NII-19 also traced the anomaly to the mitochondria themselves — Tarusov's pre-recruitment work was mitochondria-adjacent, and he looked where he knew — naming the organelle the bioplasmic mitochondrion without ever finding what made it different (Discovery of the Psi Neuron). Baseline machinery is far below what that era's instruments — or a routine modern assay — can see. The assay's "count" is read on neural tissue and reads docked function, not carriage: it counts liminically active neurons — neurons rich in docked, working complexes. In a receiver, enhanced carriage and docked function coincide, so the number serves. A carrier whose gate is shut has no docked complexes and reads baseline, as does baseline humanity; carriage itself is found only by a neural-sample plasmid probe (JDIT's lineage pipeline). The numbers the assay returns for each state, and the band caveat of The Berger Scale, stay open (Open Threads).

Death erases the evidence. When the modified mitochondria running docked complexes die — The Needle's overstimulation, or chronic stimulant erosion — the cell's own disposal machinery clears them and digests the enhanced plasmid with them. Capability degradation is therefore also evidence destruction: a needled brain reads like anyone else's, and a PRUNE target sanitizes slowly as the addiction grinds (ANSCOM).

It feels good, and it costs

Liminically active neurons are strongly dopaminergic. Using a power is therefore pleasurable, and mildly addictive — a low-level pull, always there, in every receiver. Power use also strains the body, and the body answers with endorphins — continuous use especially. Psis effectively get a runner's high off working, on top of the dopaminergic reward.

A psi's metabolism is far more calorie-hungry than an ordinary person's — roughly double, as a floor. That is the resting cost of carrying working, docked LAMs at all: the complex has to be docked to draw it, so baseline humanity — and a carrier whose nuclear gate is shut — pays nothing and eats like anyone else. Above the floor the figure rises with power use and with power potency, and heavy psi use carries massive caloric costs. Mechanics: Rules/house/character-generation.md §Increased Consumption.

Psis eat constantly and conspicuously — logistics, cover, and field endurance all acquire a mundane texture that is hard to hide.

The cells meet that demand through a pathway no other neuron has. Cells running docked complexes pull and consume far more energy from the blood than ordinary neurons can, and their ATP cycles run much faster and more efficiently. This special endocrine pathway is what makes the organelle affordable at all — and it is exactly the route the decommissioning drug uses to reach these neurons and no others (The Needle). The demand sits where metabolism happens: the LAM is the neuron's own modified mitochondrion, paying for every act of organization in membrane potential and running hot to compensate.

Ordinary methamphetamine already bites. Repeated use degrades psi capability, slowly. This is what pointed NII-19 at the drug class in the first place, and it is true of street supply as much as of anything a laboratory makes. The degradation is mitochondrial death, and it erases the molecular evidence as it goes.

Transduction — how activation becomes information

  • Reception, organelle to cell: a liminon event perturbs the cofactor → conformational change → cascade amplification (the rod-cell precedent: biology reliably reports single-particle events this way). The result is handed to the neuron through the two standard mitochondrial currencies: Ca²⁺ microdomains at synapse-adjacent mitochondria (the fast, millisecond path — modulates release probability and excitability) and ATP/metabolic state read by metabolically gated membrane channels (the slow tonic path — the pancreatic-β-cell precedent).
  • Docking is the information cliff as well as the energy cliff. The two currencies are the respiratory chain's own outputs. A docked complex sits in the middle of the machinery that generates them and writes into them at full bandwidth; an undocked baseline complex's cofactor still twitches when liminons arrive, but its state changes barely reach the currencies — trace Ca²⁺ leakage, orders of magnitude below perception, but nonzero. One mechanism prices both the power gap and the perception gap.
  • Cell to mind: no new organ. Liminon events bias release probability and excitability across the neuron population — a patterned perturbation riding on ongoing activity. The real-world precedent is sensory substitution: cortex learns to read any input channel that systematically modulates firing. Undertow input is experienced as percept-without-a-sense-organ — impressions, imagery, "knowing" — and it must be learned: early contact is noise, feeling, and wrongness, and only training (or the self-taught crutches of The Berger Scale) resolves it into content.
  • Why telepathy decodes at all: the civilian murmur is structured by the sender's own firing patterns (see Emission), and sender and receiver both run human neural architecture — a shared codebook. Deep-band signals are horror for the same reason: the Gardener and the Undertow's things perturb the same machinery, but the human brain has no codebook for them. The input lands as dread, compulsion, dream-imagery, alien mood — the Gardener's "whispering to the subconscious" (The Gardener) is deep-band input biasing limbic and affective circuits without ever reaching declarative decoding.
  • Emission: the same machinery driven in reverse — in a docked complex, off the proton-motive force at terrible gain (the calorie economy above lives here; the anomalous oxidative chemistry also leaks the ultraweak photon signature that got the cell type discovered, Discovery of the Psi Neuron). In any firing neuron, membrane-potential swings and Ca²⁺ transients jostle the cofactor's state — so emission mirrors the firing pattern, which is why the murmur carries readable thought rather than static. Emotion scales volume twice over: arousal feeds the machinery more energy, and emotional states synchronize firing across the brain — and superradiance rewards phasing. Baseline emitters get their phasing from neural oscillation — sleep shifts the regime: slow-wave synchrony carries emission deeper, REM sharpens reception (Psi in the World); psis add proton-motive-force power and trained, deliberate organization — directed, deep-band, high-gain.
  • Two routes into behavior. The perceptual route — decoded content — requires docked complexes: receivers only. The subthreshold route — mood, intuition, alienation drift, the blessing's sharpened instincts (The Gardener) — runs in everyone, scaled by coupling. Normals live entirely on the second route and call it feelings. Residue cases touching civilians (The Undertow), hot ground working on whoever stands in it (Hot Zones), and the sleep window all run on this route.
  • Discrimination ("wavelength"): the complex's sensitivity peak is set by the protein microenvironment around the cofactor — spectral tuning, exactly as cone opsins tune one shared chromophore. Variant-tuned LAM populations compared against each other give wavelength discrimination, grounding the tuning/resonance vocabulary and Corruption's LER detection channel.

Inheritance: matrilineal transmission, biparental expression

Mitochondria are maternally inherited, so plasmid variants ride the maternal line only. Everyone inherits a plasmid from their mother; what the cultivated lines transmit is the enhanced variant — a father transmits none of it, and biologically a lineage is a matriline, the Gardener's cultivation having always worked through mothers (The Gardener). The pattern was first noticed empirically by The Berger Programme — "the gift follows the mother".

Carrying the enhanced plasmid is not sufficient. The docked complex needs its assembly factor to fold the subunits into the inner membrane and seat the cofactor on the chain, and that factor is nuclear-encoded. It is not an engineered gene: it is a rare allele of an ordinary human mitochondrial assembly-factor gene — carried by people with no enhanced plasmid and no lineage, doing an unremarkable job. Inherit the permissive allele and the enhanced complex assembles and docks; inherit only the common form and the enhanced plasmid sits inert at baseline. The baseline form self-assembles with common machinery — the gate prices the docked complex only. Ordinary variation across the rest of the nuclear mitochondrial-housekeeping machinery still modulates how well a docked complex runs: the fine dial above the gate.

The gate comes from both parents. Transmission stays matrilineal; manifestation is biparental, and half the gate is the only genetic stake a father has in a receiver.

Docking is post-gestational. The permissive allele's assembly factor runs on an expression program, and the enhanced complex docks when that program fires — the timing of the program is deliberately unpinned. Docking is the main gate to manifesting, but not the only one: actually getting the neurons to fire correctly is the learning part, and it does not always happen for every carrier of the docked complex. The latent — working-LAM density present, short of manifestation — is exactly this state: docked, not yet firing right.

The gate allele is mostly known to JDIT, but its expression is frequently complicated by other factors, so it is not an absolute diagnostic: a genotype narrows the guess about a lineage member without settling it.

Silent carriers therefore exist — people who hold the enhanced plasmid and will never manifest, coupling at baseline like everyone else; the women among them pass the variant on undiminished. A matriline can run dark for generations and surface when a marriage brings the allele back in. Case-mapping genealogy sharpens and then blurs accordingly: matrilines chart cleanly, but a carrier line can produce nothing for generations and then a receiver, so a clean maternal chart predicts badly — Berger's breeding registries, JDIT's lineage files, and any genealogy work at the table all inherit both halves (Lineages).

Heteroplasmy is tissue-restricted: plasmid-bearing mitochondria — baseline and enhanced alike — segregate into neural lineages during development and are near-absent elsewhere. Blood, saliva, and consumer genomics are structurally blind for the whole species; finding the plasmid takes brain tissue and knowing what to look for. The enhanced-carrier population is necessarily larger than the receiver count, and nobody has sized it (Open Threads). Beneath it sits a buried bombshell: with the right molecular probe and brain tissue, everyone tests positive for the plasmid. Whether JDIT knows the carriage is universal is open (Open Threads).

The emission axiom — the setting's one piece of invented physics

Real physics forbids a fixed-coupling particle that is both undetectable and biologically emittable (emission and absorption share one coefficient; biology runs at electron-volt energies). The setting's single invented axiom: the liminon's coupling is coherence-gated — near-zero for matter in ordinary states, real only for phase-coherent arrays. "Organized by psionic operation" (The Undertow) means phase coherence, literally. Only the cofactor couples at all — silicon clocks, lasers, and every other coherent artifact couple to nothing — so computers and machines stay outside the physics entirely (The Closed List). Downstream, real physics supplies the theorems:

  • Superradiance (N² scaling): one cofactor emits nothing measurable; billions phased across a brain emit macroscopically. Baseline arrays reach only the loose, transient phasing that neural oscillation provides — the murmur; docked arrays under deliberate organization emit at incomparable gain. Grounds loudness scaling with the assay count, gestalt pooling and its shared crit-fail, and the amplifier's concentrated-emitter-mass logic (ANSCOM).
  • Antenna reciprocity: an array phased to emit is identically phased to receive. The loudness floor on Corruption and Corruption-as-openness are one consequence, not two decrees — the floor is reciprocity.
  • Warm coherence at body temperature is what the cofactor is engineered to protect (real, contested precedents exist: radical-pair magnetoreception, photosynthetic light harvesting). Alien engineering doing hard engineering, not breaking law.
  • Masquerade lockout: the liminon files under real physics' "hidden sector" vocabulary, and coherence-gating means no accelerator, cavity, or observatory can produce or detect one. The only instrument that couples to liminons is a nervous system — which is why the discovery took a coincidence experiment (Discovery of the Liminon) and why public physics will never find it.

The masquerade is therefore structurally sound: no autopsy, no micrograph, no blood test, no biobank ever stumbles on the secret. Exposure risk concentrates where it is dramatic — brain tissue in the wrong lab, with the wrong question. ANSCOM's body-collection doctrine doubles as biosecurity accordingly, and both instruments that end psi careers — The Needle at once, PRUNE's chemistry slowly — leave a brain that reads like anyone else's.

What stays unsolved, in-world and out

The LAM's biology is understood: the endocrine pathway that feeds it is settled science to anyone cleared to read it — NII-19 worked it out and JDIT has it. JDIT can (eventually) solve the complex's structure atom by atom and still not explain the coupling. The standing wall is, and always has been: how does the LAM control the liminon? The coherence-gating axiom itself is GM-side; no one in-world has it.

How it is known in-world

The biology comes through CANOPY science briefings (the LAM, the assay, the LER); the matrilineal rule through casework genealogy; the nuclear gate only where the genealogy refuses to behave; universal baseline carriage, the plasmid, and the coherence axiom only through late-campaign deep files or the Gardener itself. The endocrine pathway is textbook to the people cleared to read it — the mystery lives entirely at the physics-to-biology join. A receiver learns the feels-good/costs-calories half immediately and from the inside: it is their own biology.

The Needle

The decommissioning injection — the modified methamphetamine that permanently destroys a receiver's docked LAMs, mandatory for every psi leaving ANSCOM

JDIT possesses a drug that disables the enhanced LAM complement — and kills a substantial fraction of the neurons carrying it. An injection is mandatory for every psi leaving ANSCOM; it is the only form of retirement ANSCOM allows its receivers (Decommissioning).

What is true

The drug is based on methamphetamine — the real-world drug whose neurotoxicity is most squarely a matter of overstimulation killing the cells it drives. It overstimulates neurons, much more markedly the ones running docked complexes: the subject manifests erratically over the course of several hours while the overstimulated neurons die off.

It reaches the right neurons through their own feeding pathway. Cells running docked complexes run a specialized endocrine route no other neuron has, pulling far more energy from the blood to pay for the complex (The LAM). The modified methamphetamine travels that route. Cells without the pathway have no door for it, which is why the drug hits working receivers so much harder than everything else — and why baseline humanity, and a carrier whose nuclear gate is shut, have no more door than any neuron.

NII-19 found it by trial and error from an informed start. The programme understood the endocrine pathway — that part was never much of a secret — and it knew the other end of the biology too: powers feel good to use, which said the cells were dopaminergic and named the drug class. Plain methamphetamine is already a little effective — repeated use degrades psi capability, slowly. From that informed starting point the programme worked outward through chemically adjacent substances by trial and error until it had something that did the job in one dose. The needle is an empirical result, not a designed one.

The fever is part of the procedure. The injected run a dangerous hyperthermia through the hours the drug works, and it is not brought down — cooling the subject would spare neurons, and the neuronal death is the point. Whoever is in the room watches a fever they are deliberately not treating.

The needle also destroys the evidence. The dead LAMs are cleared by the cell's own disposal machinery, and the digestion takes the enhanced plasmid with it — a needled brain reads like anyone else's (The LAM). This is what makes needle-then-release compatible with the masquerade: sanitized retirees can die ordinary civilian deaths, autopsies included, with nothing left to find. Chronic street methamphetamine does the same work slowly — a PRUNE target is being erased as well as dimmed (ANSCOM).

Non-psi personnel are exempt — the needle exists to close the docked machinery, and they have nothing to close. Anti-psi personnel — DEADWOOD's included — are not needled either.

The needle is Soviet. The USSR wanted a non-lethal way of permanently disabling psis — originally something usable in the field, but what NII-19 came up with takes too long to take effect. First test 1977, on a psi the programme considered rogue (unkeyed). It crossed to JDIT immediately and in full at the 1987 founding (Founding of the JDIT). JDIT's most intimate instrument of internal control is inherited from the side that lost the negotiation.

The effects are permanent and unrecoverable: attribute damage, all psionic abilities and Talents lost outright — no refund, no recovery, beyond crippling — the power-keyed Corruption floor destroyed and the running total reset, converted disadvantages kept (the corruption has permanently molded the person's brain and psyche — that is what conversion means: "you're this, now"), and no further dreaming. Exact mechanics: Rules/house/corruption.md §The needle.

  • Baseline coupling survives the needle, so the slow environmental doors — hot ground, residue, contact (Corruption) — still work on a retiree, at the much slower pace they work on anyone. Fresh accrual counts from zero; the old total does not return.
  • The injected no longer dream. In a manifest receiver, dreaming becomes LAM-dominated: REM is the sharpened reception phase (Psi in the World), a receiver's REM reception runs at docked grade, and over a career their dreaming is progressively entrained by the docked layer — part experience, part Undertow contact. The needle rips that layer out, and what is left does not reassemble into dreaming. Ex-agents sleep dark; civilians, whose dreaming was never docked-entrained, are untouched.

Injected retirees are no longer viable as PCs. If they appear in play at all, it is to remind the players of the grim cost of doing the work. Those who leave ANSCOM alive always leave it as less than what they were when they came in. Retirement leaves the retiree broken and depressed, and the population's suicide rate is far above the general one.

How it is known in-world

Concept file

The Undertow

The astral plane — feeling-shaped, peopled by thoughtforms and echoes, joined to the material world only through the liminon

Reality is dualistic: the material plane and the astral plane — called the Undertow in ANSCOM field slang, the setting's standard term (The Undertow) — coexist. The Undertow is shaped by people's feelings, and it occasionally affects — or seeps into — the material world.

What is true

The liminon

The interface between the planes is the liminon: a subatomic particle existing simultaneously in both — the only thing that does. Normally it is neutrino-like, near-non-interactive; only when organized by psionic operation does it become interactive with matter — and "organized" means phase coherence, literally (The LAM §The emission axiom). Because of this non-interactivity, human liminon research is biological and empirical, not theoretical physics: JDIT can measure psi without understanding it. The particle was discovered by JDIT's research arm c. 1993–94 (Discovery of the Liminon); the anomalous neuron type had been identified decades earlier by the Soviet programme, which traced its source to the mitochondria and no further (Discovery of the Psi Neuron, The Berger Scale) — the particle's discovery is what let JDIT crack the mechanism and mint the current names (Liminon).

Structure

The Undertow comprises both astral layers of the PP model — the near field (overlapping the real world; "the Shallows" in field speech, where dreamers drift) and the far field (field name: "the Depths"). The far field is associated with abstract concepts and feelings — deeper resonances and echoes of human minds live there, a manifestation of the collective unconscious: shared ideas, myths, and archetypes coalesce into semi-entities. At the far field's "core" sits The Gardener's core — though topography there is non-linear, non-deterministic, and barely comprehensible. Its signal strengthens with depth — Corruption's "deep band" is literal depth.

Who lives there

The local (Terran) Undertow is mostly populated by thoughtforms: entities congealed from collective human feeling, or echoes of dead people. This is the common case — what most "spirits" and hauntings are. What witnesses call ghosts has a real taxonomy: echo (replaying imprint), thoughtform (feeling-fed, possibly hostile), or — vanishingly rare — a visitor; ANSCOM can misfile all three as "liminic events."

Alien intelligences sometimes visit. They range from minor and non-sapient to vastly intelligent and wholly alien. Such contacts are exceedingly rare: JDIT holds no first-hand account — only unconfirmed stories collected across decades of research and fieldwork. Visitor stories give the GM a sanctioned "wholly alien one-off" slot without adding a second living force to the cosmology.

The Gardener is very present in the Undertow — as a looming, diffuse presence. It coalesces into something with agency and consciousness only for brief moments.

Mass emotion is generative: disasters, cults, and panics can breed thoughtforms. The fascinated-dread exposure scenario (Psi in the World) would seed the Undertow with new entities — a hidden cost of any public revelation.

Seepage and the Victorian layer

Residue is astral seepage: haunted places and relic objects are feeling-soaked astral structures bleeding through (Residue). The Victorian layer: the séance craze was mass amateur dabbling at the Undertow's edge (Séance Craze). Mostly fraud — but some circles genuinely touched it, and a few things looked back. Surviving residue: haunted séance sites, and one or two things that never left. Victorian séance sites are ready-made residue adventure locations, and the things that never left are two unkeyed campaign assets.

The astral's true nature, seepage, and the deeper structure are field discoveries: residue cases that answer back; a séance-site case with period documents; the field-lore rumor mill; eventually, meeting something that was never human.

Discovery of the Psi Neuron

c. 1970, the NII-19 labs: Boris Tarusov and Klavdiya Zemtsova establish that some human neurons are different — the anomalous neuron type behind psionic aptitude, named the bioplasmic neuron — and trace the anomaly to the cells' mitochondria, named the bioplasmic mitochondrion. What happened

In the NII-19 labs, c. 1970, Boris Tarusov — head of biological research — and Klavdiya Zemtsova established that some human neurons are different: a distinct population that glows wrong — anomalous ultraweak photon emission, exactly the signal Tarusov's own public-career instruments had been built to catch — and runs an anomalous metabolism. A high concentration of these neurons is what makes a person psionic. The programme, taking the cells for the seat of a biological energy field, named the type the bioplasmic neuron; formal credit ran under the eponym Tarusov–Zemtsova neuron (Bioplasmic neuron). The same work traced the anomaly into the cells' mitochondria — Tarusov's pre-programme career had run beside mitochondria, and he looked where he knew — and the programme named the organelle the bioplasmic mitochondrion, eponym Tarusov–Zemtsova mitochondrion (Bioplasmic mitochondrion).

What they never found was the mechanism. The anomaly — the wrong glow, the anomalous metabolism — tracked to the mitochondria, but every instrument the programme had showed ordinary-looking mitochondria doing anomalous chemistry: there was nothing to see, which is precisely what made the energy-field theory reasonable — the field had to be generated somewhere, and the mitochondria obliged. What actually made them different — the plasmid and its complex — was invisible to the era's tools and stayed unfound until JDIT's molecular work of the early 1990s (The LAM, Discovery of the Liminon).

The discovery gave the Soviets the neuron count, from it the assay, and through it the ceiling half of the Berger scale.

Founding of the JDIT

1987 — the negotiated aftermath of the Baltic Incident: a covert UN agency, US-tilted, built out of two Cold War programmes that each kept back what they could. ANSCOM born with it.

Org detail lives in the faction notes — this note records the founding as an occurrence and carries the detail of what the new body was made of.

What happened

At the Cold War's end, the US pushed to terminate the USSR's psionics research and operations. The negotiated compromise after the Baltic Incident: a covert agency under the UN umbrella — where the US holds much greater control and say — which a weakened USSR judged better than an all-out shadow war. That agency is JDIT; ANSCOM was born with it. The US (and later China) refused the clause forbidding independent national initiatives (JDIT) — the power that caused the Incident kept its own options open while closing everyone else's.

Both Cold War programmes became the JDIT. PALE LANTERN and NII-19 did not merely hand over their files: JDIT is the body they were folded into, and it is their successor. The merged lexicon and the Berger scale's adoption as standard date to this consolidation (GLOSSARY; The Berger Scale) — the Soviet ceiling assay welded to the American manifest band into B/C notation.

What each side kept

Neither bloc transferred what it could keep. Both treated the remainder as leverage against a future nobody could see the shape of yet.

The Americans had the leverage and spent the least. They handed over operational know-how, doctrine, and basic training technique — the tradecraft that still runs ANSCOM's field practice — and they handed over people, but the wrong ones on purpose: rookies and mid-ranking officers, almost nobody above them. The advanced techniques their elite operatives had developed did not cross. Neither did the operatives. The most senior, most knowledgeable and most powerful were decommissioned on paper; those judged useless were killed, and those judged trustworthy and useful were retired and watched.

The Soviets had less and kept less. Their science had largely stalled by 1987, and what crossed was the basic corpus and some technical specifications — enough to found a discipline on, not enough to advance one. One piece crossed immediately and in full: the needle. The decommissioning drug is NII-19's own work, first tested 1977, and JDIT's exit protocol has run on Soviet technology from the founding (The Needle). "Decommissioned" meant something materially different in Moscow than in Washington. A minority of their scientists came, none of them the best or the most experienced. Their operatives were fewer than the Americans' and, by the end, less well trained; the younger ones were integrated. The advanced prototypes stayed. The old guard was decommissioned in earnest — needled — though a reserve was held back un-needled, in case it was ever wanted.

The second stage

What the Soviets kept back in 1987 came to JDIT anyway — four years later, unnegotiated, and incomplete. The state that was holding it stopped existing, and ANSCOM spent the following decade sweeping the successor states for it. Recorded in full at Dissolution of the USSR: the lion's share recovered, the remainder privatized, some of the reserve found and killed and the rest never found at all.

Founded decapitated

Both blocs sent their B team, and neither said so. The 1987 intake was a cohort of juniors who had never been taught by the people who knew, inheriting doctrine they could apply and could not extend. Thirty-nine years on, that cohort is JDIT's senior leadership — the old guard of the present agency are the recruits and near-recruits of the founding, and there has never been a generation above them.

The science since 1987

JDIT kept a real science department and did real work with it. It was founded in 1987 as the Applied Biophysics Division — Soviet transferees on NII-19's corpus, named while the bioplasmic energy-field theory was still the reigning frame — and renamed the Liminic Sciences Division c. 1994–95, after its own discovery (Discovery of the Liminon; department detail in JDIT). The mostly-American early leadership held suppression of Soviet-inherited terms and institutionality as a minor standing goal; it won on labels like the division's name and lost in the deep lexicon, which stayed Soviet because the theory was Soviet (GLOSSARY). The curve has a shape:

  • 1987 – c. 2007. Recovery-fed and genuinely productive. The merger dowry — the material both programmes did hand over at founding — fed the first years; from 1991 the successor-state sweeps (Dissolution of the USSR) kept delivering. The department spent two decades catching up to and then past what NII-19 had known. Past it, decisively, in c. 1993–94: the division detected the liminon (Discovery of the Liminon) — its first monument, its own work, needing none of the recovered hardware — and the discovery unified the theory, cracked the mechanism inside the organelle the Soviets had localized (The LAM, the LAM), and set off the renaming cascade the division itself rode.
  • c. 2007 onward. The caches ran dry. The last several recoveries held either technology JDIT already had or results it had reached independently. The department did not collapse; it simply stopped being handed anything.

The second monument, and the purely in-house one, is the cyberpsi implant — the neural digital-I/O translator that gates every useful digital application of Ergokinesis (The Closed List). It is purely JDIT-developed and post-founding. Neither parent could have built it: Soviet neural integration was too crude and Soviet computing too poor, and the Americans never worked the neural side at all. It needs the computer era, which places it in the 2000s at the earliest — so implanted operatives are a young cohort, and the first generation of them got the first version.

The timing is the part worth remembering: JDIT's research arm ran out of road roughly a decade before the phenomenon it studies began accelerating. The caseload climb of the last ten years (ANSCOM) arrives at a department with nothing left in the pipe. The institution is not complacent. It is out of ideas and covering.

The operational gap

Most of what the Americans withheld has been quietly re-derived. Thirty-nine years of continuous casework will find the useful, non-arcane technique on its own, and ANSCOM did. What remains missing is the rest: the very arcane, the very niche, and the very power-demanding — including some more lethal telepathic and telekinetic techniques that PALE LANTERN kept and JDIT never learned existed.

The Americans were also less squeamish than the Soviets about psi-boosting drugs, and pushed their best operatives with chemistry the Soviets would not have used (54-psionic-campaigns §Psi drugs). Those drugs were mostly stimulants, and they degrade an operative's capabilities in the long run — a fact the programme established within a few years of adopting them. It kept using them, and began keeping tabs on agent drug usage instead, so it could stop its good agents burning themselves out prematurely. PALE LANTERN never knew why: the mechanism runs through the LAM's feeding pathway (The LAM), which is the same door NII-19 walked through to build the needle (The Needle). Both programmes met the same fact; only one of them could read it.

Those drug-usage tabs are the ancestor of ANSCOM's monitoring regime. Operational doctrine is exactly what crossed whole in 1987, and the practice crossed with it — then hardened into the standing psychological evaluation, physical examination and liminon-signature baselining that follows every psionic operative today (Corruption). What began as asset preservation, keeping a good agent from burning himself out, became surveillance for thresholds that end careers quietly.

So JDIT invented neither of the instruments it turns on its own psis. It inherited the watching from Washington and the needle from Moscow, and put them at the two ends of the same career.

What survives of all this inside JDIT is rumor, and thin rumor: stories the 1987 intake overheard from seniors who never taught them, about Lantern agents who did the impossible — crossed the world in a step, lifted a building. The stories are secondhand, unattributable, and dying with the cohort that carries them.

They are also, marginally, true. Nothing a human reaches unaided goes that high (The Closed List), and the drugs were never the mechanism. What those agents were doing was opening the door (Corruption) — borrowed power ignoring the Berger ceiling — in a programme that tolerated it and may have encouraged it, without ever having a concept for what it was or what it cost. It was never systematic and it was never common. It happened.

Jena Inheritance

What became of Hans Berger's Jena programme after he died: the 1944 wind-down, the sorting of the subjects into killed-on-site and shipped-to-Kaltenroda, the handful who lived, and the 1945 split of the programme's remains between the Americans and the Soviets.

What happened

Hans Berger hanged himself on 1 June 1941 and The Berger Programme outlived him by three years, then was closed, then was carried off in pieces by two governments that each thought they had got the whole of it. Two things came out of that building: paper and people. Both are still in play.

The wind-down (1941–1944). A few understudies carried the work on after Berger's death. As the war turned, the programme was closed and its funding redirected to more pressing projects; the scientists were dismissed or reassigned. The subjects were disposed of on a two-way sort: those judged too dangerous to move were killed on site, and the rest were sent to a section of Kaltenroda kept separate from the rest of the camp. Several died there. At least a couple came out alive.

The survivors. The people the programme could take without anyone asking questions were the people nobody was permitted to ask about: communists, Jews, Roma, and others the regime had already marked. The handful who survived both the lab and the camp went back into a Europe that had no framework for what had been done to them. Not all of them went unnoticed: the recovered paperwork carried subject records as well as staff records, and PALE LANTERN worked those lists too. Some of the survivors it found were neutralized, and some were recruited — the same sort Berger had run on them, applied a second time by a different flag. The ones nobody found had children. Those descended through an unbroken female line are alive now carrying lineages that were selected twice over: once by the Gardener's standing pressure, once by Berger on purpose. Descent through a male survivor carries no enhanced endowment at all (The LAM).

1945, and the split inheritance. US forces took Jena in April 1945 and stripped the former lab of everything they could carry. Going in, they knew only that Berger had run experiments — not their extent, and not whether anything had come of them. They came out knowing the work had succeeded. From the recovered paperwork they also got names, and recruited several of the surviving scientists through Operation Paperclip. In July the city passed to the Soviet zone by prior agreement, and the Soviets got the site itself — by then stripped to the walls. What they recovered was not material. It was the bare fact that the Germans had believed they had proof of psychic powers and had been experimenting on that belief: no results, no method, no theory. An existence proof, second-hand.

It bought a small programme and no more — the proposition was speculative and Soviet leadership was pragmatic. The break, years later, was intelligence rather than science: having found out about PALE LANTERN, NII-19 infiltrated it at modest clearance, the same trade they had worked against Manhattan. It was access enough to settle the only question that mattered — the Americans had succeeded — and from then on Moscow treated it as an arms race and funded it accordingly. Everything the money produced was still Soviet work, built from nothing, because there was nothing to build on (The Berger Scale).

What the Americans carried west included the thing that now bears Berger's name. He had built a proto-scale in the lab — bands of demonstrated capability, graded by what a subject could be made to do, because in 1939 capability was the only thing he could measure. That banding logic, and the name attached to it, became the manifest half of the modern Berger scale. The ceiling half is Soviet and biological, and arrived by a completely separate road.

The Berger Programme

The Reich's psionic breeding programme at Jena, 1938–44 — run by Hans Berger under cover of his emeritus status, aimed at psi super-soldiers, and the ancestor of both Cold War programmes.

Also called the Nazi Programme — the vault's English handle, and JDIT's. It carried two German designations at once:

  • Forschungsstelle Jena-Ost ("Research Post Jena-East") — the ledger name, and the only one that reached paper an accountant could see. It describes nothing, which was the point; internally it was just die Forschungsstelle.
  • Sonderkommando F (in files also the F-Sonderauftrag) — the Deckname for the work itself, 1938–1942: crushed to a letter on paper, F für Fernsinn among the people doing it and the few above them who knew what it was. In 1942, under the understudies, the work was renamed Unternehmen Fernsinn ("Operation Far-Sense") and carried that name to the end.

Ran 1938–1941 under Hans Berger and 1941–1944 under his understudies; wound down for want of funding. Site: The Jena Lab. Its afterlife is the Jena Inheritance.

Agenda

  • Public face: none. The programme had no public existence; Berger's 1938 emeritus appointment was the cover, and the public record shows a distinguished neurologist shelved and ridiculed.
  • True goal: psionic übermenschen — psi super-soldiers, and past them the next step in human evolution as Berger understood it: the biologically superior first class of the new Reich. The mandate rested on his own lifelong conviction that if the faculty is heritable, it can be bred.
  • Current move: n/a — dead since 1944.

Members

  • Hans Berger — architect and director, 1938 until his death on 1 June 1941
  • The understudies who carried the work to ~1944 (unnamed; some of them are the Paperclip recruits — Jena Inheritance)
  • The captured subjects (unnamed; some receivers, most not)

Assets & territory

  • The Jena Lab — a compartmented laboratory inside Jena, Thuringia. Stripped by US forces in April 1945; the ground has been hot ever since.
  • Two working tracks: selective breeding and forced induction, run on captured subjects. It was cruel on a scale the surviving paperwork only gestures at.
  • The doctrine of the Durchbruch. The programme believed the first act of manifestation — the Durchbruch, the breakthrough — is forced out of a person by extreme physical or psychological suffering, and cannot be reached any other way. The evidence was Hans Berger's own: he had transmitted at the instant he expected to die, and nothing less than that had ever done it. So the induction track's method was to reproduce the moment deliberately and repeatedly — subject a captive to terror and agony, let them recover enough to be broken again, and wait for the Durchbruch. Most subjects never had one. The programme read those as Träger who had been insufficiently pressed, and pressed harder. This is the mechanism behind both hot sites: deliberate, concentrated, repeated suffering at The Jena Lab, and mass suffering at Kaltenroda.
  • A subject pool drawn from the already-marked — communists, Jews, Roma, and others the regime had condemned: the people the programme could take without anyone asking questions.
  • The two kinds. The programme's foundational taxonomy: psis divide into Sender and Empfänger — emitters and receivers. It comes straight out of Hans Berger's 1892 diary account of his sister, where he transmitted and she received, generalised from that single event into a doctrine and never seriously re-examined. The programme sorted its subjects by it and designed both tracks around it. It is wrong in a way nobody could see until much later: everyone emits (Psi in the World).
  • The lexicon. The programme did the theorising the American programme lived off — the Soviets inherited none of it and built their own (The Berger Scale) — so it owns the vocabulary for the basic concepts of psi and for the heredity and grading its breeding track ran on — the GLOSSARY's Berger-German stratum. It travelled west with the Paperclip recruits and entered the modern lexicon through PALE LANTERN, never directly. Almost all of it is dead now, retired or superseded by Soviet theory in '87 and by JDIT's science after. The conspicuous survivor is receiver — Empfänger, translated straight, kept because the field says it fifty times a day. The quieter one is lineage — Sippe, translated to something less awkward to say, and never replaced because the word is so rarely needed.

The concepts. Sender and Empfänger, the two kinds, both taken from Berger's diary. Gedankenübertragung — thought-transmission — is his word for the act itself, and stayed the programme's. Fernsinn, far-sense, is the programme's name for the whole phenomenon — the faculty in both its modes; on paper it hid behind the F of the Deckname until the 1942 rename put it on the folder. Its two modes are Empfang, the receiving, and Fernwirkung, far-effect, the projecting mode that a Sender does; the programme took the word from the physics of action at a distance because it wanted to be read as physics. Durchbruch is the first manifestation, forced. Fernsinn died at the '87 standardization, superseded by the Soviet-frame bioplasmic function — renamed the liminic function at the c. 1994–95 cascade (Discovery of the Liminon).

The heredity and grading words. Erbbegabung — hereditary giftedness — is aptitude understood as an inherited endowment. A Träger is a carrier: someone whose blood holds the endowment with nothing yet showing. The Sippe is the unit of heredity — the kin-group a power runs in; a Sippschaftstafel charts a Sippschaft (all descendants of the parents and grandparents, a frame wide enough to show a trait surfacing across siblings and cousins) together with the manifestations observed in it. The Sippenbuch, the kin-book, is the breeding registry — bound runs of Sippschaftstafeln, a stud book kept on human families and the direct ancestor of a lineage file. Subjects were graded on descent by the regime's fractional model: Vollempfänger and Halbempfänger, Vollsender and Halbsender — full and half. Nobody could say those four words out loud after 1945, which is most of why they died.

  • The maternal rule. The breeding track noticed the pattern empirically and early: the gift follows the mother — read, in the programme's own race-science frame, as one more law of blood. It is correct as far as it goes: transmission does ride the maternal line (The LAM), which makes this the oldest in-world record of the fact, predating both Cold War programmes. What the rule cannot see is the nuclear gate, so it cannot account for the carrier mothers whose children never manifested. Whether the observation travelled west with the proto-scale or was re-derived later is deliberately undefined (Open Threads).
  • The Reizschwelle test. The programme's one real measurement: a subject's stimulus threshold, meaning how loud a thought had to be shouted before they caught it. A Sender the programme already trusted was set to broadcast at a fixed distance and told to push harder by graded steps, over and over, until the Empfänger reported the thought; the step at which they caught it was their Reizschwelle. A low threshold meant a sensitive receiver. The test measured only what a subject could be made to demonstrate on the day — it could not see potential at all, which is the whole reason the proto-scale that went west had a manifest half and nothing else (The Berger Scale). The Soviets reached the other half by a road the Germans never found (Assay).
  • The proto-scale — bands of demonstrated capability, graded by what a subject could be made to do, because capability was the only thing Berger's instruments could reach. It went west in 1945 and became the manifest half and the name of the modern Berger scale.
  • Kaltenroda — not an asset but a disposal route: the movable subjects were sent there in 1944.

  • NII-19 — inheritor of the site only, from July 1945, by then stripped to the walls; likewise JDIT from 1987. What the Soviets got was one second-hand fact, not a method (The Berger Scale).
  • The Gardener — no relation the programme knew of. Berger was duplicating by force, in three years, what the Gardener's standing pressure had been doing to human lineages across deep time (The Gardener).

The cultivation

Human psionics is not native: psi is the Gardener's cultivation, and receivers are the crop coming in. The cultivation is artificial selection on human Lineages: aptitude is strongly inborn, the engineered element rides the maternal line (The LAM), and receivers have been getting stronger over generations as the Gardener selects for potency. It engineered the plasmid into the hominid line — the breeding program predates Homo sapiens (Fall).

The cultivation is crude. The Gardener senses psis far more clearly than regular people — receivers are distinctly present in the Undertow, while mundane minds are dim (faintly less so in slow-wave sleep — Psi in the World). Its selection mechanism is simply fitness amplification of what it can see: it whispers to sensitive psis' subconscious — the whispers land in sleep, where reception sharpens — and seeps into their physiology, making them more alert to danger, more alluring to others — and so, over generations, more reproductively successful. No matchmaking, no design: the potent get blessed, and the blessing compounds. The Seeds are its breeding instruments — within their reach they nudge pairings, dreams, and migrations of carrier lineages, and the "nudging" is this whispering-and-seeping, not deliberate pairing management. Selection is emergent, which is why it took megayears and why the pattern is hard to prove: there is no plan to uncover, only a gradient.

  • The blessing is real, which is the horror: sharper instincts, magnetism, luck-adjacent survival — PCs benefit from the thing that is colonizing them. The blessing's trap is a loop: the whispering is a source of Corruption, and Corruption is what makes a mind exploitable.
  • Psi itself is not location-bound: an awakened receiver works at full strength anywhere. Incidents therefore follow lineages, not places — any geographic clustering is demographic, potent lineages still living near their ancestral seed.
  • Its clear sensing of receivers vs. dim sensing of mundanes has tactical weight: mundane operatives are near-invisible to it; strong psis are beacons. ANSCOM would invert its own doctrine if it knew.
  • Rogue receivers are victims of cultivation, not freaks of nature — a tragic undertone under full-lethality containment work. PC psis are themselves part of the crop: what the Gardener wants with strong receivers is a personal threat to the party, not just a global one.
  • The campaign's long question is seeded from day one: the colonization is ongoing, not awaited — can it be seen in time, and can it be stopped?

Cognition and mood

The more the Gardener corrupts, the more "brain power" it gains — and the more aware and sophisticated its actions become. Its primal state is a stage, not a nature: given sufficient psis to corrupt, it might become a cunning alien intelligence shaping the world from the shadows — or openly. Corrupted psis are the active substrate — the stakes clock has a mechanism: receiver count × potency ≈ how much mind the Gardener can run. ANSCOM's lethal containment of violent rogues is — unknowingly — enemy lobotomy, which partially explains why the Flowering has taken this long despite the bloom; containment literally keeps the enemy stupid, without anyone knowing it.

Its emotional register evolves with its cognition. Right now it is driven by anger and revenge; as it strengthens it may become colder and more calculating. The anger shows: corrupted individuals tend to become more violent and aggressive than they were before — not always, but normally (the rare non-Gardener wavelength is the exception, Corruption). The entity's current mood bleeds through its thralls. The violence signature gives overexposure-syndrome cases a diagnostic texture JDIT misreads: aggression looks like madness but is mood — the entity is angry, and its thralls inherit it. If the anger cools as it grows, late-campaign corrupted will turn eerily calm — a dreadful tell that things are getting worse, not better.

  • Erratic intent explains inconsistent opposition: some cases feel orchestrated, others random — both are true. The Gardener's attention flickers, and the corrupted's tuning waxes and wanes with its focus. Its interventions are episodic by nature — plans executed in brief coalescences, then left running on instinct and seed automation. This explains both deep patience and occasional blunders.
  • The Undertow is feeling-shaped: the Gardener's rage is weather there. Undertow travelers may read its emotional state directly — the campaign's strangest intelligence source.
  • Endgame geometry: force the dial down (cull/limit receivers — monstrous), break substrate linkage (how?), or treat with a mind that only ever grows more capable. All three are live.

History

Real-world biography (historical record)

Life. Born 21 May 1873 in Neuses, now part of Coburg (Saxe-Coburg and Gotha). Abitur at the Casimirianum, 1892. Enrolled at Jena to read mathematics, meaning to become an astronomer; quit after one semester and enlisted for a year of cavalry service. Married Baroness Ursula von Bülow in 1911; she worked as his technical assistant. Served as an army psychiatrist in the First World War.

The accident (1892). On a cavalry exercise his horse reared and threw him into the path of a horse-drawn artillery battery; the driver halted the team just short of him. That evening a telegram arrived from his father: his sister, kilometres away, had been seized by a certainty that he was in mortal danger and had insisted the family wire him. Berger recorded it in his diary as "a case of spontaneous telepathy in which… as I contemplated certain death, I transmitted my thoughts, while my sister who was particularly close to me, acted as the receiver." He treated it as the organising event of his working life.

Career. Returned to Jena for medicine explicitly to find "the physiological basis of psychic energy." M.D. 1897; joined Otto Binswanger's psychiatric clinic at Jena. Habilitation 1901; senior lecturer 1906; physician-in-chief 1912; succeeded Binswanger as director in 1919; Rector of the university in 1927. Collaborated with Oskar Vogt and Korbinian Brodmann on brain lateralisation.

The long approach. For roughly two decades before the EEG he chased the same quarry by other instruments — cerebral blood flow measured through skull defects (plethysmography) and brain temperature — hunting the conversion of brain metabolism into heat, into electricity, and into Lehmann's third term, "P-energy" (psychische Energie). His Jena lecture course was published as Psychophysiologie in 12 Vorlesungen (1921).

The EEG. First human recording: 6 July 1924, on a 17-year-old boy during neurosurgery by Nikolai Guleke. Berger coined Elektrenkephalogramm. He then sat on the result for five years, accumulating traces: 73 sessions on his son Klaus (aged 15, head shaved close), 56 on himself with needle electrodes under the skin, plus hospital staff and patients. Technique evolved from silver wires pushed under the scalp to silver-foil electrodes held by rubber bandage; recording from a Lippmann capillary electrometer (disappointing) to a string galvanometer to a double-coil Siemens galvanometer reading down to ~1/10,000 volt. Output was three-second traces photographed by an assistant. He published "Über das Elektrenkephalogramm des Menschen" in 1929 and continued the series under that identical title through report 14 (1938). He described the ~8–13 Hz alpha rhythm (still called Berger waves), alpha blockade on eye-opening with faster beta replacing it, and the first EEG signatures of brain disease, epilepsy among them.

Reception. Ridicule first. Grey Walter noted in 1935 that Berger's associates thought him a crank and "completely ignorant of the technical and physical basis of his method." Vindication came from outside Germany: Adrian and Matthews confirmed the alpha rhythm in Britain in 1934, international recognition followed in 1937, and by 1938 the EEG was in diagnostic use in the US, Britain and France. He was nominated for the Nobel Prize in 1940 — a year in which no prizes were awarded; further nominations in 1942 and 1947 went unevaluated because he was dead.

The regime, and the end. Made professor emeritus in 1938 at 65. The traditional account has the Nazis forcing him out and banning his EEG work; archival research by Susanne Zimmermann at Jena (2005) complicates it — he sat on the committee choosing his own successor, served on the Erbgesundheitsgericht ordering compulsory sterilisations, left antisemitic remarks in his diaries, and accepted an appointment to the higher genetic-health court (Erbgesundheitsobergericht) in 1941. He never joined the NSDAP, SA or SS, but was a "supporting member" of the SS, possibly protectively. After prolonged depression and a severe skin infection he hanged himself on 1 June 1941, in the south wing of the Jena clinic, aged 68. In 2021 the Hans-Berger-Preis was renamed to distance the award from him.

Setting layer

The programme (1938–1941). The Berger Programme. The emeritus appointment was the cover, not the end of his career. Publicly shelved and publicly ridiculed, he was quietly given a compartmented laboratory at Jena and a mandate that happened to match his own lifelong conviction: if the faculty is heritable, it can be bred. His aim was psionic übermenschen — psi super-soldiers, and past them the next step in human evolution as he understood it: the biologically superior first class of the new Reich. The work ran on two tracks, selective breeding and forced induction, and on captured subjects — some receivers, most not. It was cruel on a scale the surviving paperwork only gestures at.

He did not know what he was duplicating. The thing Berger tried to force in three years, with restraints and a budget, had been happening to humanity anyway across deep time — not as a design but as a pressure. The Gardener does not plan and does not choose; it leans. Its presence in the Undertow is a standing gradient that has bent human lineages toward psi since long before there was anyone to notice (The Gardener, Psi in the World, The Gardener).

Why he died. It worked well enough to be heard. Ground where enough people suffer badly enough for long enough can go hot, and the odds of it scale with three things: how intense the suffering is, how long it runs, and how psionically potent the sufferers are. Jena had all three, concentrated into a few rooms and sustained for years, and Berger worked inside them daily. The despair the record calls his clinical depression was exposure (Corruption) — hot ground works on whoever stands in it, and being a receiver is not a prerequisite. He never diagnosed it. He experienced it as his own despair, and on 1 June 1941 he hanged himself in the south wing of the clinic.

What became of the programme after him — the 1944 wind-down, the disposal of the subjects, the survivors who walked out, and the 1945 split of the inheritance between the Americans and the Soviets — is the Jena Inheritance.
