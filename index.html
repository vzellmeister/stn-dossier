Overview

Primer

Start here: the whole setting in one read — what is actually true, how it got this way, and who the player characters work for. Everything after this page is detail.

Signal to Noise is a modern-day cosmic-horror procedural: GURPS, TL8, fully lethal, set in present-day Europe. The player characters are a fresh flying squad of ANSCOM — a covert agency that handles psionic incidents — working a continental circuit from a hub office in Vienna. The tone is creeping cosmic dread first, procedural casework second. This page is the whole picture, including the things nobody in the setting knows.

What is actually true

Reality is dualistic: the material world, and a second plane called the Undertow — shaped by human feeling, occasionally seeping through into the world. One particle exists in both planes at once: the liminon, the interface between them. The Undertow is inhabited — by thoughtforms congealed from collective feeling and the echoes of the dead, and, exceedingly rarely, by alien Visitors passing through. Its inner layer, the Depths, is non-linear and barely comprehensible; time dilates there.

In deep time, an entity was cast to Earth. The notes call it The Gardener — a GM-doc name; nothing in-world has a word for it, because nothing in-world knows it exists. It engineered an alien plasmid into the hominid line and has been cultivating psionic bloodlines ever since, the way a breeder improves stock. Its purpose: to grow receivers potent enough to serve as the neurons of its fully instantiated mind — and as vessels for its kind. Its instruments are seeds, fragments of itself buried worldwide, which nudge the pairings, dreams, and migrations of the bloodlines within their reach, and through which it senses the corrupted at range.

The Gardener is primal — half-awake, its cultivation instinctual, its attention a flicker. But its mind grows with its thralls: nearly every corrupted psi adds cognition. Its current register is anger, so the corrupted trend violent; the long arc runs from instinctive flicker toward cunning, strategy, and — unchecked — open mastery, the rage cooling as it grows.

The biology: psionic aptitude rides a modified mitochondrion, the LAM, which carries a small alien DNA circle coupling the cell's energy machinery to liminons. Every human nervous system carries a feeble baseline form; the enhanced variant rides down maternal lineages, and whether it ever docks is gated by a rare nuclear allele needed from both parents — so carrier lines can run dark for generations. Roughly one person in a hundred thousand manifests: about 80,000 receivers alive today, and rising.

One more thing is true of everyone: every human broadcasts their thoughts psychically, louder with emotion — the hum of a crowd. Human chatter rides a short wavelength; corruption tunes the deep band. The waking crowd never reaches the Gardener — but sleep carries a person's emission deeper, and dreaming sharpens reception. The whispers land there.

How it got this way

The cultivation is visible across millennia: real oracles, witches, and saints; folk belief is garbled truth; haunted places and relic objects are residue of the old work. The séance craze of c. 1850–1910 was the first mass contact with the Undertow — some circles touched it, and things looked back.

In 1892, Hans Berger survived an accident and his sister, far away, knew — he transmitted, she received. His diary account of that event became the foundation of The Berger Programme, the German psi-research effort of the 1930s–40s: breeding registries, a forced-breakthrough doctrine, and the first taxonomy of psis. Its theory crossed to the Americans with the Paperclip paperwork.

The Cold War split the field in two. PALE LANTERN, the US programme, judged the German inheritance foundation enough and went straight to weaponizing — operations, tradecraft, elite operatives. NII-19, the Soviet programme, inherited nothing and had to build a science from scratch — the biology and clinical work, including the discovery of the anomalous neurons and their mitochondria. Both programmes were real and both were productive. Meanwhile, from about 1900, psionic incidents were quietly climbing all century — the GM notes call it the bloom, and nobody in-world has yet recognized it as a phenomenon.

In 1986, Chernobyl: the tumors of Subject 357, NII-19's incurable patient, secreted a psionically reactive substance (Subject 357 Case). In 1987, the Baltic Incident — publicly an oil spill, in truth a self-replicating psionically reactive bio-substance, its hotspot persisting to this day — forced the blocs to the table. JDIT was founded as a UN treaty body, and both Cold War programmes became it (Founding of the JDIT). But each side kept its best back as leverage: the US its elite operatives and advanced techniques, the USSR its prototypes and old-guard personnel. Both sent their juniors — JDIT was founded decapitated, and the 1987 intake, never having had a generation above it, is today's senior leadership.

In 1991 the USSR dissolved and the withheld Soviet material went loose (Dissolution of the USSR); JDIT swept the successor states and recovered most of it, the rest privatized. That recovered material fed JDIT's science until about 2007, then the well ran dry. The research arm's one earned triumph of its own is the liminon, discovered c. 1993–94 (Discovery of the Liminon), which unified the theory and renamed the entire technical vocabulary. And for the last ten years the caseload has climbed, year over year — the bloom's visible face. Offices are understaffed, veterans mutter that something changed, and nobody has connected the curve to a cause.

The official world

Publicly, psi does not exist, and JDIT is a boring international defense initiative that does nothing. The truth is compartmented as CANOPY: all JDIT employees know psi is real. Inside CANOPY, the working theory is that psis pick up an external signal — which is wrong (the powers are cultivated biology), but it named the word "receiver" and the agency itself: Anomalous Signals Command. The irony the org cannot see: the corruption channel genuinely is a transmission — the Gardener broadcasts and the corrupted receive — so ANSCOM named itself for a wrong theory that happens to describe exactly what is eating its agents.

JDIT keeps the field clear: any organization that gets big enough to notice is eliminated or driven back underground. It holds every known hot zone — guarded, not cleaned. ANSCOM, its operations arm, does not publicly exist; it detects, contains, and closes noises, and manages receivers before they go loud.

ANSCOM is genuinely grey, and never a bastion against evil. It collects its fallen operatives' bodies and feeds a brain-salvage amplifier that corrupts its operators. Its psis may only retire by decommissioning — a mandatory injection, Soviet-built and inherited whole in '87, that kills the docked LAMs and takes part of the mind with them; recruits know the policy, not the price. It runs PRUNE, a city-agent program that gets suspected latents addicted to methamphetamine so street chemistry degrades them quietly; it funds itself through TAPROOT, drug-trade logistics facilitation; it keeps DEADWOOD, a secret internal squad that brings down rogue agents; and it wipes the memories of people who brush against CANOPY. It invented neither instrument it turns on its own psis: the monitoring regime is American, the needle Soviet.

And it is caught in a double bind it cannot see. Its rogue-hunting doctrine is correct — every contained corrupted psi genuinely slows the Gardener — but every receiver it recruits and strengthens, and every psi-tech experiment it runs, feeds the enemy. The institution fighting the colonization is its supply line. Worse: corruption is already inside. Independent sleepers — uncoordinated, some of them receive-only and thus invisible to emission monitoring — converge on the entity's wants without any conspiracy existing. That is the first planned arc, The Blight.

Life as a receiver

JDIT rates receivers on the Berger scale: manifest power over ceiling, per power — "a three over five." Telepathy manifests first; that is universal biology. Many receivers lean on crutches — verbal or gestural routines that make manifestation reliable — and are sneered at for it. Psi runs hot: the active neurons are strongly dopaminergic, so using a power feels good and pulls mildly like an addiction, and carrying working LAMs roughly doubles a body's caloric baseline.

The cost is Corruption, and Corruption measures alienation from the human world — which is the same thing as openness to the Undertow. The Gardener is the loudest broadcaster there, so the corrupted usually tune to it. Loudness sets a floor: the stronger the receiver, the more Corruption they carry that can never be cleansed. The door opens from inside: any psi can voluntarily open to the Undertow for a boost, at a price, and every receiver instinctively knows how. The involuntary roads in are hot zones, the Undertow itself, vile encounters, and cursed artifacts. Alienation can recede — above the floor only, through months of deliberately mundane humanity, and any psi use resets the cycle: retire or rot. Past the point of no return lie catatonia, mutation — and, if the body keeps enough energy, a new seed. The network propagates itself.

Every agent is a tragic character by the nature of their powers alone. There is no good way out; the ones who take the needle come out broken. ANSCOM monitors its operatives constantly — psychological evaluation, liminon-signature baselining, LER drift — and careers end at thresholds, quietly.

Canon glossary

Signal to Noise — canon glossary

Modern-world secret-psionics campaign. The vault's vocabulary splits along two axes. The first is in-world versus GM-side: ANSCOM's clipped signals-bureaucracy jargon (what gets said aloud) versus the truth vocabulary (what the notes mean, words nothing in-world possesses) — keep them apart. The second axis runs through the in-world language itself, and it is setting lore in its own right.

How the program talks — the two registers

Two registers run through the program's language. The formal/technical register is what the scientists and the higher-ups write and say; the field register is what agents actually use. The field register is not a stratum of its own — it is a living second register eroding all the others. Field forms arise two ways: by corruption of a formal term (shortening, chewing down — "a B6" becomes "a six") or by native replacement (fresh imagery with no formal parent, the way "the Undertow" displaced "astral plane"). The older a term, the more worn it is; a field coinage dates from when agents started chewing on the term.

Every formal term carries two coordinates — tradition × minting era — from the canonical stratigraphy of the program's history: Occult inheritance (pre-program, up to the 1940s), Berger-German (1930s–40s loanwords), the US program (Cold War, mystical flavor), the Soviet program (Cold War, dry-descriptive), and JDIT bureaucratese (post-1987). Provenance is domain-keyed: the US program was results-focused — practical application in warfare and espionage — so it yielded the ops, tradecraft, and doctrine vocabulary; the Soviet program was scientific, after the whys and hows, so it yielded the physics, biology, and clinical vocabulary; compartments and administration are JDIT's own. Berger-German owns the two things Berger's programme actually theorised: the basic concepts of psi — what the faculty is, what it does, what kinds of people have it — and the race-science vocabulary of heredity and grading that a breeding programme could not do without. A term's subject matter therefore predicts its stratum. The split has a cause: the US inherited Berger's theory through Paperclip and judged it foundation enough to start weaponizing, while the Soviets inherited nothing and had to understand the phenomenon before they could use it (013-the-berger-scale).

German terms reach the lexicon through the Americans, never directly. PALE LANTERN carried the Jena science largely as it received it — that was the part of the corpus it expanded least, being busy weaponizing rather than theorizing — so the German layer is thickest exactly where American science is thinnest. Most of that layer is nonetheless dead: retired, replaced, or superseded, first by Soviet theory at the '87 merger and then by JDIT's own science. What survives, survives because the field kept saying it — or, in one case, because nobody ever needed to replace it (lineage). Discovery of the Psi Neuron

What happened

In the NII-19 labs, c. 1970, Boris Tarusov — head of biological research — and Klavdiya Zemtsova established that some human neurons are different: a distinct population that glows wrong — anomalous ultraweak photon emission, exactly the signal Tarusov's own public-career instruments had been built to catch — and runs an anomalous metabolism. A high concentration of these neurons is what makes a person psionic. The programme, taking the cells for the seat of a biological energy field, named the type the bioplasmic neuron; formal credit ran under the eponym Tarusov–Zemtsova neuron (Bioplasmic neuron). The same work traced the anomaly into the cells' mitochondria — Tarusov's pre-programme career had run beside mitochondria, and he looked where he knew — and the programme named the organelle the bioplasmic mitochondrion, eponym Tarusov–Zemtsova mitochondrion (Bioplasmic mitochondrion).

What they never found was the mechanism. The anomaly — the wrong glow, the anomalous metabolism — tracked to the mitochondria, but every instrument the programme had showed ordinary-looking mitochondria doing anomalous chemistry: there was nothing to see, which is precisely what made the energy-field theory reasonable — the field had to be generated somewhere, and the mitochondria obliged. What actually made them different — the plasmid and its complex — was invisible to the era's tools and stayed unfound until JDIT's molecular work of the early 1990s (033-psi-rides-a-modified-mitochondrion, Discovery of the Liminon).

The discovery gave the Soviets the neuron count, from it the assay, and through it the ceiling half of the Berger scale.

Founding of the JDIT

1987 — the negotiated aftermath of the Baltic Incident: a covert UN agency, US-tilted, built out of two Cold War programmes that each kept back what they could. ANSCOM born with it.

Canonical ruling: 008-the-founding-of-the-jdit. Org detail lives in the faction notes — this note records the founding as an occurrence and carries the detail of what the new body was made of.

What happened

At the Cold War's end, the US pushed to terminate the USSR's psionics research and operations. The negotiated compromise after the Baltic Incident: a covert agency under the UN umbrella — where the US holds much greater control and say — which a weakened USSR judged better than an all-out shadow war. That agency is JDIT; ANSCOM was born with it. The US (and later China) refused the clause forbidding independent national initiatives (003-jdit-keeps-the-field-clear) — the power that caused the Incident kept its own options open while closing everyone else's.

Both Cold War programmes became the JDIT. PALE LANTERN and NII-19 did not merely hand over their files: JDIT is the body they were folded into, and it is their successor. The merged lexicon and the Berger scale's adoption as standard date to this consolidation (GLOSSARY; 013-the-berger-scale) — the Soviet ceiling assay welded to the American manifest band into B/C notation.

What each side kept

Neither bloc transferred what it could keep. Both treated the remainder as leverage against a future nobody could see the shape of yet.

The Americans had the leverage and spent the least. They handed over operational know-how, doctrine, and basic training technique — the tradecraft that still runs ANSCOM's field practice — and they handed over people, but the wrong ones on purpose: rookies and mid-ranking officers, almost nobody above them. The advanced techniques their elite operatives had developed did not cross. Neither did the operatives. The most senior, most knowledgeable and most powerful were decommissioned on paper; those judged useless were killed, and those judged trustworthy and useful were retired and watched.

The Soviets had less and kept less. Their science had largely stalled by 1987, and what crossed was the basic corpus and some technical specifications — enough to found a discipline on, not enough to advance one. One piece crossed immediately and in full: the needle. The decommissioning drug is NII-19's own work, first tested 1977, and JDIT's exit protocol has run on Soviet technology from the founding (023-retirement-is-a-needle). "Decommissioned" meant something materially different in Moscow than in Washington. A minority of their scientists came, none of them the best or the most experienced. Their operatives were fewer than the Americans' and, by the end, less well trained; the younger ones were integrated. The advanced prototypes stayed. The old guard was decommissioned in earnest — needled — though a reserve was held back un-needled, in case it was ever wanted.

The second stage

What the Soviets kept back in 1987 came to JDIT anyway — four years later, unnegotiated, and incomplete. The state that was holding it stopped existing, and ANSCOM spent the following decade sweeping the successor states for it. Recorded in full at Dissolution of the USSR: the lion's share recovered, the remainder privatized, some of the reserve found and killed and the rest never found at all.

Founded decapitated

Both blocs sent their B team, and neither said so. The 1987 intake was a cohort of juniors who had never been taught by the people who knew, inheriting doctrine they could apply and could not extend. Thirty-nine years on, that cohort is JDIT's senior leadership — the old guard of the present agency are the recruits and near-recruits of the founding, and there has never been a generation above them.

The science since 1987

JDIT kept a real science department and did real work with it. It was founded in 1987 as the Applied Biophysics Division — Soviet transferees on NII-19's corpus, named while the bioplasmic energy-field theory was still the reigning frame — and renamed the Liminic Sciences Division c. 1994–95, after its own discovery (034-the-liminon-came-late; department detail in JDIT). The mostly-American early leadership held suppression of Soviet-inherited terms and institutionality as a minor standing goal; it won on labels like the division's name and lost in the deep lexicon, which stayed Soviet because the theory was Soviet (GLOSSARY). The curve has a shape:

  • 1987 – c. 2007. Recovery-fed and genuinely productive. The merger dowry — the material both programmes did hand over at founding — fed the first years; from 1991 the successor-state sweeps (Dissolution of the USSR) kept delivering. The department spent two decades catching up to and then past what NII-19 had known, working The Jena Lab and Kaltenroda in-house alongside the recovered material. Past it, decisively, in c. 1993–94: the division detected the liminon (Discovery of the Liminon) — its first monument, its own work, needing none of the recovered hardware — and the discovery unified the theory, cracked the mechanism inside the organelle the Soviets had localized (033-psi-rides-a-modified-mitochondrion, the LAM), and set off the renaming cascade the division itself rode.
  • c. 2007 onward. The caches ran dry. The last several recoveries held either technology JDIT already had or results it had reached independently. The department did not collapse; it simply stopped being handed anything.

The second monument, and the purely in-house one, is the cyberpsi implant — the neural digital-I/O translator that gates every useful digital application of Ergokinesis (028-the-closed-list). It is purely JDIT-developed and post-founding. Neither parent could have built it: Soviet neural integration was too crude and Soviet computing too poor, and the Americans never worked the neural side at all. It needs the computer era, which places it in the 2000s at the earliest — so implanted operatives are a young cohort, and the first generation of them got the first version.

The timing is the part worth remembering: JDIT's research arm ran out of road roughly a decade before the phenomenon it studies began accelerating. The caseload climb of the last ten years (ANSCOM; World Bible §Timeline) arrives at a department with nothing left in the pipe. The institution is not complacent. It is out of ideas and covering.

The operational gap

Most of what the Americans withheld has been quietly re-derived. Thirty-nine years of continuous casework will find the useful, non-arcane technique on its own, and ANSCOM did. What remains missing is the rest: the very arcane, the very niche, and the very power-demanding — including some more lethal telepathic and telekinetic techniques that PALE LANTERN kept and JDIT never learned existed.

The Americans were also less squeamish than the Soviets about psi-boosting drugs, and pushed their best operatives with chemistry the Soviets would not have used (54-psionic-campaigns §Psi drugs). Those drugs were mostly stimulants, and they degrade an operative's capabilities in the long run — a fact the programme established within a few years of adopting them. It kept using them, and began keeping tabs on agent drug usage instead, so it could stop its good agents burning themselves out prematurely. PALE LANTERN never knew why: the mechanism runs through the LAM's feeding pathway (031-psi-runs-hot), which is the same door NII-19 walked through to build the needle (023-retirement-is-a-needle). Both programmes met the same fact; only one of them could read it.

Those drug-usage tabs are the ancestor of ANSCOM's monitoring regime. Operational doctrine is exactly what crossed whole in 1987, and the practice crossed with it — then hardened into the standing psychological evaluation, physical examination and liminon-signature baselining that follows every psionic operative today (012-power-estranges). What began as asset preservation, keeping a good agent from burning himself out, became surveillance for thresholds that end careers quietly.

So JDIT invented neither of the instruments it turns on its own psis. It inherited the watching from Washington and the needle from Moscow, and put them at the two ends of the same career.

What survives of all this inside JDIT is rumor, and thin rumor: stories the 1987 intake overheard from seniors who never taught them, about Lantern agents who did the impossible — crossed the world in a step, lifted a building. The stories are secondhand, unattributable, and dying with the cohort that carries them.

They are also, marginally, true. Nothing a human reaches unaided goes that high (030-the-impossible-tier-is-real), and the drugs were never the mechanism. What those agents were doing was opening the door (020-the-door-opens-from-inside) — borrowed power ignoring the Berger ceiling — in a programme that tolerated it and may have encouraged it, without ever having a concept for what it was or what it cost. It was never systematic and it was never common. It happened.

Jena Inheritance

What became of Hans Berger's Jena programme after he died: the 1944 wind-down, the sorting of the subjects into killed-on-site and shipped-to-Kaltenroda, the handful who lived, and the 1945 split of the programme's remains between the Americans and the Soviets.

What happened

Hans Berger hanged himself on 1 June 1941 and The Berger Programme outlived him by three years, then was closed, then was carried off in pieces by two governments that each thought they had got the whole of it. Two things came out of that building: paper and people. Both are still in play.

The wind-down (1941–1944). A few understudies carried the work on after Berger's death. As the war turned, the programme was closed and its funding redirected to more pressing projects; the scientists were dismissed or reassigned. The subjects were disposed of on a two-way sort: those judged too dangerous to move were killed on site, and the rest were sent to a section of Kaltenroda kept separate from the rest of the camp. Several died there. At least a couple came out alive.

The survivors. The people the programme could take without anyone asking questions were the people nobody was permitted to ask about: communists, Jews, Roma, and others the regime had already marked. The handful who survived both the lab and the camp went back into a Europe that had no framework for what had been done to them. Not all of them went unnoticed: the recovered paperwork carried subject records as well as staff records, and PALE LANTERN worked those lists too. Some of the survivors it found were neutralized, and some were recruited — the same sort Berger had run on them, applied a second time by a different flag. The ones nobody found had children. Those descended through an unbroken female line are alive now carrying lineages that were selected twice over: once by the Gardener's standing pressure, once by Berger on purpose. Descent through a male survivor carries no enhanced endowment at all (033-psi-rides-a-modified-mitochondrion).

1945, and the split inheritance. US forces took Jena in April 1945 and stripped the former lab of everything they could carry. Going in, they knew only that Berger had run experiments — not their extent, and not whether anything had come of them. They came out knowing the work had succeeded. From the recovered paperwork they also got names, and recruited several of the surviving scientists through Operation Paperclip. In July the city passed to the Soviet zone by prior agreement, and the Soviets got the site itself — by then stripped to the walls. What they recovered was not material. It was the bare fact that the Germans had believed they had proof of psychic powers and had been experimenting on that belief: no results, no method, no theory. An existence proof, second-hand.

It bought a small programme and no more — the proposition was speculative and Soviet leadership was pragmatic. The break, years later, was intelligence rather than science: having found out about PALE LANTERN, NII-19 infiltrated it at modest clearance, the same trade they had worked against Manhattan. It was access enough to settle the only question that mattered — the Americans had succeeded — and from then on Moscow treated it as an arms race and funded it accordingly.   • Ursula von Bülow — wife (m. 1911) and technical assistant

History

Real-world biography (historical record)

Life. Born 21 May 1873 in Neuses, now part of Coburg (Saxe-Coburg and Gotha). Abitur at the Casimirianum, 1892. Enrolled at Jena to read mathematics, meaning to become an astronomer; quit after one semester and enlisted for a year of cavalry service. Married Baroness Ursula von Bülow in 1911; she worked as his technical assistant. Served as an army psychiatrist in the First World War.

The accident (1892). On a cavalry exercise his horse reared and threw him into the path of a horse-drawn artillery battery; the driver halted the team just short of him. That evening a telegram arrived from his father: his sister, kilometres away, had been seized by a certainty that he was in mortal danger and had insisted the family wire him. Berger recorded it in his diary as "a case of spontaneous telepathy in which… as I contemplated certain death, I transmitted my thoughts, while my sister who was particularly close to me, acted as the receiver." He treated it as the organising event of his working life.

Career. Returned to Jena for medicine explicitly to find "the physiological basis of psychic energy." M.D. 1897; joined Otto Binswanger's psychiatric clinic at Jena. Habilitation 1901; senior lecturer 1906; physician-in-chief 1912; succeeded Binswanger as director in 1919; Rector of the university in 1927. Collaborated with Oskar Vogt and Korbinian Brodmann on brain lateralisation.

The long approach. For roughly two decades before the EEG he chased the same quarry by other instruments — cerebral blood flow measured through skull defects (plethysmography) and brain temperature — hunting the conversion of brain metabolism into heat, into electricity, and into Lehmann's third term, "P-energy" (psychische Energie). His Jena lecture course was published as Psychophysiologie in 12 Vorlesungen (1921).

The EEG. First human recording: 6 July 1924, on a 17-year-old boy during neurosurgery by Nikolai Guleke. Berger coined Elektrenkephalogramm. He then sat on the result for five years, accumulating traces: 73 sessions on his son Klaus (aged 15, head shaved close), 56 on himself with needle electrodes under the skin, plus hospital staff and patients. Technique evolved from silver wires pushed under the scalp to silver-foil electrodes held by rubber bandage; recording from a Lippmann capillary electrometer (disappointing) to a string galvanometer to a double-coil Siemens galvanometer reading down to ~1/10,000 volt. Output was three-second traces photographed by an assistant. He published "Über das Elektrenkephalogramm des Menschen" in 1929 and continued the series under that identical title through report 14 (1938). He described the ~8–13 Hz alpha rhythm (still called Berger waves), alpha blockade on eye-opening with faster beta replacing it, and the first EEG signatures of brain disease, epilepsy among them.

Reception. Ridicule first. Grey Walter noted in 1935 that Berger's associates thought him a crank and "completely ignorant of the technical and physical basis of his method." Vindication came from outside Germany: Adrian and Matthews confirmed the alpha rhythm in Britain in 1934, international recognition followed in 1937, and by 1938 the EEG was in diagnostic use in the US, Britain and France. He was nominated for the Nobel Prize in 1940 — a year in which no prizes were awarded; further nominations in 1942 and 1947 went unevaluated because he was dead.

The regime, and the end. Made professor emeritus in 1938 at 65. The traditional account has the Nazis forcing him out and banning his EEG work; archival research by Susanne Zimmermann at Jena (2005) complicates it — he sat on the committee choosing his own successor, served on the Erbgesundheitsgericht ordering compulsory sterilisations, left antisemitic remarks in his diaries, and accepted an appointment to the higher genetic-health court (Erbgesundheitsobergericht) in 1941. He never joined the NSDAP, SA or SS, but was a "supporting member" of the SS, possibly protectively. After prolonged depression and a severe skin infection he hanged himself on 1 June 1941, in the south wing of the Jena clinic, aged 68. In 2021 the Hans-Berger-Preis was renamed to distance the award from him.

Setting layer

The programme (1938–1941). The Berger Programme. The emeritus appointment was the cover, not the end of his career. Publicly shelved and publicly ridiculed, he was quietly given a compartmented laboratory at Jena and a mandate that happened to match his own lifelong conviction: if the faculty is heritable, it can be bred. His aim was psionic übermenschen — psi super-soldiers, and past them the next step in human evolution as he understood it: the biologically superior first class of the new Reich. The work ran on two tracks, selective breeding and forced induction, and on captured subjects — some receivers, most not. It was cruel on a scale the surviving paperwork only gestures at.

He did not know what he was duplicating. The thing Berger tried to force in three years, with restraints and a budget, had been happening to humanity anyway across deep time — not as a design but as a pressure. The Gardener does not plan and does not choose; it leans. Its presence in the Undertow is a standing gradient that has bent human bloodlines toward psi since long before there was anyone to notice (002-psi-is-the-gardeners-cultivation, 004-ancient-cultivation-modern-bloom, 007-one-in-a-hundred-thousand-suspected-world, 015-the-gardener-is-primal).

Why he died. It worked well enough to be heard. Ground where enough people suffer badly enough for long enough can go hot, and the odds of it scale with three things: how intense the suffering is, how long it runs, and how psionically potent the sufferers are. Jena had all three, concentrated into a few rooms and sustained for years, and Berger worked inside them daily. The despair the record calls his clinical depression was exposure (019-loudness-sets-a-floor, 021-the-roads-in) — hot ground works on whoever stands in it, and being a receiver is not a prerequisite. He never diagnosed it. He experienced it as his own despair, and on 1 June 1941 he hanged himself in the south wing of the clinic.

What became of the programme after him — the 1944 wind-down, the disposal of the subjects, the survivors who walked out, and the 1945 split of the inheritance between the Americans and the Soviets — is the Jena Inheritance.

Canon decision 002

Canon 002: Psi is the Gardener's cultivation

Date: 2026-08-22 Status: Accepted

Context

Campaign genesis. The premise needs one load-bearing truth: where psi comes from and why incidents are escalating. Without it, monster-of-the-week cases would have no pattern to converge on, and the "source stirs" stakes would be improvised later.

Decision

Human psionics is not native. A vast alien intelligence — GM-side name the Gardener — is dormant but stirring, and psionic-enabled humanity is its goal: it is cultivating humans as vessels for itself and its kind (X-COM Ethereals crossed with a Lovecraftian semi-awakened sleeper — see 006-the-gardener-fell-in-deep-time). Its presence on Earth is multiple seeds — fragments buried or hidden worldwide; the whole is one mind, greater than its parts.

The cultivation is artificial selection on human lineages: psionic aptitude is strongly inborn, and the engineered element is transmitted matrilineally — it rides the maternal line, so a lineage is, biologically, a matriline, though whether any given carrier manifests is gated by a rare nuclear allele inherited from both parents (033-psi-rides-a-modified-mitochondrion) — and receivers have been getting stronger over generations as the Gardener selects for potency. Seeds are its breeding instruments — within their reach they nudge pairings, dreams, and migrations of carrier lineages. (Refined in 015-the-gardener-is-primal: the "nudging" is instinctual whisper-and-seep fitness amplification, not deliberate matchmaking — selection is emergent, and all the cruder for it.) Psi itself is not location-bound: an awakened receiver works at full strength anywhere. Incidents therefore follow lineages, not places — any geographic clustering is demographic, potent lineages still living near their ancestral seed. Receivers (psis) are the crop coming in.

The PCs' employer knows almost none of this: JDIT/ANSCOM knows psi is real and contains incidents under the CANOPY compartment, but has not discovered the seeds, the Gardener, or the vessel project. The PCs can be the first to see the pattern.

Consequences at the table

  • Every case is secretly a data point: incidents map to lineages, and lineages trace back to ancestral seed grounds. Long-term play rewards players who keep their own files — genealogy is the real map.
  • Rogue receivers are victims of cultivation, not freaks of nature — a tragic undertone under full-lethality containment work.
  • PC psis are themselves part of the crop. What the Gardener wants with strong receivers is a personal threat to the party, not just a global one.
  • The campaign's long question is seeded from day one: the colonization is ongoing, not awaited — can it be seen in time, and can it be stopped?
  • Depends on: 001-table-agreements (tone: creeping cosmic dread).

Alternatives rejected

  • A broadcast/transmission source — rejected as the literal truth of ability genesis, but survives as flavor: ANSCOM's "signals" framing is wrong in an interesting way. The corruption channel genuinely is a transmission (two-layer "Signal" ruling, GLOSSARY §Flagged ambiguities; 025-corruption-is-openness) — ability genesis stays cultivation.
  • Psi use stirs the sleeper (use-coupled waking) — rejected; the Gardener's program advances by its own design, not by PC power use. Keeps players free to use psi without meta-guilt.
  • Program secretly knows / is compromised — rejected for campaign start; ANSCOM's ignorance is real. May emerge later in play, but is not canon now.
  • Geographic clustering (noises pool physically around seeds) — adopted at genesis, dropped same day during worldbuilding Stage 1 (2026-08-22-stage-1): psi is not location-bound; incidents follow lineages.

Rules basis

  • Psi as a power with source "psionic", per 10-powers — power modifiers, Antipsi countermeasures, and psi-tech (antipsi shielding/serum sidebars) all apply.
  • Powers notes that origin (alien cultivation) and source (psionic) are independent: the powers work the same regardless of how they came to exist (10-powers, "Source vs. origin"). This decision is therefore pure setting canon, no house rules needed.
  • Not covered by the KB: GURPS Psionic Powers and Psi-Tech are not extracted (listed but unextracted in 00-library); detailed psi ability catalogs will need extraction or table rulings.

How the players can learn it

Case files that keep featuring the same family names; genealogy work revealing selected lineages and their ancestral grounds; a seed physically uncovered during a containment; a strong receiver's dreams during a noise event; eventually, contact.

Canon decision 005

Canon 005: The world is dualistic

Date: 2026-08-22 Status: Accepted

Context

PSC's defining axes (54-psionic-campaigns §1.3) forced the question of what psi physically is. The answer turned out to be the setting's metaphysics, so it is recorded as one load-bearing decision.

Decision

Reality is dualistic: the material plane and the astral plane — called the Undertow in ANSCOM field slang, the setting's standard term (GLOSSARY) — coexist. The Undertow is shaped by people's feelings, and it occasionally affects — or seeps into — the material world.

The interface between the planes is the liminon: a subatomic particle existing simultaneously in both — the only thing that does. Normally it is neutrino-like, near-non-interactive; only when organized by psionic operation does it become interactive with matter. Because of this non-interactivity, human liminon research is biological and empirical, not theoretical physics.

Every human nervous system carries the liminically active mitochondrion — a modified mitochondrial subtype, outwardly an ordinary mitochondrion, that holds beside the standard mitochondrial genome a small separate alien DNA circle whose gene products couple the cell's energy machinery to liminons (033-psi-rides-a-modified-mitochondrion). In baseline humanity the complex is undocked and feeble — enough for the murmur every mind gives off and the feelings that shape the Undertow, nothing more. A high concentration of neurons running the enhanced, chain-docked variant is what makes a person psionic; because mitochondria are maternally inherited, the enhanced variant rides the maternal line, while whether its complex docks at all is gated by a rare nuclear allele from both parents. This is the mechanism behind inborn, lineage-bred aptitude (002-psi-is-the-gardeners-cultivation). Those neurons are strongly dopaminergic, and carrying working ones roughly doubles a person's caloric baseline — psi is pleasurable, mildly addictive, and expensive (031-psi-runs-hot).

On PSC's axes: psi is scientific (detectable, measurable, ultimately blockable) and not strictly mental — the body is fair game. Advanced receivers can manifest physical changes; body horror is a signature of late-stage cultivation.

JDIT's research effort discovered the liminon, c. 1993–94 (Discovery of the Liminon, 034-the-liminon-came-late); the anomalous neuron type had been identified decades earlier by the Soviet programme, which traced its source to the mitochondria and no further (Discovery of the Psi Neuron, 013-the-berger-scale) — the particle's discovery is what let JDIT crack the mechanism and mint the current names (Liminically active mitochondria, 034-the-liminon-came-late). Its biology is understood — the endocrine pathway that feeds it is textbook to the people cleared to read it (031-psi-runs-hot). What remains unsolved is the physics-to-biology join: how the LAM controls the liminon (033-psi-rides-a-modified-mitochondrion). Receiver screening is possible; detection and early psi-tech exist. This is where the setting's TL9 edge lives: JDIT's classified, CANOPY-compartmented instruments, not the public economy.

Residue (004-ancient-cultivation-modern-bloom) is astral seepage: haunted places and relic objects are feeling-soaked astral structures bleeding through.

Consequences at the table

  • ANSCOM casework has real forensics: liminon detectors, neural assays, screening programs. But the plasmid is confined to neural tissue (033-psi-rides-a-modified-mitochondrion), so "is this person a receiver" is emphatically not a blood test — it takes the right tissue and the right question.
  • The empirical-not-theoretical stance keeps mystery alive despite the scientific framing — JDIT can measure psi without understanding it.
  • Astral scenes are playable space (projection, seepage sites, echo dives), and the astral's feeling-shaped nature ties horror tone to mechanics.
  • Body-horror manifestations telegraph advanced cultivation — a visual escalation ladder for the metaplot.
  • Psychotronics (anti-psi tech) is physically possible; its availability is a separate dial (PSC questionnaire E28, still open).

Alternatives rejected

  • Strict mental line (levitation yes, wings no) — rejected; vessels are bodies, so cultivation must be able to reshape them.
  • Mystical/unmeasurable psi — rejected; a half-blind tech-heavy program is a good story, but the chosen empirical-but-shallow science is better: measurement without comprehension.
  • Single-plane world (no astral) — rejected with the "psi + residue" choice in 004-ancient-cultivation-modern-bloom; the astral gives residue and projection a home.

Rules basis

  • Axes: 54-psionic-campaigns §1.3; astral-plane canvas §2.5; countermeasures/psychotronics §3.3.
  • Astral projection and psi ability mechanics: pending the Psionic Powers extraction (53-psionic-powers, in progress).
  • House cosmology (liminon/LAM) is setting canon with no KB equivalent — flagged per vault convention.

Canon decision 006

Canon 006: The Gardener fell in deep time

Date: 2026-08-22 Status: Accepted

Context

Deciding the organelle's origin (005-the-world-is-dualistic) forced the question of what the Gardener actually is and when its program began.

Decision

The Gardener is a Lovecraftian entity — alien metaphorically and literally — cast to Earth in the prehistoric past, millions of years ago. Its mind is semi-awakened, Cthulhu-like: not scheming in real time but pressing on the world through deep, slow intent — intent that is real but erratic, unfocused, primal (015-the-gardener-is-primal).

Since arriving it has been subtly and quietly shaping the world and human history. It engineered the plasmid into the hominid line — an alien DNA circle grafted into the mitochondrion, fixed in the whole species — and cultivates enhanced variants through selected matrilines, riding the maternal line and switched on by a nuclear allele from both parents (033-psi-rides-a-modified-mitochondrion) — the breeding program predates Homo sapiens. The seeds are its instruments; the selected lineages its crop; vessel-capable receivers its goal (002-psi-is-the-gardeners-cultivation).

Consequences at the table

  • "Cast to Earth" implies an elsewhere it came from — and possibly others of its kind, wherever they remain. Left deliberately unresolved.
  • Human history is quietly authored: any historical turning point (Stage 3 work) may bear its fingerprints. Use sparingly — the horror is that you can never be sure which events were its.
  • A semi-awakened mind cannot be negotiated with or meaningfully fought at TL8/9 — campaigns against it are about its instruments, crop, and timetable, never the entity itself.
  • The 20th-century bloom (004-ancient-cultivation-modern-bloom) reads as the program compounding — or the sleeper's sleep growing shallow. Both may be true.

Alternatives rejected

  • Natural organelle, alien exploitation — rejected; psi is truly not native, preserving 002-psi-is-the-gardeners-cultivation as written. The mitochondrial recast does not revive this: the chassis is a native organelle, but the function is wholly the engineered plasmid's — a natural power plant, alien wiring (033-psi-rides-a-modified-mitochondrion).
  • Seed-stuff infection (lineages part-Gardener at cellular level) — rejected as the mechanism: the plasmid is an engineered artifact, not Gardener substance, so receivers carry alien design without being alien tissue.
  • Millennia-scale program — rejected: the Gardener's presence is deep time, millions of years (004-ancient-cultivation-modern-bloom). Human lineage cultivation is the millennia-scale, historically visible layer on top of it.

How the players can learn it

Geological context of an excavated seed (impossibly old strata); the Gardener's fingerprints converging across historical research; direct astral contact with the semi-awakened mind — a late-campaign event.

Canon decision 007

Canon 007: One in a hundred thousand, a suspecting world

Date: 2026-08-22 Status: Accepted

Context

PSC questionnaire C15/C17 (54-psionic-campaigns §2.2–2.3): how many psis, and what the public knows. The two must be set together — frequency drives how much secrecy is even possible.

Decision

Receiver frequency is Uncommon: about 1 per 100,000. The frequency is the anchor and the counts flow from it — at the 2026 population, roughly 80,000 worldwide, a few hundred in the largest metros, at least one in every significant nation. The 20th-century bloom (004-ancient-cultivation-modern-bloom) means the counts are rising.

Public awareness is Suspected: the world officially agrees psi does not exist — no conclusive proof, no clean media capture — but roughly 1 in 10 people wholeheartedly believes, and a quarter of those say so openly. Charlatans abound, some quietly sponsored by JDIT specifically to discredit the idea. Receivers need to keep what they are secret.

Public reaction, if the truth broke (PSC §2.4): fascinated dread. Not uniform persecution — real fear laced with celebrity worship, cults, and recruitment frenzies. Exposure outcomes are unpredictable, which is precisely what JDIT dreads: chaos, not pogroms. An outed receiver faces a lottery — mob, media circus, cult adoption, or a foreign recruiter's van, sometimes all in one week.

Consequences at the table

  • PSC warns that Uncommon frequency naturally degrades secrecy toward "suspected" and defeats all-encompassing factions — which is exactly the world as chosen: JDIT's monopoly (003-jdit-keeps-the-field-clear) is policy, not omniscience. Free receivers, believer networks, and small underground groups exist everywhere ANSCOM isn't looking.
  • The disbelief shield: most people write off psi effects as coincidence, so discreet receivers get away with a great deal — but a true believer may recognize the real thing (PSC §2.3's "compensating advantage").
  • Word-of-mouth makes receivers organize for self-protection — the minor organizations JDIT mows down are a permanent crop, not a finite list.
  • PC receivers carry Secret (Psi) [-30] — costed via PSC §2.4's procedure (PSC questionnaire C20, with B10–14) and settled as ANSCOM doctrine: with the Enemy that exposure buys, the halved total passes the maximum "Possible Death" tier (ANSCOM).
  • Debunker NPCs (Quester / Skeptic / Gainsayer, PSC §2.3) are a standing cast resource; some are JDIT assets.

Alternatives rejected

  • Rare (~2,000) — cleaner for the monopoly, but too sparse for monster-of-the-week casework density and the bloom's visible pressure.
  • Unknown awareness — at these numbers PSC prices this as a massive conspiracy under terminal strain; the table preferred a world already murmuring.
  • Denied awareness — one rung too loud: half-open operation by receivers would undercut the containment premise.

Rules basis

  • Frequency tiers and consequences: 54-psionic-campaigns §2.2.
  • Awareness states, charlatan ecology, Secret requirement: 54-psionic-campaigns §2.3.
Canon decision 008

Canon 008: The founding of the JDIT

Date: 2026-08-22 Status: Accepted

Context

Stage 3 history work: the turning point that created the current order (003-jdit-keeps-the-field-clear needed an origin). Core questionnaire asks for the event, its three versions, and its physical remains. The record then had to answer a second question the first one raised — what JDIT is actually made of, given that two hostile programmes were supposed to have become it.

Decision

1987, the Baltic. A Soviet shipment of crude psychotronic tech was targeted for sabotage by a US operations team while in transit on the Baltic Sea. Among the cargo was an experimental biological substance, psionically reactive. The raid breached it; the substance leaked and began self-reproducing by consuming marine biological matter. Containment and cleanup succeeded but took months, and was covered up as an oil spill. That area of the Baltic remains a hotspot for weird phenomena to this day (Baltic Hotspot).

The bargain. At the Cold War's end the US pushed to terminate the USSR's psionics research and operations. The negotiated compromise: a covert agency under the UN umbrella — where the US holds much greater control and say — which a weakened USSR judged better than an all-out shadow war between the superpowers. That agency is JDIT. The US (and later China) refused the clause forbidding independent national initiatives (003-jdit-keeps-the-field-clear) — the power that caused the Baltic Incident kept its own options open while closing everyone else's.

The inheritance. PALE LANTERN and NII-19 became JDIT. It is their successor body, not the keeper of their archives. But neither bloc transferred what it could keep, and both treated the remainder as future leverage:

  • The US, holding the leverage, gave up operational know-how, doctrine, and basic training technique — and its junior and mid-ranking personnel. It kept the advanced techniques its elite operatives had developed, and, more importantly, it kept the operatives. Its most senior, knowledgeable, and powerful people were decommissioned on paper: those judged useless were killed, those judged trustworthy and useful were retired and kept tabs on. Where the second group went is not recorded here and must not be (003-jdit-keeps-the-field-clear — the parallel-programme rumor is this question, and its unconfirmed status is the canon).
  • The USSR, holding less, gave up the basic scientific corpus and some tech specs — its science had largely stalled by 1987 — a minority of its scientists, none of them the best or most experienced, and its younger, less-trained operatives. It kept the advanced prototypes. Its old guard was decommissioned in earnest — needled (023-retirement-is-a-needle; the drug is the USSR's own, first tested 1977, and crossed whole at the founding) — with an un-needled reserve held back against future need.
  • The Soviet half arrived in two stages. The second was 1991: the state holding the remainder dissolved, nobody was maintaining the reserve, and JDIT took by sweep what it had not been given by treaty — the lion's share of the withheld material, plus the lives of such reservists as it found. What it did not reach first was privatized. See Dissolution of the USSR.

JDIT was therefore founded decapitated, from both directions and by design. Its 1987 intake was two B teams who had never been taught by the people who knew. Thirty-nine years on, that intake is JDIT's senior leadership.

Consequences at the table

  • JDIT's DNA explained: born of superpower guilt and leverage, tilted toward US influence, allergic to biological psi-materials.
  • The hotspot is a standing case generator and the campaign's first canonical weird-site (Baltic Hotspot).
  • The institution's blind spots are structural, not moral. JDIT is not complacent so much as under-taught and out of road — see Founding of the JDIT for the science curve and the operational gap this produced.
  • Reopened-file arcs have an anchor: 1987 cleanup veterans and missing samples.
  • Living memory splits by what it can actually reveal. JDIT's own '87 cohort hold no founding-era secrets: presence at the merger conferred nothing, and whatever secrets they do carry come from JDIT's own later work. The people with something left to reveal are the withheld old guard on both sides — the hidden Soviet reservists and the US operatives retired and watched. They are very old — though a few may have quietly extended themselves with biokinetic or psychometabolic technique: chronologically ancient, biologically nothing of the sort. The PCs can be too late — or can find someone who declined to age.
  • Post-Soviet poachers are a case class: withheld prototypes in private hands, sold on, and turning up in incidents nobody at ANSCOM can source.
  • The substance's origin is settled and buried: secreted by a Chernobyl containment worker, a tumor-ridden latent psi (011-the-substance-came-from-subject-357, Subject 357).

Three versions of history (core Stage 3):

  • Commoner: a bad tanker spill in the Baltic in '87; fishing was ruined for a couple of seasons.
  • Ruler (the machinery of national governments, not CANOPY-read — each signatory's read-in few sit almost always inside its intelligence service, JDIT §Governance): a US sabotage operation against Soviet weapons tech went wrong; classified environmental damage; the diplomatic fallout quietly midwifed a UN defense initiative.
  • Specialist (CANOPY-read): the substance was psionically reactive and self-replicating; cleanup took months and some phenomena never fully stopped. What the substance was and where the Soviets got it — still restricted, still argued about. (The truth: 011-the-substance-came-from-subject-357.)

Physical remains: the hotspot zone itself; sealed cleanup archives; retired cleanup personnel (aging, scattered, some talkative); whatever samples survived containment — officially none.

Alternatives rejected

  • Absorption, not succession — JDIT as a new body that merely took custody of two dead programmes' files. Rejected: it makes the '87 lexicon merger and the welded Berger scale (013-the-berger-scale) inexplicable, and it leaves JDIT with no institutional character to inherit.
  • Wholesale merger of both staffs — two hostile cultures in one corridor. Rejected: it hands JDIT the expertise of both programmes, which makes its present-day ignorance of the bloom impossible to motivate.
  • Skeleton transfer only — a vetted handful crossing, succession as a legal fiction. Rejected: too thin to carry the doctrine and vocabulary that demonstrably did cross.

Rules basis

  • Cold War remote-viewing research as the standard modern hook: 54-psionic-campaigns §1.2.
  • Psychotronics as tech: 54-psionic-campaigns §3.3; detailed gadgets pending need from 53-psionic-powers.
  • Psi-boosting drugs are supported psi-tech: 54-psionic-campaigns §Psi drugs — which also advises using real drugs rather than inventing custom ones.

How the players can learn it

Clearance climb; a hotspot case that matches 1987 cleanup signatures; a dying cleanup veteran; Soviet-era archives surfacing through the 003-jdit-keeps-the-field-clear parallel-program rumor mill; an old hand who was a rookie in Vienna in 1987 and remembers what his seniors would not teach him.

Canon decision 009

Canon 009: The Undertow is peopled

Date: 2026-08-22 Status: Accepted

Context

Stage 3's Victorian turning point ("some séance circles genuinely touched the Undertow, and a few things looked back") forced the question 005-the-world-is-dualistic left silent: does the Undertow have inhabitants?

Decision

The local (Terran) Undertow is mostly populated by thoughtforms: entities congealed from collective human feeling, or echoes of dead people. This is the common case — what most "spirits" and hauntings are.

Alien intelligences sometimes visit. They range from minor and non-sapient to vastly intelligent and wholly alien. Such contacts are exceedingly rare: JDIT holds no first-hand account — only unconfirmed stories collected across decades of research and fieldwork.

The Gardener is very present in the Undertow — as a looming, diffuse presence. It coalesces into something with agency and consciousness only for brief moments (010-the-gardener-thinks-through-receivers).

Structure: the Undertow comprises both astral layers of the PP model — the near field (overlapping the real world; "the Shallows" in field speech, where dreamers drift) and the far field (field name: "the Depths", confirmed 2026-08-25). The far field is associated with abstract concepts and feelings — deeper resonances and echoes of human minds live there, a manifestation of the collective unconscious: shared ideas, myths, and archetypes coalesce into semi-entities. At the far field's "core" sits the Gardener's core — though topography there is non-linear, non-deterministic, and barely comprehensible. Its signal strengthens with depth (025-corruption-is-openness's "deep band" is literal depth).

The Victorian layer (004-ancient-cultivation-modern-bloom era detail): the séance craze was mass amateur dabbling at the Undertow's edge. Mostly fraud — but some circles genuinely touched it, and a few things looked back. Surviving residue: haunted séance sites, and one or two things that never left.

Consequences at the table

  • Ghost cases have a real taxonomy: echo (replaying imprint), thoughtform (feeling-fed, possibly hostile), or — vanishingly rare — a visitor. ANSCOM can misfile all three as "liminic events."
  • Mass emotion is generative: disasters, cults, and panics can breed thoughtforms. The fascinated-dread exposure scenario (007-one-in-a-hundred-thousand-suspected-world) would seed the Undertow with new entities — a hidden cost of any public revelation.
  • Visitor stories give the GM a sanctioned "wholly alien one-off" slot without adding a second living force to the cosmology (004-ancient-cultivation-modern-bloom).
  • Victorian séance sites are ready-made residue adventure locations, and "the things that never left" are two unkeyed campaign assets.

Alternatives rejected

  • Echoes only / empty astral — rejected; loses thoughtform horror.
  • Full native ecosystem — rejected; the Undertow's population should track humanity's feelings, keeping people the source of most monsters.

Rules basis

  • Astral plane as setting canvas: 54-psionic-campaigns §2.5.
  • Astral projection mechanics: 53-psionic-powers (Astral Projection power). Thoughtform/echo stat-work is house territory — build via 10-powers ability construction when first needed.

How the players can learn it

Residue cases that answer back; a séance-site case with period documents; the field-lore rumor mill; eventually, meeting something that was never human.

Canon decision 010

Canon 010: The Gardener thinks through receivers

Date: 2026-08-22 Status: Accepted

Context

Describing the Gardener's presence in the Undertow (009-the-undertow-is-peopled) surfaced the mechanism behind "semi-awakened" (006-the-gardener-fell-in-deep-time) and gave the breeding program (002-psi-is-the-gardeners-cultivation) its precise purpose.

Decision

In the Undertow the Gardener exists as a looming, diffuse presence. It coalesces into agency and consciousness only for brief moments. The reason is structural: it needs more psis to act as its "neurons" to fully instantiate its cognition. Receivers are not merely future housing — they are the distributed substrate its full mind will run on.

Therefore:

  • "Semi-awakened" is not injury or sleep alone — it is insufficient hardware. The Fall left it without whatever once carried its mind.
  • The breeding program is the slow manufacture of a neural substrate: every new receiver, every potency gain per generation, adds capacity.
  • Integration is already happening: through its Undertow presence the Gardener psionically infects receivers, colonizing cognition gradually — not a harvest event awaited, but an ongoing wiring-in (015-the-gardener-is-primal).
  • The 20th-century bloom (004-ancient-cultivation-modern-bloom) means the substrate is compounding — its moments of coalesced consciousness are growing longer, more frequent, more deliberate. There is no threshold or inflection ahead: capability and cognitive complexity rise continuously with seed and vessel count — perhaps exponentially; the curve's exact shape is deliberately undefined (016-its-mind-grows-with-its-thralls).

Consequences at the table

  • The stakes clock has a mechanism: receiver count × potency ≈ how much mind the Gardener can run — refined by 016-its-mind-grows-with-its-thralls: corrupted psis are the active substrate, and sophistication scales with them. ANSCOM containment (and lethality) literally keeps the enemy stupid — without anyone knowing it.
  • Horrific dilemma baked in: every receiver the PCs save, recruit, or are is capacity for the enemy's cognition. The PCs' own existence feeds the threat.
  • Its interventions are episodic by nature — plans executed in brief coalescences, then left running on instinct and seed automation. This explains both deep patience and occasional blunders.
  • Endgame geometry: force the dial down (cull/limit receivers — monstrous), break substrate linkage (how?), or treat with a mind that only ever grows more capable. All three are live.

Alternatives rejected

  • Vessels as pure housing (bodies to wear, cognition intact elsewhere) — rejected as the primary mechanism: wearing bodies may still be part of the end state, but cognition-through-receivers is why the program needs many receivers, not a few perfect ones.

How the players can learn it

Receivers' shared dreams during its coalescence moments; incident timing correlating with receiver-population milestones; a seed's behavior when local receivers die; late-campaign direct contact.

Canon decision 012

Canon 012: Power estranges

Date: 2026-08-23 Status: Accepted

Context

Setting the campaign's psi-strength dial (PSC E26, 54-psionic-campaigns §3.1) surfaced a world rule rather than a number.

Decision

Psi runs at standard Psionic Powers strength — but growing very powerful changes the receiver: the higher the power, the more alien the person becomes, mentally and even physically. This is the cultivation showing (002-psi-is-the-gardeners-cultivation, 005-the-world-is-dualistic's body-is-fair-game axis, 010-the-gardener-thinks-through-receivers) — potency is progress toward being usable substrate, and it does not come free.

JDIT knows this pattern (empirically — not its cause) and monitors its psionic operatives closely and frequently: psychological evaluation, physical examination, liminon-signature baselining. ANSCOM careers end at thresholds, quietly.

The regime is inherited, and it did not start as surveillance. PALE LANTERN kept tabs on its agents' use of the stimulants it dosed them with, having learned those drugs wore an operative down over time (031-psi-runs-hot) — the point was to stop a good agent burning himself out. The practice crossed with the tradecraft in 1987 and hardened into what ANSCOM runs now (Founding of the JDIT). JDIT invented neither of the instruments it turns on its own psis: the watching came from Washington, the needle from Moscow (023-retirement-is-a-needle).

Consequences at the table

  • PC advancement carries a horror tax: buying up potency is buying toward alienation. The GM narrates drift (sleep, empathy, appetite, anatomy — scaled to points in psi) as powers grow.
  • Monitoring is a standing in-fiction pressure on PCs: hide your growth from your own side, or accept scrutiny — both are stories.
  • High-power NPC receivers are presumptively strange — rogue cases at the top end are body-horror cases by default.
  • "What happened to the operatives who crossed the line?" — answered 2026-08-24: the decommissioning needle for those who leave (023-retirement-is-a-needle); catatonia and seed-formation for those who go all the way (026-the-end-of-the-line-is-a-seed).
  • JDIT cannot reliably distinguish power-driven estrangement from breaks caused by weird experiences (Undertow contact, trauma from a noise) — the monitoring regime treats strong receivers and weird-case survivors with the same nervous scrutiny. Jargon for the "psi-crazy": one clinical state — liminic overexposure syndrome (its proper name still unminted — Open Threads); field says "deaf" (Liminic overexposure syndrome). The etiology split was never real: all roads feed the same Corruption (021-the-roads-in).

Alternatives rejected

  • Progressive psi (world-wide escalation via bonus capability, PSC §3.1) — declined as a mechanic; the bloom already escalates the world diegetically.
  • Wimpy psi — declined; standard strength keeps rogue receivers genuinely dangerous under full lethality.

Rules basis

  • Strength dials: 54-psionic-campaigns §3.1.
  • Standard framework: 53-psionic-powers.
  • The estrangement trajectory is house canon (no KB mechanic); model drift narratively or via disadvantages when milestones hit.

How the players can learn it

The monitoring regime itself; a veteran operative's file; meeting what a crossed-threshold receiver became.

Canon decision 013

Canon 013: The Berger scale

Date: 2026-08-23 Status: Accepted

Context

The GM wanted a StarCraft-Ghost / WH40K-style power ladder distinguishing potential (what you could become) from manifest power (what you can do), grounded in the LAM biology of 005-the-world-is-dualistic and the potential-vs-training spectrum ruled in 2026-08-23-mechanics (B12: Talent uncapped).

Decision

The fiction

JDIT rates every receiver on the Berger scale: manifest rating and ceiling, written B3/C7, spoken "a three over seven." The rating is properly per power — "a Berger 3, ceiling 5 telepath" — with an overall figure quoted in dossier headlines. Ceiling is estimated by assay — the percentage of liminically active neurons — but the correlation is imperfect: more organelle usually means more potential, not always. A measured ceiling can be wrong by a band either way.

Telepathy comes first. Every receiver manifests some telepathic aptitude before anything else: minds are liminon-active matter (liminically active neurons; even baseline minds couple feebly), so affecting them is cheap. Manifesting kinetic effects means organizing vastly more liminons against their native non-interactivity (005-the-world-is-dualistic) — it is far easier to control someone's mind than to move a coin.

Converting potential into manifest power is training the nervous system to fire in specific patterns — muscle memory for the mind. During training, some receivers develop crutches: verbal and/or gestural routines (mudras, mantras, sketched signs) that make manifestation deliberate and reliably repeatable. ANSCOM trains its agents to push past crutches; it doesn't always succeed, especially with receivers who self-trained before recruitment. Crutch-use carries real stigma inside the psionic community.

The scale is named for Hans Berger — the inventor of the EEG, who built it trying to prove telepathy existed, and who spent his last three years running a Reich programme at Jena to breed and force psionic supermen out of captured subjects (The Berger Programme, The Jena Lab).

The scale has two parents, and that is why it is a slash-pair. Berger built a proto-scale in the Jena programme (1938–41, Hans Berger, The Jena Lab): bands of demonstrated capability, graded by what a subject could actually be made to do, because capability was the only thing his instruments could reach. US forces stripped the lab in 1945 and recruited surviving programme scientists through Operation Paperclip; that lineage — PALE LANTERN — carried the proto-scale, the banding logic, and the name. It became the manifest rating.

The Soviets took the site that July (Jena Inheritance), and what they recovered from it was not data. It was a fact: that the Germans had believed they had proof, and had been experimenting on that belief. No results, no method, no theory — an existence proof, and a second-hand one.

That bought a small programme and no more (NII-19). Soviet leadership was pragmatic and the proposition was speculative, so the work started minor, stayed minor, and produced little for years. The break was intelligence, not science. The Soviets learned of the US programme and then infiltrated it — at relatively low clearance, the same trade they had worked against Manhattan. Modest access was enough for the only question that mattered: the Americans had succeeded. From that moment it was another arms race, and the Party funded it like one.

What the money bought was still entirely Soviet work. Having inherited no theory, they had to build one — biologically rather than behaviourally — and arrived pre-'87 at the anomalous neuron type (Discovery of the Psi Neuron) and at the assay: ceiling estimated from the percentage of those neurons — now formally the liminically active neurons (Liminically active neurons). That became the ceiling.

The notation is a fossil of the two doctrines. The Americans, handed a working theory, judged it foundation enough and went straight to application — a programme organized around warfare and espionage, and a rating that measures what an asset can be made to do. The Soviets, handed nothing, had to understand the phenomenon before they could use it — the physics, the biology, the clinical picture, and a rating that measures what a person is. Each bloc contributed the half its doctrine made it good at, which is why the domain-keyed stratigraphy in GLOSSARY works at all: ops vocabulary is American because the Americans skipped the theory, and the science vocabulary is Soviet because the Soviets had no choice but to do it.

The 1987 standardization welded them together. B/C notation is a US-carried name and capability band bolted to a wholly independent Soviet biological assay — two research traditions with no common ancestor past a rumour, forced into one notation by a treaty. That is why the halves disagree often enough that "a measured ceiling can be wrong by a band either way" had to become a standing rule. They are not measuring the same thing, they never were, and nobody at the merger was in a position to say so out loud.

Canon decision 014

Canon 014: ANSCOM salvages its dead

Date: 2026-08-24 Status: Accepted

Context

The GM's ethos ruling: JDIT/ANSCOM must not read as a bastion against evil — the organization needs genuinely shady practices. This is the first one made canon.

Decision

ANSCOM always collects the bodies of dead operatives. Everyone in the organization knows this. Few know what happens next: if the fallen operative was strong enough and death was recent enough, surviving brain matter is salvaged and integrated into a gestalt of several other such brains.

This grisly assembly is used as a very powerful psi-amplifier for wide-ranging or long-range telepathy work — the canonical example: editing the memories of an entire small town's population. The brains carry echoes of their deaths' trauma and shards of the persons' personalities (009-the-undertow-is-peopled — they are, in effect, bottled echoes wired together). Use is therefore sparing, and restricted to very senior, very trusted agents.

Timeline: the amplifier is post-1987 JDIT technology — it became possible only with bloom-era receiver strength and JDIT's industrialized salvage doctrine (008-the-founding-of-the-jdit). Most veterans assume the body-collection tradition is older than its true purpose.

The doctrine is also biosecurity (ruled 2026-08-31). A receiver's corpse is a molecular exposure vector: the enhanced plasmid and its docked complexes survive in uncollected neural tissue at anomalous density, and one research autopsy or donated brain in the wrong pipeline is a masquerade breach (033-psi-rides-a-modified-mitochondrion). Collection protects the secret as much as it feeds the amplifier — and the interest extends past ANSCOM's own dead to any receiver or watched latent whose body might route toward research channels (current doctrine in ANSCOM — the death-watch). The tradition thus has three readings, one per clearance stratum: dignity, inventory, quarantine.

Mechanics: modeled as psychotronics — a large installed psi-amplifier (gadget/installation), not the living-gestalt rules in 53-psionic-powers; it is gestalt-like in fiction only. Stat it if and when play requires.

Consequences at the table

  • Explains the durability of the Suspected equilibrium (007-one-in-a-hundred-thousand-suspected-world): major exposure events can be rolled back. Town-scale memory editing is the deepest tool behind 003-jdit-keeps-the-field-clear.
  • Every operative — including the PCs — is future amplifier stock. The known body-collection policy reads as dignity; the truth reads as inventory. Discovering the gap is a campaign-grade betrayal beat.
  • Operator duty is horror duty: senior agents working the amplifier touch dying trauma and personality shards. Ties to 012-power-estranges — answered 2026-08-24: it does; the amplifier is a cursed artifact and operating it corrupts (021-the-roads-in).
  • Deliberate ambiguity (do not resolve casually): the amplifier is a miniature of the Gardener's own architecture — amplified cognition on networked receiver brains (010-the-gardener-thinks-through-receivers). Convergence, or quiet steering? And is the amplifier visible to the diffuse presence in the Undertow when lit?

Alternatives rejected

  • Modeling as a PP gestalt — rejected by the GM: mechanically it is a psychotronic amplifier; the gestalt is flavor.
  • Cold War origin — rejected: however competent the programmes were (013-the-berger-scale), the amplifier needs bloom-era donor strength and an industrialized salvage pipeline, and neither existed before 1987 (008-the-founding-of-the-jdit).

Rules basis

  • Psychotronics as a setting technology: 54-psionic-campaigns §3.3; 53-psionic-powers (psychotronics topic). The amplifier itself is house canon — no published gadget matches it.

How the players can learn it

Clearance climb; a collection team arriving before the medevac; a salvage-eligible PC death handled strangely; an operator NPC who has touched the thing and drinks; the amplifier's signature on a case that was "resolved" by mass amnesia.

Canon decision 015

Canon 015: The Gardener is primal

Date: 2026-08-24 Status: Accepted

Context

Freeform refinement of the Gardener's cognition and methods (006-the-gardener-fell-in-deep-time, 010-the-gardener-thinks-through-receivers) — how deliberate is the cultivation, really?

Decision

The Gardener is a half-awake, half-asleep alien cognition. Its cultivation is instinctual rather than thought-out: intent exists, but it is erratic and unfocused. It is a very primal entity.

Growing receivers is how it expands cognitively. Through its presence in the Undertow it psionically "infects" psis — integration into its substrate (010-the-gardener-thinks-through-receivers) is not a future harvest event but an ongoing colonization, receiver by receiver.

The cultivation is crude. The Gardener senses psis far more clearly than regular people — receivers are distinctly present in the Undertow, while mundane minds are dim (faintly less so in slow-wave sleep — 027-everyone-is-broadcasting). Its selection mechanism is simply fitness amplification of what it can see: it whispers to sensitive psis' subconscious — the whispers land in sleep, where reception sharpens (027-everyone-is-broadcasting) — and seeps into their physiology, making them more alert to danger, more alluring to others — and so, over generations, more reproductively successful. No matchmaking, no design: the potent get blessed, and the blessing compounds.

Consequences at the table

  • The seeds' "nudging" (002-psi-is-the-gardeners-cultivation) is this whispering-and-seeping, not deliberate pairing management — selection is emergent, which is why it took megayears and why the pattern is hard to prove: there is no plan to uncover, only a gradient.
  • Erratic intent explains inconsistent opposition: some cases feel orchestrated, others random — both are true. The Gardener's attention flickers.
  • Being a strong receiver feels good: sharper instincts, magnetism, luck-adjacent survival. The blessing is real, which is the horror — PCs benefit from the thing that is colonizing them.
  • Its clear sensing of receivers vs. dim sensing of mundanes has tactical weight: mundane operatives are near-invisible to it; strong psis are beacons. ANSCOM would invert its own doctrine if it knew.
  • Corruption is alienation plus the openness model (018-corruption-is-alienation, 025-corruption-is-openness) — one substance reached through many doors (021-the-roads-in), with the Gardener as the usual occupant but not the definitional one. That is why JDIT cannot tell alienation drift from weird-experience breaks, and why the power-keyed Corruption floor has a mechanism: the powerful are louder in the Undertow, hence more whispered-at (019-loudness-sets-a-floor).

Alternatives rejected

  • Chess-master Gardener (deliberate breeding plans, orchestrated history) — rejected as the current state: too knowable, too fightable. A gradient cannot be out-plotted. But the rejection is era-scoped: 016-its-mind-grows-with-its-thralls makes the cunning mastermind the explicit endgame trajectory if corruption proceeds.

How the players can learn it

The blessing's statistics (strong receivers are uncannily lucky, pretty, hard to ambush); dream-content convergence among potent psis; the flicker — cases where the opposition's competence varies wildly for no tactical reason.

Canon decision 016

Canon 016: Its mind grows with its thralls

Date: 2026-08-24 Status: Accepted

Context

Follow-on to 015-the-gardener-is-primal: is the primal state permanent, and what does the Gardener feel?

Decision

The more the Gardener corrupts, the more "brain power" it gains — and the more aware and sophisticated its actions become. Its primal state (015-the-gardener-is-primal) is a stage, not a nature: given sufficient psis to corrupt, it might become a cunning alien intelligence shaping the world from the shadows — or openly.

Its emotional register evolves with its cognition. Right now it is driven by anger and revenge; as it strengthens it may become colder and more calculating. The anger shows: corrupted individuals tend to become more violent and aggressive than they were before — not always, but normally. The entity's current mood bleeds through its thralls.

Consequences at the table

  • The metaplot's stakes are a dial, not a threshold: almost every corrupted psi raises the opposition's intelligence (softened 2026-08-24 — the rare non-Gardener wavelength is the exception, 018-corruption-is-alienation). Campaign eras can be paced by the Gardener's visible sophistication — instinctive flicker → tactical cunning → strategy → (if unchecked) open mastery.
  • 010-the-gardener-thinks-through-receivers: corrupted psis are the active substrate. ANSCOM's lethal containment of violent rogues is — unknowingly — enemy lobotomy, which partially explains why the Flowering has taken this long despite the bloom.
  • The violence signature gives overexposure-syndrome cases (012-power-estranges, Liminic overexposure syndrome) a diagnostic texture JDIT misreads: aggression looks like madness but is mood — the entity is angry, and its thralls inherit it. If the anger cools as it grows, late-campaign corrupted will turn eerily calm — a dreadful tell that things are getting worse, not better.
  • Per 009-the-undertow-is-peopled, the Undertow is feeling-shaped: the Gardener's rage is weather there. Undertow travelers may read its emotional state directly — the campaign's strangest intelligence source.
  • GM discipline: run early-campaign opposition angry and crude; let cold competence arrive gradually and unannounced. Players noticing "it's getting smarter" is the intended dread beat.

Alternatives rejected

  • Fixed primal nature — rejected; a static antagonist wastes the bloom's escalation.
  • Cold from the start — rejected; anger-first gives the violence signature and makes the later cooling legible as escalation.

How the players can learn it

The violence pattern across overexposure-syndrome case files (rising, then — worse — falling); Undertow weather; the opposition's competence curve across seasons.

Canon decision 017

Canon 017: ANSCOM feeds what it fights

Date: 2026-08-24 Status: Accepted

Context

Drawing the institutional consequences of 015-the-gardener-is-primal and 016-its-mind-grows-with-its-thralls: what does the corruption ecology mean for ANSCOM's mission?

Decision

Three linked facts:

1. ANSCOM's rogue-hunting doctrine is correct on its own terms. Rogue psis who aren't already violent and dangerous are bound to become so (the violence signature, 016-its-mind-grows-with-its-thralls). The doctrine needs no conspiracy to justify it — the case files keep proving it right. 2. The hunting genuinely slows the Gardener. Almost every corrupted psi contained or killed is substrate removed — unknowing enemy lobotomy (010-the-gardener-thinks-through-receivers; softened 2026-08-24: the rare non-Gardener wavelength is the exception, 018-corruption-is-alienation). 3. But ANSCOM's growth feeds the enemy. Every receiver it recruits, trains, and strengthens is a new potential vessel shining in the Undertow — and its experiments with psionically reactive substances and technology (the amplifier program, 014-anscom-salvages-its-dead; whatever Baltic-derived research survives, 011-the-substance-came-from-subject-357) may be opening doors nobody can see. The institution fighting the colonization is also its supply line.

Corruption is already inside. Some individuals within JDIT — including ANSCOM — are corrupted; extent and seniority undefined. Coordination ruled 2026-08-24: none — the Gardener cannot yet run a network; they are uncoordinated sleepers converging on one will (025-corruption-is-openness; first concretization: The Blight).

Consequences at the table

  • The campaign's central irony has a full circuit: hunt harder → slow the enemy; grow stronger to hunt harder → feed the enemy. There is no clean policy, which is the cosmic-dread thesis in institutional form.
  • Every ANSCOM victory is real AND every ANSCOM expansion is a loss — the GM can honestly play both without contradiction.
  • Internal corruption converts the org from backdrop to battlefield: vetting, monitoring (012-power-estranges), and the amplifier's senior-trusted operator pool are all now attack surfaces.
  • The PCs themselves are inside the bind: getting stronger makes them better hunters and better hosts.

Rules basis

  • None needed — pure setting structure. Mechanical face arrives with the Corruption design pass (Open Threads).

How the players can learn it

The math of their own case history (kills correlate with quiet periods); a corrupted colleague; eventually, the unbearable brief: "our recruitment program is their agriculture."

Canon decision 018

Canon 018: Corruption is alienation

Date: 2026-08-24 Status: Accepted

Context

The Corruption design pass (Open Threads §Corruption mechanics), unblocked by the Horror KB extraction (55-horror §Power Corrupts). The candidate meaning on the table was Corruption = thralldom (the GM's "maybe" in 015-the-gardener-is-primal).

Decision

Corruption measures alienation — the degree to which a psyche has become alien to the mundane world and to human experience. It is not inherently Gardener colonization: colonization is one road in, but any door — power growth, Undertow exposure, weird events — deposits the same substance.

The openness model. Alienation opens the mind to the Undertow. Corruption is the opening; what comes through depends on who is broadcasting. The Gardener is by far the strongest and closest broadcaster, so the corrupted usually pick up its proto-mind and tune to its primal desires — usually, but not necessarily. Other presences (009-the-undertow-is-peopled's residents, Visitors) can capture a corrupted mind that strays into their reach.

The tuning evolves with the Gardener (016-its-mind-grows-with-its-thralls): today the corrupted are merely tuned to its erratic primal desires; as it grows more sophisticated, its influence over corrupted psis becomes stronger and more calculated, trending toward direct control.

Mechanical chassis: GURPS Horror Corruption, close to RAW (55-horror §Power Corrupts). Corruption converts into mental disadvantages drawn from a curated, weighted list aligned with the Gardener's ethos: disadvantages matching its current register are more probable (e.g. Bloodlust while it is angry), misaligned ones less. The weights are era-dependent — as the entity's emotional register cools (canon 016), the table drains from hot violence (Bloodlust, Bad Temper, Berserk) toward cold calculation — so the "eerily calm late-campaign corrupted" tell emerges from the dice. List curation, thresholds, and accrual sources: this design pass, continued.

Consequences at the table

  • 012-power-estranges gets its mechanical vehicle: estrangement drift is the Corruption total. One substance through many doors — JDIT's diagnostic blindness is preserved because there is genuinely nothing to split.
  • The blessing's trap is now a loop: the Gardener's whispering is a source of Corruption, and Corruption is what makes a mind exploitable. Contact corrupts; corruption opens.
  • A corrupted psi is not automatically an enemy asset — "whose signal is he picking up?" is a live question per case, and the rare non-Gardener wavelength is premium plot inventory.
  • The Gardener's flickering attention (015-the-gardener-is-primal) has a mechanical face: the tuning waxes and wanes with its focus, not with the victim's total alone.

Alternatives rejected

  • Corruption = thralldom identity — rejected: makes every high-Corruption psi Gardener-aligned by definition, wastes the Undertow's other broadcasters, and closes the "whose signal?" question.
  • Split substances (power-floor Corruption as colonization, weird-experience Corruption as mere damage) — rejected: mechanically distinguishable etiologies contradict 012-power-estranges's load-bearing rule that JDIT cannot tell them apart.

Rules basis

  • Corruption framework: 55-horror §Power Corrupts; disadvantage conversion: §Effects of Corruption; GM-secret totals: §Many Roads to Hell.
  • The curated weighted disadvantage table and era-shifting weights are house rules layered on the RAW conversion.

How the players can learn it

The pattern in overexposure-syndrome case files; a corrupted contact who is manifestly not running the Gardener's errands; the day a PC's own disadvantage roll lands and the GM describes what it felt like to receive.

Canon decision 019

Canon 019: Loudness sets a floor

Date: 2026-08-24 Status: Accepted

Context

The Corruption design pass, continuing from 018-corruption-is-alienation. The Open Threads ledger's original question: a minimum Corruption floor set by power level, as the mechanical vehicle for 012-power-estranges.

Decision

Every psi has a Corruption floor — a minimum total that cannot be bought off or cleansed below. The rationale is the openness model: power and potential make a mind loud in the Undertow, and the loud are whispered-at whether or not they ever act. Alienation is the standing cost of being audible. (Physically this is antenna reciprocity: an array phased to emit is identically phased to receive — 033-psi-rides-a-modified-mitochondrion.)

Potential counts — but only Undertow-linked potential. Talent in powers that work through the Undertow (Telepathy, Astral Projection, ESP; full taxonomy pending the ability-curation pass) contributes to loudness even if never manifested. Talent in powers that merely organize liminons locally (Psychokinesis — Pyrokinesis and kin are abilities within PK, not separate powers) does not — an unmanifested kinetic is silent; an unmanifested telepath is already an open ear.

Formula (TEMPORARY calibration, revisit before session 0):

> Floor = 5 × max(manifest Berger band, highest Undertow-linked > Talent level)

  • Since every receiver has Telepathy Talent ≥ 1 (013-the-berger-scale), every receiver has a floor of at least 5 — there are no clean receivers, only quiet ones.
  • A never-manifested high-Talent latent carries a real floor and rolls monthly against it: civilians who go quietly mad, hearing voices, without ever manifesting — a canonical case-genesis JDIT will misfile, since there is no manifest power to flag.
  • The rolls machinery (monthly Will at -1 per full 10, conversion per 55-horror §Effects of Corruption, 25-point single-gain surge rolls) grinds on the floor forever: estrangement is a machine, not a narration.
  • Conversion clamps at the floor (ruled 2026-08-24): the RAW rule that converting Corruption reduces the running total applies only to the above-floor margin. A psi who keeps using their powers is never done being subject to corruption.

Consequences at the table

  • 012-power-estranges's "growing very powerful changes the receiver" is now an is, not a risk — no discipline keeps a strong psi clean.
  • Canon 013's monitoring thresholds (clinically notable ~B6, career review ~B8) should fall out of the floor math once calibrated — tune the formula so they do.
  • Advancement decisions price alienation in: raising your band raises your floor, permanently.
  • The latent-madness genesis quietly seeds cases for JDIT files and ties the civilian world to the metaplot without any manifest event.

Alternatives rejected

  • No floor — rejected: a careful psi could stay clean forever, contradicting canon 012.
  • Fold Talent into the band table (count Undertow Talent as ability points, floor = 5 × resulting band) — declined for now in favor of simplicity; revisit at calibration.
  • Two-term sum (5 × B + k × Talent) — declined for now; most tunable, but a second dial to maintain.

Rules basis

  • Corruption effects machinery: 55-horror §Effects of Corruption (monthly cadence and 25-point surge trigger are house adjustments to the RAW weekly roll).
  • The floor itself, its formula, and the Undertow-linked Talent rule are house rules.

How the players can learn it

The monitoring regime's actuarial tables; a latent case file with no manifest history and a familiar decline curve; their own sheets, slowly.

Canon decision 020

Canon 020: The door opens from inside

Date: 2026-08-24 Status: Accepted

Context

The Corruption design pass (018-corruption-is-alienation, 019-loudness-sets-a-floor): can psis voluntarily draw on Corruption for power?

Decision

Any psi can open themselves to the Undertow for power — and almost everyone instinctively knows how. The maneuver takes one extra second (an additional Concentrate maneuver), then the power simply arrives: no FP cost, no extra-effort roll. It is mechanically a dark variant of psi extra effort (53-psionic-powers §Getting tricky) and deliberately far more effective than the legitimate version — that is what makes it tempting.

Two modes, both priced in dice of Corruption, not fixed points — the gamble is the point; deterministic costs would read as a fee, not a risk:

  • Skill boost — bonuses to the psionic skill roll (RAW: +1 per point, 55-horror §Extending Corruption to other abilities).
  • Level-borrowing — one-time use of ability levels not owned (RAW: 1/5 the CP cost of the additional levels, rounded up).

Conversion (TEMPORARY): every ~3.5 points of RAW Corruption cost becomes 1d6 — pure statistical equivalence; find a more idiomatic GURPS expression at calibration.

Borrowed power ignores the Berger ceiling (013-the-berger-scale). A documented C3 can perform C6 work — for a price.

Critical failures on any psi use (all powers, Undertow-linked or not) are the involuntary version: the channel flaps open with nobody holding the door. Corruption gain — a flat die (size TBD at calibration) — is the new default critical-failure special effect, replacing GM improvisation as the baseline; strange manifestations and other punishments may stack on top, but Corruption always accrues.

Consequences at the table

  • Every desperate moment is a devil's bargain the player strikes with themselves — the oppression is interactive, not just ambient.
  • JDIT tracks above-ceiling manifestation as a standing red flag for all agents: receivers repeatedly performing above assay are presumed to be going bad. Assay error (canon 013) is bounded at one band, so mismeasurement innocently explains only modest overshoots — repeated multi-band performances have no innocent explanation JDIT knows of. The dossier ambiguity lives in the one-band margin (and, unknown to everyone, in the astral corpus — 024-the-astral-corpus).
  • ANSCOM's institutional stance on the maneuver (banned? trained against? quietly tolerated in the field?) is deliberately open — it belongs with the "more JDIT shade" thread.
  • Added 2026-08-24: door-opened (and borrowed/stolen) power is what reaches the -30 uber-technique tier no unaided human touches — 030-the-impossible-tier-is-real.

Alternatives rejected

  • Gated access (learned/forbidden technique) — rejected: instinct universalizes the temptation and fits the openness model; the door is part of being a receiver, not a secret.
  • Deterministic costs — rejected: a known price is a budget line, not a gamble.
  • Ceiling-capped borrowing — rejected: exceeding your own biology is the temptation's sharpest edge and JDIT's cleanest tell.

Rules basis

  • Boost/borrow pricing: 55-horror §Extending Corruption to other abilities (psionic rows).
  • Legitimate extra effort, for contrast: 53-psionic-powers §Getting tricky.
  • The opening maneuver, dice conversion, ceiling exemption, and crit-fail default are house rules.

How the players can learn it

They already know — their characters grew up with the instinct. What they learn at the table is the price curve, and what the monitoring regime does to agents who get flagged.

Canon decision 022

Canon 022: Alienation can recede

Date: 2026-08-24 Status: Accepted (rates provisional)

Context

The Corruption design pass: does Corruption cleanse (55-horror §Cleansing Corruption), given the "hopeless in the long run" tone anchor and the uncleansable floor (019-loudness-sets-a-floor)?

Decision

Above-floor Corruption recedes only through sustained, deliberate, mundane humanity — ordinary life, human connection, no exposure. The penance-shadow, secularized. Cleansing runs in cycles (length and rate TBD at calibration), and any use of psionics resets the current cycle's progress to zero. Not psi failure — psi use. The door must stay shut.

Exotic mercies exist but are unkeyed — probably certain less-malignant intelligences in the Undertow (009-the-undertow-is-peopled) can lift Corruption. What they are, what they want, and what they cost is a deliberate ambiguity — plot inventory, do not resolve casually.

Conversion remains the third relief, per RAW: Corruption stabilizes by becoming disadvantages — by becoming you (023-retirement-is-a-needle, "You're this, now").

Mental Surgery exception: regular Mental Surgery cannot touch corruption-converted disadvantages. Excising them is a dedicated psi technique at a ~-30 default — godlike-feat territory: theoretically possible, in practice impossible — and any failure on the attempt is treated as a critical failure. "You're this, now" stands; the asterisk exists so that somewhere in the deep files, someone has tried (2026-08-24-ability-curation).

Consequences at the table

  • Retire or rot is the standing operative's dilemma: the job is psi use, and psi use is the reset trap. Working operatives effectively cannot cleanse — hope exists, and its price is the campaign.
  • JDIT-mandated leave becomes a real institution: pulled off rotation, forbidden to manifest, sent somewhere quiet. Whether an operative complies off the books is a story every time.
  • A PC who wants to come back from the edge must genuinely stop being a psi for months of play-time — the mechanic enforces the theme.

Alternatives rejected

  • Nothing cleanses — rejected: removes recovery arcs and the desperate search for a way back; hopelessness reads strongest when hope exists and is expensive.
  • RAW Meritorious Act / Penance as written — theology doesn't port; their secular shadows (sustained humanity, mandated leave) do.

Rules basis

  • Cleansing framework: 55-horror §Cleansing Corruption (heavily adapted; the reset trap and cycle structure are house rules).

How the players can learn it

Mandated-leave doctrine; a veteran who got better and won't say how; a veteran who tried, slipped once, and stopped trying.

Canon decision 023

Canon 023: Retirement is a needle

> Cross-ref (added 2026-08-24, 2026-08-24-options): the > needle has an uncontrolled natural analogue — astral cord > severance, which violently halts the LAMs and damages the > nervous system; the injection is the engineered version of a > catastrophe the Undertow can inflict on its own.

Date: 2026-08-24 Status: Accepted

Context

The Corruption design pass, cleansing branch — the surgical shadow of 55-horror's "cut it off" ritual, crossed with canon 012's dangling question: ANSCOM careers "end at thresholds, quietly." This is how quietly.

Decision

JDIT possesses a drug that disables the enhanced LAM complement — and kills a substantial fraction of the neurons carrying it. The drug overstimulates neurons, much more markedly the ones running docked complexes: the subject manifests erratically over the course of several hours while the overstimulated neurons die off. It is based on methamphetamine — the real-world drug whose neurotoxicity is most squarely a matter of overstimulation killing the cells it drives.

It reaches the right neurons through their own feeding pathway. Cells running docked complexes run a specialized endocrine route no other neuron has, pulling far more energy from the blood to pay for the complex (031-psi-runs-hot). The modified methamphetamine travels that route. Cells without the pathway have no door for it, which is why the drug hits working receivers so much harder than everything else — and why baseline humanity, and a carrier whose nuclear gate is shut, have no more door than any neuron (033-psi-rides-a-modified-mitochondrion).

NII-19 found it by trial and error from an informed start. The programme understood the endocrine pathway — that part was never much of a secret — and it knew the other end of the biology too: powers feel good to use, which said the cells were dopaminergic and named the drug class. Plain methamphetamine is already a little effective — repeated use degrades psi capability, slowly. From that informed starting point the programme worked outward through chemically adjacent substances by trial and error until it had something that did the job in one dose. The needle is an empirical result, not a designed one.

The fever is part of the procedure. The injected run a dangerous hyperthermia through the hours the drug works, and it is not brought down — cooling the subject would spare neurons, and the neuronal death is the point. Whoever is in the room watches a fever they are deliberately not treating.

The needle also destroys the evidence (ruled 2026-08-31). The dead LAMs are cleared by the cell's own disposal machinery, and the digestion takes the enhanced plasmid with it — a needled brain reads like anyone else's (033-psi-rides-a-modified-mitochondrion). This is what makes needle-then-release compatible with the masquerade: sanitized retirees can die ordinary civilian deaths, autopsies included, with nothing left to find. Chronic street methamphetamine does the same work slowly — a PRUNE target is being erased as well as dimmed (ANSCOM, 031-psi-runs-hot).

An injection is mandatory for every psi leaving ANSCOM. It is the only form of retirement ANSCOM allows its receivers. Non-psi personnel are exempt — the needle exists to close the docked machinery, and they have nothing to close. Anti-psi personnel — DEADWOOD's included — are not needled either.

The needle is Soviet. The USSR wanted a non-lethal way of permanently disabling psis — originally something usable in the field, but what NII-19 came up with takes too long to take effect. First test 1977, on a psi the programme considered rogue (unkeyed). It crossed to JDIT immediately and in full at the 1987 founding (Founding of the JDIT). JDIT's most intimate instrument of internal control is inherited from the side that lost the negotiation.

Mechanical effects, all permanent and unrecoverable:

  • 1d6 IQ damage and 1d6-3 DX damage (minimum 1).
  • All psionic abilities and Talents are lost outright. No refund, no recovery. Beyond crippling.
  • The Corruption floor drops to zero — the docked openness is destroyed, and with it the power-keyed floor (019-loudness-sets-a-floor, 025-corruption-is-openness).
  • No further power-keyed Corruption rolls, and the running total resets to zero. Power-driven Corruption is over forever. What remains is what any mundane faces: baseline coupling survives the needle, so the slow environmental doors — hot ground, residue, contact (021-the-roads-in) — still work on a retiree, at the much slower pace they work on anyone (033-psi-rides-a-modified-mitochondrion). Fresh accrual counts from zero; the old total does not return.
  • Converted disadvantages remain. The corruption has permanently molded the person's brain and psyche — that is what conversion means. "You're this, now."
  • The injected no longer dream. In a manifest receiver, dreaming becomes LAM-dominated: REM is the sharpened reception phase (027-everyone-is-broadcasting), a receiver's REM reception runs at docked grade, and over a career their dreaming is progressively entrained by the docked layer — part experience, part Undertow contact. The needle rips that layer out, and what is left does not reassemble into dreaming. Ex-agents sleep dark; civilians, whose dreaming was never docked-entrained, are untouched.

Injected retirees are no longer viable as PCs. If they appear in play at all, it is to remind the players of the grim cost of doing the work. Those who leave ANSCOM alive always leave it as less than what they were when they came in.

Retirement leaves the retiree broken and depressed, and the population's suicide rate is far above the general one.

Consequences at the table

  • Joining ANSCOM is a one-way door, and every operative is walking toward the needle by one road or another: convert, cross the line, or retire diminished. The org's greyness (Open Threads "more JDIT shade") is now structural, not incidental.
  • The monitoring thresholds (013-the-berger-scale: B6 notable, B8 career review; canon 012's "careers end at thresholds, quietly") get their endpoint: career review at B8 is not a euphemism for a desk.
  • Ex-agents are a distinctive NPC class: dimmed, marked by their old disadvantages, deaf to the broadcast — and possibly bitter about all three. Many do not last long after the chair.
  • There is no good way out. Every agent is a tragic character by the nature of their powers alone, before any personal trouble they bring with them; those pile on top (031-psi-runs-hot, 012-power-estranges, 022-alienation-can-recede).
  • Recruits know the policy, not the price (ruled 2026-08-24): "decommissioning" is disclosed at signing as a mandatory, classified, safe-sounding medical procedure. The IQ/DX damage, the diminishment, the death sentence for advanced cases — none of it. You learn what it costs the first time you meet a decommissioned veteran. Field slang (ruled 2026-08-25): "getting dimmed" / PT "ser apagado" (Decommissioning).

Alternatives rejected

  • Corruption-targeted psychosurgery (excise the taint, keep the psi) — rejected: a return trip would make the horror negotiable. The needle removes the capacity, not the stain; the stain stays.
  • Retirement by surveillance (let them go, watch them) — rejected as the only policy; nothing prevents it existing informally for assets too valuable to dim, which is more JDIT shade to mine.

Rules basis

  • Attribute damage and trait loss are house rules; the "cut it off" precedent: 55-horror §Cleansing Corruption.
  • LAM biology: 005-the-world-is-dualistic, 013-the-berger-scale (assay = liminically-active-neuron count).

How the players can learn it

An ex-agent NPC who used to be brilliant; the exit-interview room with a medical chair in it; the moment a PC first asks "so how do I quit?"

Canon decision 024

Canon 024: The astral corpus

Date: 2026-08-24 Status: Accepted

Context

Pinning the retirement injection's efficacy (023-retirement-is-a-needle): does it always take?

Decision

An astral corpus is any cohesive Undertow-matter with agency — the Undertow's equivalent of a body. Every denizen has one, and JDIT knows the concept: its most readily available data is the astral bodies its own astral-projecting psis manifest, and denizen observations corroborate (in-world term in GLOSSARY).

The liminon exists in both planes simultaneously (005-the-world-is-dualistic) and interacts with both, so the real variable is which side does the controlling — the physical brain, for the overwhelming majority of human psis. In very rare humans it is the astral corpus instead: it carries "astral LAMs," and it, not the brain, is what manifests the powers.

  • This is the default mode for Undertow denizens (009-the-undertow-is-peopled): they operate on astral LAMs. In humans it is VERY rare.
  • The retirement injection does not take on such people — its target is the physical LAM, and theirs is elsewhere.
  • JDIT does not know a psi's astral corpus can become the source of manifestation. It knows astral corpora exist (denizen anatomy); what it does not know is that one can grow developed and complex enough to displace the brain. All consequences below stand on that narrower ignorance.
  • Powers manifested from the Undertow are usually of the Undertow-aligned flavor (Telepathy, Astral Projection, and kin — taxonomy per 019-loudness-sets-a-floor).

Consequences at the table

  • The nightmare file: an injected retiree who keeps manifesting. ANSCOM's protocol says that is impossible, so the first confirmed case breaks doctrine, not just a rule of thumb.
  • The retiree who still dreams (ruled 2026-08-24, ability-curation pass; mechanism re-derived 2026-09-02): the needle ends a receiver's dreaming by ripping out the docked layer that had entrained it (023-retirement-is-a-needle) — but an astral-corpus human's LAMs are on the other side, where the needle has no door at all, so they keep dreaming after injection. A quiet, clean tell for the nightmare file — if anyone thinks to ask a retiree about their sleep.
  • Assays measure physical LAM density (013-the-berger-scale) — an astral-corpus human may read as a low-ceiling nobody while carrying real power. A second, deeper source of "impossible performance" alongside assay error and the open door (020-the-door-opens-from-inside). Unlike canon 013's one-band error bound, this failure mode is unbounded — and nobody knows it exists.
  • Extends 005-the-world-is-dualistic: the dualism runs through biology itself — some anatomy is in the Undertow.
  • How an astral corpus arises in a human (birth? Undertow trauma? the Gardener's doing?) is deliberately undefined — plot inventory.

Alternatives rejected

  • Infallible injection — rejected: an airtight institutional control leaves no crack for horror to get in through.
  • Known-to-JDIT rare resistance — rejected: JDIT ignorance is the sharper edge; their confidence in the needle is part of the institutional lie.

Rules basis

  • House cosmology; no KB mechanic. Liminon dualism: 005-the-world-is-dualistic.

How the players can learn it

They almost certainly shouldn't, until the case file that proves it.

Canon decision 025

Canon 025: Corruption is openness

Date: 2026-08-24 Status: Accepted

Context

The Corruption design pass, detection branch: who — and what — can see Corruption (018-corruption-is-alienation)?

Decision

Corruption is openness. The more corrupted a psi, the more sensitive they become to Undertow signals — and, almost always, the more they emit on the same wavelength. Reception and emission are coupled — the antenna reciprocity of the coherence-gated emission model (033-psi-rides-a-modified-mitochondrion).

Instruments: the openness assay

("Openness" is GM-side shorthand; the in-world clinical term is the LER — liminon-evoked response (GLOSSARY), and drift from baseline is "LER drift.")

JDIT measures openness with fMRI coupled to a liminon-emitting device — a hearing test with a different signal: emit, watch which brain areas light up, grade the sensitivity. (In theory you could just ask the psi "did you feel that?" — in practice JDIT and ANSCOM do not trust their agents enough to run it on self-report.) This is the concrete procedure behind canon 012's "liminon-signature baselining."

JDIT knows openness can indicate growing corruption — especially marked increases. But the LER is an indicator, never a diagnosis. The corruption signal is a marked change in profile — especially toward certain wavelengths — and it is confounded: growing skill as a telepath or astral projector also yields better LER results. A drifting profile is therefore one data point among several (psych evals, physicals, time in service) that ANSCOM weighs when deciding between mandatory leave (022-alienation-can-recede) and permanent decommissioning (023-retirement-is-a-needle). A naturally sensitive psi and a corrupting one look identical in absolute terms (019-loudness-sets-a-floor — loudness is baseline openness); etiology never shows; totals stay GM-secret per 55-horror. The diagnostic fog of 012-power-estranges survives measurement.

Perception is embodied and idiosyncratic

Each psi feels the Undertow's influence differently, set by where their liminon-sensitive neurons concentrate: one smells emotions, another sees them, another knows them abstractly. Powers should usually feel embodied — presentation rule for narrating psi at the table.

Living psis

Almost never perceivable. Very sensitive, very skilled telepaths or astral projectors MIGHT read a specific receiver as unusually open to signals that feel wrong — rare and unformalized: the felt read is not institutional knowledge anywhere in JDIT. (Instrumental wavelength detection is another matter — see below.)

The Undertow side

The emission is how the Gardener senses the corrupted: its seeds are very powerful sensors spread around the world (002-psi-is-the-gardeners-cultivation). It reads wavelength alignment easily; for a human psi the same read takes extraordinary capability.

What JDIT knows on this channel: certain broadcasts echo through the Undertow; it suspects some of them are malign; and it can sometimes detect a psi emitting on those wavelengths — unreliably, and more easily the more powerful and less controlled the psi. This instrumental capability is as far as institutional knowledge goes.

Some very rare psis only receive — corrupted, tuned to it, and leaving no Undertow footprint. The emission channel cannot find them; their entire risk surface is behavioral. These are the Enemy's natural sleeper agents (The Blight) — not a coordinated cell (the Gardener cannot yet run one, 015-the-gardener-is-primal): its will simply becomes theirs, unconsciously, and their actions converge without any of them knowing the others exist.

Consequences at the table

  • Monitoring scenes get a physical procedure: the tube, the emitter, the tech reading the bloom on the scan. An operative hiding growth (012-power-estranges) is now hiding it from a machine.
  • The Blight's sleepers are not caught, they are churned: an erratic sleeper gets removed as a misbehaver or an overexposure-syndrome case, never identified as enemy influence. The Gardener needs no roster — it made them, and its broadcast is one-way. From outside, convergence is indistinguishable from conspiracy.
  • Deep-corruption veterans are walking sensor arrays: they hear more than clean psis, which makes the corrupted genuinely better at parts of the job. The blessing again (015-the-gardener-is-primal).
  • Embodied qualia give every psi NPC a sensory signature worth one line in their note.

Alternatives rejected

  • Corruption invisible to instruments — rejected: monitoring needs teeth for the paranoia to bite.
  • A true corruption-meter — rejected: cited against the Open Threads ruling that the formal register's confidence is an institutional lie; a real meter would make JDIT actually able to tell.

Rules basis

  • Aura-visibility precedent adapted: 55-horror §Effects of Corruption. Reaction penalties from the RAW aura rule are not used; perception is per the rules above.

How the players can learn it

Their own monitoring sessions; a veteran's "something's wrong about him" that turns out true; the case where a screen came back clean and shouldn't have.

Canon decision 026

Canon 026: The end of the line is a seed

Date: 2026-08-24 Status: Accepted (threshold number deferred to calibration)

Context

The Corruption design pass, terminal branch: what full corruption is (55-horror's 125-point forced conversion), converging with the crossed-threshold-operatives thread and canon 016's substrate rule.

Decision

A point of no return exists; its number does not yet — pinned at calibration with the rest of the provisional values.

The terminal trajectory: the person goes catatonic for a time, then slowly begins to mutate. If the body retains enough energy, the mutations pull it toward a seed — a fleshy growth of tumorous appearance. The Gardener's seed network is self-propagating: its terminal victims become its new sensing organs (025-corruption-is-openness).

Era-gated conservation: when the Gardener grows sentient — even sapient (016-its-mind-grows-with-its-thralls) — it becomes able to conserve taken bodies un-mutated and run them as discreet agents. Today it cannot; its finished works are lumps, not people. (GM-side inference, not ruled: this may be what "vessel" readiness means — the GLOSSARY's vessel goal requires the conservation capability, so the current era's terminal cases are all failures by the Gardener's own standard.)

What JDIT knows: advanced Undertow openness sometimes triggers physical mutations — cancer-like at first, then taking on a subtly different signature. They know the mutations are the end of the line. If they decide on mercy, the decommissioning injection (023-retirement-is-a-needle) is usually a death sentence at this stage: severed from the Undertow's proto-regulation, the body crumbles under the weight of its abnormal growths. Jargon (ruled 2026-08-25): the clinical term is liminic neoplasia; there is deliberately no field word (Liminic neoplasia).

Consequences at the table

  • Every terminal case ANSCOM fails to contain is future enemy infrastructure — the case queue's stakes quietly compound.
  • Body-collection doctrine (014-anscom-salvages-its-dead) acquires a second, unintended meaning: ANSCOM is either interrupting seed-formation or concentrating it in a vault — feeding the standing amplifier-convergence ambiguity (Open Threads). Do not resolve casually.
  • The overexposure-syndrome clinical picture gets its final stage: rage or eerie calm, then silence, then growth. A hospice ward ANSCOM doesn't put in writing.
  • "Mercy" is now a genuinely grey verb: the needle at this stage kills, and everyone administering it knows.
  • Late-era discreet agents are a second infiltration class alongside The Blight's receive-only corrupted — one the campaign should meet only after the players have learned the old rules.

Alternatives rejected

  • Hard RAW 125-point line now — deferred, not rejected: players who count conversions can reverse-engineer a known number; the calibration pass decides where and how visible the line is.
  • Terminal state as death or simple NPC-villainy — rejected: the setting's horror is transformation with a function; the enemy wastes nothing.

Rules basis

  • Forced-conversion precedent: 55-horror §Effects of Corruption (125+). Everything else is house canon.

How the players can learn it

A containment that arrives too late and finds the growth; the medical annex where the merciful injections happen; an old seed that, examined closely, has dental work.

Canon decision 027

Canon 027: Everyone is broadcasting

Date: 2026-08-24 Status: Accepted

Context

Adopting the PSC "world broadcasts loudly" insert (54-psionic-campaigns §Telepaths and mental screens, the on-the-world variant) after the Corruption pass settled the wavelength model (025-corruption-is-openness).

Decision

Every civilian broadcasts their thoughts, all the time — more intensely the stronger the emotion. Mechanically: Easy to Read (Psychic Only, +0%) is part of the standard human template; assume every NPC has it unless bought off. It counts against no disadvantage limit, per the PSC guidance.

The buy-off is ANSCOM training, granted free. Part of the training ANSCOM gives prospective agents is learning to shut off the noise of the crowds — and non-psi ANSCOM personnel get the same training: in an agency whose enemies include telepaths, being unreadable is basic opsec. PCs have the buy-off at no point cost. Civilians at large are stuck with the trait — they don't know there is anything to train.

Structurally, ANSCOM's mental-discipline curriculum is one thing in fiction and two Unusual Backgrounds on the sheet:

  • UB (psi-combat conditioning) [5] — unlocks both mental maneuvers (All-Out Concentrate, All-Out Defense (Mental Defense)).
  • UB (trained silence) [5] — permission only: it allows buying off Easy to Read (Psychic Only) [-10]; it is not the buy-off itself.

The PC package: every PC gets both [5] UBs free, plus the [10] to buy back Easy to Read. A PC may legally refuse the buyback and spend those 10 points elsewhere; in-fiction the agent is ridiculed for the lack of discipline. NPCs do not follow PC point-total rules. The UBs are a class, not an ANSCOM monopoly — rival psi-agency training grants either under other names, and an analyst can hold trained silence without the combat UB, so the desk/field seam falls out mechanically.

Screens slip. Keeping the screen up is an acquired habit, not a switch: temporary degradation of cognitive capacity — especially prefrontal function, from any source (exhaustion, alcohol, sedation, concussion, terror) — lowers the screen's effectiveness or drops it entirely. Some very experienced people have the habit so ingrained it cannot drop.

Wavelength separation. Human minds broadcast on a much "shorter wavelength" (metaphorically) than the deep-Undertow signals that openness tunes to. Consequences:

  • Corruption does not amplify crowd noise — the corrupted are more sensitive to signals from deeper in the Undertow, not to the surface chatter of minds.
  • This is why mundane minds are dim to the Gardener (015-the-gardener-is-primal): it listens on the deep band, and the crowd's waking noise never carries there. Receivers are loud where it lives (019-loudness-sets-a-floor); waking civilians are loud only in the room.

Sleep is the exception, by phase. Dreams touch the Undertow — the dream sea is the Undertow (009-the-undertow-is-peopled, 028-the-closed-list) — and the sleep state changes the coupling (033-psi-rides-a-modified-mitochondrion). In slow-wave sleep, the brain's global synchrony phases the baseline machinery and the sleeper's emission carries deeper: sleeping civilians are marginally louder toward the Depths, and so slightly more visible to the Gardener than waking ones. In REM, reception sharpens instead: sleepers are more susceptible to emissions from the Depths — which is where the Gardener's broadcast originates; its whispering (015-the-gardener-is-primal) lands here. Flavor, no mechanical rider.

The relief is part of the hook. Recruits whose telepathy manifested before recruitment lived immersed in the murmur; ANSCOM's training is the first silence of their adult lives. The org knows this — the gift of quiet helps it sell itself as genuinely concerned with its operatives' well-being, and the gratitude is a loyalty instrument it doesn't have to fake.

Consequences at the table

  • Untrained and rogue telepaths are people drowning: crowds are torment, riots and panics are deafening. Sympathy and horror in the same case file — and a texture for why self-taught receivers (013-the-berger-scale's crutch class) are strange before they are dangerous.
  • Emotion scales volume: a telepath reads a poker table poorly and a panicking mob overwhelmingly. Crowd scenes have a psychic weather report.
  • Trained silence is itself a signature (ruled): to a psi who knows what to listen for, an ANSCOM-trained mind in a civilian crowd is a hole in the noise — and very experienced rogue psis know it, using the silence as a tell to spot and avoid ANSCOM and its agents. The hunters' opsec is the prey's tripwire.
  • A slipped screen is a security breach: whatever degrades cognition makes an operative readable again — and an adversary who knows that can arrange the degradation. Hospitals, bars, and interrogation rooms are all soft points.
  • Session-0 texture for the pre-mades: every PC remembers the day the noise stopped.

Alternatives rejected

  • On-the-telepath implementation (Reflexive +40% / Uncontrollable -10% passive reception, PSC's other variant) — declined as the world-rule; may still model untrained NPC telepaths' misery mechanically when needed.
  • Corruption-scaled crowd noise (cities unbearable for the deeply corrupted) — rejected: wrong wavelength; openness points down, not around.

Rules basis

  • Easy to Read + Psychic Only (+0%), template placement, buy-off and no-limit guidance: 54-psionic-campaigns §Telepaths and mental screens (PSC p.27).
  • Free buy-off via ANSCOM training (including mundane personnel) is a house rule.

How the players can learn it

They lived it: the training montage, the first quiet crowd. The other side arrives with the first rogue telepath who never got the gift — or the first time a target's panic gets loud enough to hurt.

Canon decision 030

Canon 030: The impossible tier is real

Date: 2026-08-24 Status: Accepted

Context

The PSC p.11 options sitting (2026-08-24-options) reached item #12: PP's "uber-techniques" — Permanent Effect, Lethal Attack, and Omnicast, each defaulting at psi skill -30 and written for cinematic campaigns expecting skill 25–30 (53-psionic-powers §Cinematic psis and uber-techniques). A 150+50-point lethal procedural never reaches that tier, so "off" looked free — except the campaign had already minted one such technique (the anti-corruption Mental Surgery variant at ~-30, "godlike-feat territory", 2026-08-24-ability-curation §Telepathy), and the setting already contains performance beyond human reach (020-the-door-opens-from-inside).

The -30 technique tier exists in the world, and no unaided human reaches it. Baseline psis — PCs included, at any realistic skill — cannot buy or attempt uber-techniques. But power that does not come from the psi alone can get there: door-opened performance (020-the-door-opens-from-inside), borrowed power, and stolen power (the Psychic Vampirism adjudication note, 2026-08-24-ability-curation). A Gardener-fed thrall or deeply tuned receiver may exhibit uber-technique effects on screen.

The anti-corruption Mental Surgery technique (~-30 default, any failure treated as critical) is the first member of this class, not a one-off house number.

Held in reserve: Omnicast beats Anti-Psi. RAW, Omnicast affects subjects with Immunity to Psi and Psi Static — meaning something at this tier can reach through the elimination squad's screens. That beat is spent deliberately or not at all.

Consequences at the table

  • The tier is a legibility horizon: JDIT's models of what psi can do are calibrated on human performance. An uber-technique effect on a case is evidence of the door, not of skill — the players can learn to read it that way (013-the-berger-scale's assay worldview breaks down exactly here).
  • Permanent Effect gives the Gardener's side a signature atrocity: edits that never heal, curses with no recovery roll.
  • Anti-Psi personnel are safe from everything except the things this canon exists to describe — the elimination squad's confidence is well-founded right up until it isn't.
  • ANSCOM cleanup doctrine does not depend on this tier: RAW Mindwipe permanence (an hour at -4) covers wipes.
  • Depends on / props up: 020-the-door-opens-from-inside, 022-alienation-can-recede (the Mental Surgery ruling), 028-the-closed-list (the tier is outside the list's economy).

Alternatives rejected

  • Off entirely — rejected: it would orphan the Mental Surgery ~-30 ruling and discard a clean mechanical expression of what door-opened power looks like on screen.
  • On as written (available to anyone with the skill) — rejected: flatly misfits the campaign's power level and lethality (001-table-agreements).

Rules basis

  • Uber-technique write-ups: 53-psionic-powers §Cinematic psis and uber-techniques (PP p.76).
  • Restricting the tier to door-opened/borrowed/stolen power is a house rule; the KB offers only "extremely optional" as guidance.

How the players can learn it

The hard way: a case where the damage does not heal, the wipe does not fade, or the anti-psi escort goes down mid-sentence. Formal disclosure never happens — JDIT does not have this tier in its manuals.

Canon decision 031

Canon 031: Psi runs hot

Date: 2026-08-28 Status: Accepted

Context

Expansion of the LAM's biology (005-the-world-is-dualistic), opened while settling the decommissioning drug's pharmacology (023-retirement-is-a-needle). Two questions the setting had never answered: what using a power feels like from the inside, and what it costs the body.

Decision

It feels good

Liminically active neurons are strongly dopaminergic. Using a power is therefore pleasurable, and mildly addictive — a low-level pull, always there, in every receiver.

Power use also strains the body, and the body answers with endorphins. Continuous use especially. Psis effectively get a runner's high off working, on top of the dopaminergic reward.

It costs

A psi's metabolism is far more calorie-hungry than an ordinary person's — roughly double, as a floor. That is the resting cost of carrying working, docked LAMs at all: the complex has to be docked to draw it, so baseline humanity — and a carrier whose nuclear gate is shut — pays nothing and eats like anyone else (033-psi-rides-a-modified-mitochondrion). Above the floor the figure rises with power use and with power potency, and heavy psi use carries massive caloric costs.

The cells meet that demand through a pathway no other neuron has. Cells running docked complexes pull and consume far more energy from the blood than ordinary neurons can, and their ATP cycles run much faster and more efficiently. This special endocrine pathway is what makes the organelle affordable at all — and it is exactly the route the decommissioning drug uses to reach these neurons and no others (023-retirement-is-a-needle). The demand sits where metabolism happens: the LAM is the neuron's own modified mitochondrion, paying for every act of organization in membrane potential and running hot to compensate (033-psi-rides-a-modified-mitochondrion).

This pathway is not a mystery to anyone cleared to know about it. NII-19 worked it out and JDIT has it; the endocrine side of the LAM is settled science. The unsolved problem is elsewhere and always has been: how the LAM controls the liminon — the physics-to-biology join (005-the-world-is-dualistic, 033-psi-rides-a-modified-mitochondrion).

Ordinary methamphetamine already bites. Repeated use degrades psi capability, slowly. This is what pointed NII-19 at the drug class in the first place, and it is true of street supply as much as of anything a laboratory makes. The degradation is mitochondrial death, and it erases the molecular evidence as it goes (033-psi-rides-a-modified-mitochondrion).

Mechanically

Increased Consumption (23-b1-disadvantages, [-10 points/level]; level 1 = six meals a day, i.e. double). Level 1 is the floor and applies to receivers as such.

Consequences at the table

  • Every receiver has a standing reason to reach for a power, separate from any tactical one. The pull toward use is now biological, and it sits underneath the Corruption economy rather than replacing it (018-corruption-is-alienation, 025-corruption-is-openness).
  • Psis eat constantly and conspicuously. Logistics, cover, and field endurance all acquire a mundane texture that is hard to hide.
  • A receiver who uses street methamphetamine is dimming themselves by degrees. The pleasure of power use and the common drug that erodes it sit on the same biology.
  • Every agent is a tragic character by the nature of their powers alone, before any personal trouble they bring with them; those pile on top. There is no good way out (023-retirement-is-a-needle, 012-power-estranges).

Alternatives rejected

Rules basis

  • Increased Consumption (P) [-10 points/level]: 23-b1-disadvantages — level 1 = 6 meals/day, level 2 = 12, doubling per level. B1 p.140.
  • The floor's application to receivers, and any scaling above it, are house rules.

How the players can learn it

Immediately and from the inside: it is their own biology.

Canon decision 033

Canon 033: Psi rides a modified mitochondrion

Date: 2026-08-31; revised 2026-09-02 Status: Accepted

Context

The organelle had been a distinct novel neural structure since 005-the-world-is-dualistic — which meant any electron micrograph of psi neural tissue since the 1960s could have exposed it. The GM judged that too easy to spot, and the recast opened the whole mechanism question: what the organelle is, how it is inherited, how its genes are read, and how emission can square with real physics. The 2026-09-02 sitting extended the recast: set against the universal thought-broadcast (027-everyone-is-broadcasting) and the feeling-shaped Undertow (005-the-world-is-dualistic), an endowment confined to psi lineages left ordinary minds with no emitter at all — and the GM ruled that the way normal people project their thoughts must not be handwaved.

Decision

The organelle

The seat of psi is a modified mitochondrial subtype — formally the liminically active mitochondrion, "the LAM" in JDIT's own shorthand. Morphologically it is an ordinary mitochondrion — nothing to see under any microscope. Beside the standard, fully ordinary human mitochondrial genome it carries one or more copies of a small, separate, circular DNA element — the plasmid (GM-doc name; its in-world formal name is unminted, Open Threads) — whose gene sequences match no known life: the Gardener's engineering signature (006-the-gardener-fell-in-deep-time).

The plasmid brings no machinery of its own. Its genes sit behind mimics of the human mitochondrial promoter sequences and are transcribed, translated, and replicated entirely by the host mitochondrion's ordinary equipment. Two consequences:

  • The genes are necessarily written in the mitochondrial genetic code, which differs from the nuclear code — so any fragment that migrated to the nucleus would be untranslatable there. The trait cannot escape the organelle, and stray alien reads in sequencing data look exactly like the routine artifacts every real pipeline discards.
  • The whole anomaly reduces to one small extra DNA circle and the handful of proteins it encodes. Everything else is standard-issue human mitochondrion.

The proteins assemble into a novel complex built on the photoreceptor template: an alien cofactor clamped in the protein scaffold does the actual liminon coupling (the plasmid also encodes its synthesis enzymes); the protein never touches the physics, it only tunes the cofactor and transduces its state changes.

Universal carriage, graded coupling

Every human nervous system carries the plasmid. It is the Gardener's base engineering, fixed in the species — the breeding program predates Homo sapiens (006-the-gardener-fell-in-deep-time). What separates a receiver from the rest of humanity is not carriage but what the complex docks to:

  • Baseline humanity — the undocked complex. The baseline plasmid encodes a complex that assembles but does not dock onto the oxidative-phosphorylation chain: it floats free of the proton-motive force, scraping by on ambient ATP. Underpowered, leaky, untuned — enough for a feeble short-band murmur (the thought-broadcast telepaths read, the feelings that shape the Undertow, the sleep-window drift), nowhere near enough for reception as perception or for directed work.
  • Psi lineages — the docked variant. The Gardener's cultivation (002-psi-is-the-gardeners-cultivation) is not handing out the plasmid — everyone has that. It is upgrading it in selected matrilines: enhanced plasmid variants whose complex carries the docking module, seating it on the respiratory chain as effectively a sixth respiratory complex tapping the proton-motive force. Full power, deep-band tuning, real gain.
  • The cliff is bandwidth, not a switch. Baseline coupling is feeble but two-way — subthreshold in both directions. Docked coupling is perceptual and operative. Nobody is at zero.

A neuron rich in docked, working LAMs is a Tarusov–Zemtsova neuron — the anomalous cell type the Soviets identified c. 1970. NII-19 also traced the anomaly to the mitochondria themselves — Tarusov's pre-recruitment work was mitochondria-adjacent, and he looked where he knew — naming the organelle the bioplasmic mitochondrion without ever finding what made it different (Discovery of the Psi Neuron). Baseline machinery is far below what that era's instruments — or a routine modern assay — can see. The assay's "count" is read on neural tissue: in a receiver, enhanced carriage and docked function coincide, so the number serves; whether the assay physically reads carriage or docked function — and so what it reads on a carrier whose gate is shut, or on baseline humanity — is unruled (see Inheritance, below, and Open Threads).

Death erases the evidence (ruled 2026-08-31). When the modified mitochondria running docked complexes die — the needle's overstimulation, or chronic stimulant erosion — the cell's own disposal machinery clears them and digests the enhanced plasmid with them. Capability degradation is therefore also evidence destruction: a needled brain reads like anyone else's (023-retirement-is-a-needle), and a PRUNE target sanitizes slowly as the addiction grinds (ANSCOM).

Transduction — how activation becomes information

  • Reception, organelle to cell: a liminon event perturbs the cofactor → conformational change → cascade amplification (the rod-cell precedent: biology reliably reports single-particle events this way). The result is handed to the neuron through the two standard mitochondrial currencies: Ca²⁺ microdomains at synapse-adjacent mitochondria (the fast, millisecond path — modulates release probability and excitability) and ATP/metabolic state read by metabolically gated membrane channels (the slow tonic path — the pancreatic-β-cell precedent).
  • Docking is the information cliff as well as the energy cliff. The two currencies are the respiratory chain's own outputs. A docked complex sits in the middle of the machinery that generates them and writes into them at full bandwidth; an undocked baseline complex's cofactor still twitches when liminons arrive, but its state changes barely reach the currencies — trace Ca²⁺ leakage, orders of magnitude below perception, but nonzero. One mechanism prices both the power gap and the perception gap.
  • Cell to mind: no new organ. Liminon events bias release probability and excitability across the neuron population — a patterned perturbation riding on ongoing activity. The real-world precedent is sensory substitution: cortex learns to read any input channel that systematically modulates firing. Undertow input is experienced as percept-without-a-sense-organ — impressions, imagery, "knowing" — and it must be learned: early contact is noise, feeling, and wrongness, and only training (or the self-taught crutches of 013-the-berger-scale) resolves it into content.
  • Why telepathy decodes at all: the civilian murmur is structured by the sender's own firing patterns (see Emission), and sender and receiver both run human neural architecture — a shared codebook. Deep-band signals are horror for the same reason: the Gardener and the Undertow's things perturb the same machinery, but the human brain has no codebook for them. The input lands as dread, compulsion, dream-imagery, alien mood — canon 015's "whispering to the subconscious" is deep-band input biasing limbic and affective circuits without ever reaching declarative decoding.
  • Emission: the same machinery driven in reverse — in a docked complex, off the proton-motive force at terrible gain (the calorie economy of 031-psi-runs-hot lives here; the anomalous oxidative chemistry also leaks the ultraweak photon signature that got the cell type discovered, Discovery of the Psi Neuron). In any firing neuron, membrane-potential swings and Ca²⁺ transients jostle the cofactor's state — so emission mirrors the firing pattern, which is why the murmur carries readable thought rather than static. Emotion scales volume twice over: arousal feeds the machinery more energy, and emotional states synchronize firing across the brain — and superradiance rewards phasing. Baseline emitters get their phasing from neural oscillation — sleep shifts the regime: slow-wave synchrony carries emission deeper, REM sharpens reception (027-everyone-is-broadcasting); psis add proton-motive-force power and trained, deliberate organization — directed, deep-band, high-gain.
  • Two routes into behavior. The perceptual route — decoded content — requires docked complexes: receivers only. The subthreshold route — mood, intuition, alienation drift, the blessing's sharpened instincts (015-the-gardener-is-primal) — runs in everyone, scaled by coupling. Normals live entirely on the second route and call it feelings. Residue cases touching civilians (004-ancient-cultivation-modern-bloom, 009-the-undertow-is-peopled), hot ground working on whoever stands in it (021-the-roads-in), and the sleep window all run on this route.
  • Discrimination ("wavelength"): the complex's sensitivity peak is set by the protein microenvironment around the cofactor — spectral tuning, exactly as cone opsins tune one shared chromophore. Variant-tuned LAM populations compared against each other give wavelength discrimination, grounding the tuning/resonance vocabulary and canon 025's detection channel.

Inheritance: matrilineal transmission, biparental expression

Mitochondria are maternally inherited, so plasmid variants ride the maternal line only. Everyone inherits a plasmid from their mother; what the cultivated lines transmit is the enhanced variant — a father transmits none of it, and biologically a lineage is a matriline, the Gardener's cultivation having always worked through mothers (002-psi-is-the-gardeners-cultivation). The pattern was first noticed empirically by the Berger programme — "the gift follows the mother" (The Berger Programme).

Carrying the enhanced plasmid is not sufficient (ruled 2026-09-02). The docked complex needs its assembly factor to fold the subunits into the inner membrane and seat the cofactor on the chain, and that factor is nuclear-encoded. It is not an engineered gene: it is a rare allele of an ordinary human mitochondrial assembly-factor gene — carried by people with no enhanced plasmid and no lineage, doing an unremarkable job. Inherit the permissive allele and the enhanced complex assembles and docks; inherit only the common form and the enhanced plasmid sits inert at baseline. The baseline form self-assembles with common machinery — the gate prices the docked complex only. Ordinary variation across the rest of the nuclear mitochondrial-housekeeping machinery still modulates how well a docked complex runs: the fine dial above the gate.

The gate comes from both parents. Transmission stays matrilineal; manifestation is biparental, and half the gate is the only genetic stake a father has in a receiver.

Silent carriers therefore exist — people who hold the enhanced plasmid and will never manifest, coupling at baseline like everyone else; the women among them pass the variant on undiminished. A matriline can run dark for generations and surface when a marriage brings the allele back in.

Heteroplasmy is tissue-restricted: plasmid-bearing mitochondria — baseline and enhanced alike — segregate into neural lineages during development and are near-absent elsewhere. Blood, saliva, and consumer genomics are structurally blind for the whole species; finding the plasmid takes brain tissue and knowing what to look for.

The emission axiom — the setting's one piece of invented physics

Real physics forbids a fixed-coupling particle that is both undetectable and biologically emittable (emission and absorption share one coefficient; biology runs at electron-volt energies). The setting's single invented axiom: the liminon's coupling is coherence-gated — near-zero for matter in ordinary states, real only for phase-coherent arrays. "Organized by psionic operation" (005-the-world-is-dualistic) means phase coherence, literally. Only the cofactor couples at all — silicon clocks, lasers, and every other coherent artifact couple to nothing — so computers and machines stay outside the physics entirely (028-the-closed-list). Downstream, real physics supplies the theorems:

  • Superradiance (N² scaling): one cofactor emits nothing measurable; billions phased across a brain emit macroscopically. Baseline arrays reach only the loose, transient phasing that neural oscillation provides — the murmur; docked arrays under deliberate organization emit at incomparable gain. Grounds loudness scaling with the assay count, gestalt pooling and its shared crit-fail, and the amplifier's concentrated-emitter-mass logic (014-anscom-salvages-its-dead).
  • Antenna reciprocity: an array phased to emit is identically phased to receive. Canons 019-loudness-sets-a-floor and 025-corruption-is-openness collapse from two decrees into one consequence — the loudness floor on Corruption is reciprocity.
  • Warm coherence at body temperature is what the cofactor is engineered to protect (real, contested precedents exist: radical-pair magnetoreception, photosynthetic light harvesting). Alien engineering doing hard engineering, not breaking law.
  • Masquerade lockout: the liminon files under real physics' "hidden sector" vocabulary, and coherence-gating means no accelerator, cavity, or observatory can produce or detect one. The only instrument that couples to liminons is a nervous system — which is why the discovery took a coincidence experiment (Discovery of the Liminon) and why public physics will never find it.

What stays unsolved, in-world and out

JDIT can (eventually) solve the complex's structure atom by atom and still not explain the coupling. The standing wall is: how does the LAM control the liminon? The coherence-gating axiom itself is GM-side; no one in-world has it.

Consequences at the table

  • The masquerade is structurally sound: no autopsy, no micrograph, no blood test, no biobank ever stumbles on the secret. Exposure risk concentrates where it is dramatic — brain tissue in the wrong lab, with the wrong question. ANSCOM's body-collection doctrine doubles as biosecurity accordingly (014-anscom-salvages-its-dead, the death-watch in ANSCOM), and both instruments that end psi careers — the needle at once, PRUNE's chemistry slowly — leave a brain that reads like anyone else's.
  • Genealogy sharpens and then blurs: case-mapping follows matrilines (002-psi-is-the-gardeners-cultivation) and a psi father passes no enhanced plasmid — but the nuclear gate means a carrier line can produce nothing for generations and then a receiver, so a clean maternal chart predicts badly. Berger's breeding registries, JDIT's lineage files, and the players' own genealogy work all inherit both halves.
  • The enhanced-carrier population is necessarily larger than the receiver count, and nobody has sized it (Open Threads) — every exposure estimate and lineage map rests on a number not yet run. Beneath it sits a buried bombshell: with the right molecular probe and brain tissue, everyone tests positive for the plasmid. Whether JDIT knows the carriage is universal is open (Open Threads).
  • The murmur (027-everyone-is-broadcasting), residue cases felt by civilians, hot ground working on mundanes, and the sleep window all run on baseline coupling — mechanics touching them should derive from the bandwidth cliff, not add new decrees.
  • The Tarusov–Zemtsova neuron's real-world profile (dopaminergic, mitochondria-dense, Ca²⁺-coupled, meth-vulnerable) is a recognized cell-biology phenotype — the needle's pharmacology (023-retirement-is-a-needle) and street meth's slow erosion (031-psi-runs-hot) run on real mitochondrial neurotoxicity.
  • Loudness, gestalts, the amplifier, and the LER all rest on one physics; new mechanics in that space should derive from the axiom, not add new decrees.

Alternatives rejected

  • A distinct novel organelle (the pre-recast model) — rejected: an EM-visible structure is one unlucky autopsy from exposure, and its concealment for eight decades strained belief.
  • Endowment-only carriage (the 2026-08-31 form — the plasmid confined to selected matrilines, most of humanity carrying nothing) — rejected 2026-09-02: it left ordinary minds with no emitter, contradicting the universal thought-broadcast (027-everyone-is-broadcasting), the feeling-shaped Undertow (005-the-world-is-dualistic), and hot ground working on mundanes. The GM ruled that the way normal people project their thoughts is not to be handwaved — scientific plausibility is the horror engine.
  • Extramitochondrial complexes in baseline humans (the same complexes seated elsewhere in the cell, starved of energy) — rejected: the plasmid's proteins are translated in the mitochondrial code inside the organelle and cannot exist elsewhere, and a nuclear-encoded copy would sit in every cell and be findable in a blood tube. The energy-starvation intent survives as the undocked state.
  • Alien genes inserted into the main mitochondrial genome — rejected for the separate plasmid: the main circle stays fully ordinary, so haplogroup analysis reads a normal human matriline, and the plasmid shape has real precedent (plant and fungal mitochondrial plasmids).
  • Body-wide heteroplasmy — rejected for tissue restriction: in the sequencing era, body-wide alien mtDNA makes the genomics masquerade a permanent five-alarm problem rather than a contained one.
  • Single-factor matrilineality (the enhanced plasmid alone decides, with ordinary nuclear variation as a soft dial) — rejected: a soft dial yields no silent carriers and no skipped generations, so carrier lines could not go dark, and a novel multi-subunit complex docking with no dedicated assembly factor is the least realistic thing in the model.
  • An engineered alien gene in the nucleus — rejected for the ordinary-allele form: a novel reading frame sitting in nuclear DNA is present in every nucleated cell and findable in a blood tube, which is precisely what the plasmid's neuron-locked confinement exists to prevent.

Rules basis

  • House cosmology throughout — no KB equivalent, flagged per vault convention. The GURPS-facing surfaces (assay, Berger bands, Corruption floor, needle mechanics) are unchanged by this record.

How the players can learn it

The biology, through CANOPY science briefings (the LAM, the assay, the LER); the matrilineal rule, through casework genealogy; the nuclear gate, only where the genealogy refuses to behave; universal baseline carriage, the plasmid, and the coherence axiom, only through late-campaign deep files or the Gardener itself.

Canon decision 034

Canon 034: The liminon came late

Date: 2026-08-31 Status: Accepted

Context

Three open threads converged: the JDIT science department (unnamed, unlocated, unled), the undated discovery of the liminon, and a standing tension — canon 005 said JDIT's research discovered the particle and that the discovery prompted the organelle's naming, while several notes compressed that naming into the 1987 standardization, which would require a just-founded department to produce the unifying discovery in year zero.

Decision

The department

JDIT's research arm was founded in 1987 as the Applied Biophysics Division — staffed by the second-rank Soviet scientists of the transfer, built on NII-19's basic corpus, and named while the bioplasmic energy-field theory was still the reigning frame ("biophysics" was both the Soviet cover-discipline heirloom and the standing theory's name). It was renamed the Liminic Sciences Division c. 1994–95, after its own discovery of the liminon (Discovery of the Liminon). Leadership and location remain open (Open Threads).

The mostly-American early leadership held suppression of Soviet-inherited terms and institutionality as a minor standing goal. It won on institutional labels — the division's rename scrubbed "biophysics" from the door — and lost in the deep lexicon: the physics, biology, and clinical vocabulary stayed Soviet because the theory was Soviet, and you cannot rename a discipline you cannot rebuild (GLOSSARY stratigraphy).

The discovery and the cascade

  • c. 1993–94: the division detects the liminon in a coincidence experiment — a working psi under first-generation neural imaging (fMRI, whole-head SQUID magnetometry) beside a detector in an ultra-low-background underground chamber. No recovered Soviet hardware was required: the Soviet advances were biology-side; the mechanism of mind–matter interaction was their holy grail and they never found it. Detail in Discovery of the Liminon.
  • c. 1994–95: the renaming cascade. The limin- vocabulary is coined from the particle; JDIT's molecular work cracks open the organelle the Soviets had localized decades earlier — the plasmid, the complex, the mechanism — and the cascade mints the adjectival formal terms: liminically active mitochondria and neurons, LAM and LAN in the documentation (033-psi-rides-a-modified-mitochondrion, Liminically active mitochondria); the Soviet names (bioplasmic neuron and mitochondrion, the Tarusov–Zemtsova eponyms) are formally retired, surviving informally; the division renames itself. The suppression goal rode the cascade; it did not cause the science.

The dating, untangled

The 1987 standardization covers what a treaty can standardize: B/C notation welded from the two parents, the merged lexicon, and the death of the bioplasmic index (013-the-berger-scale). The limin- coinages are not 1987: they date to the c. 1994–95 cascade, because the particle that unified the theory had to be found first. The discovery is the division's first monument; the cyberpsi implant (2000s) remains its second and its purely-in-house one (Founding of the JDIT).

Consequences at the table

  • The department's culture is legible in its two names: veterans of the ABD years who still say "biophysics" date themselves the way "the index" dates old Soviet hands — one more stratum in the erosion archaeology (GLOSSARY).
  • The division earned its own name once, thirty years ago, and has been handed nothing since c. 2007 (Founding of the JDIT) — the institutional memory of one triumph is part of what it is covering for now.
  • Every liminon-era instrument (detectors, the LER emitter) descends from the discovery method, and the method needs a psi in the loop — JDIT's science has a human in its foundations, and someone sat in that chamber.

Alternatives rejected

  • Liminon discovered at/before the 1987 founding — rejected: a just-born department producing the unifying discovery in year zero strained belief, and it left the department with no earned identity.
  • Discovery enabled by recovered Soviet prototypes — rejected by ruling: the Soviets' strength was biology; making the department's founding achievement recovery-fed would have contradicted their never-found holy grail and cheapened the division's one triumph.
  • "Applied Biophysics Division" kept to the present — rejected: the GM wanted the department's name to cohere with the limin- vocabulary, and the rename gives the suppression policy a visible institutional act.

Rules basis

  • Pure setting canon; no KB equivalent, flagged per vault convention.

How the players can learn it

The division's current name, in any CANOPY science briefing; the old name and the cascade, from veterans and old file headers; the coincidence experiment and who sat in the chamber, above their starting clearance.
