Cosmology (Stage 1)

Load-bearing truth recorded in 002-psi-is-the-gardeners-cultivation; vocabulary in GLOSSARY.

  • The premise (what is true here): human psionics is real and alien in origin — The Gardener, a Lovecraftian entity cast to Earth in deep time, engineered the liminosome into hominid lineages and cultivates receivers as vessels for its kind (006-the-gardener-fell-in-deep-time). TL8 world; the TL9 edge is JDIT's classified psi-tech.
  • Metaphysics: dualistic reality — material + astral plane (feeling-shaped, occasionally seeping through). Interface particle: liminon; neural organelle: liminosome. Psi is scientific but researched empirically, not theoretically. Body is fair game — advanced receivers manifest physical changes (005-the-world-is-dualistic).
  • Deep history: cultivation visible across millennia — real oracles/witches/ saints; folk beliefs are garbled truth; residue (haunted places, relics) is astral seepage. 20th-century critical mass (004-ancient-cultivation-modern-bloom).
  • The entity's mind: the Gardener is primal — half-awake, its cultivation instinctual fitness-amplification (the "blessing": strong receivers get sharper instincts, magnetism, luck), its attention a flicker; integration into it is an ongoing colonization, not a future harvest (015-the-gardener-is-primal). Its mind grows with its thralls: nearly every corrupted psi adds cognition; its current register is anger — corrupted individuals trend violent — and the long arc runs primal flicker → cunning → (unchecked) open mastery, with the rage cooling as it grows (016-its-mind-grows-with-its-thralls).
  • Official story vs. truth: public — psi does not exist; JDIT is a do-nothing defense initiative. Inside CANOPY — psi is real, cause unknown, "signal" theory (wrong). Truth — cultivation by seeds (002-psi-is-the-gardeners-cultivation).
  • Distribution/topology: psi is not location-bound — awakened receivers work anywhere. Aptitude is strongly inborn; the Gardener runs artificial selection on lineages via its seeds (breeding instruments), so receivers grow stronger each generation. Incidents follow lineages, not places (decided in S000-worldbuilding-stage-1).
  • Common beliefs (and which are wrong): awareness Suspected — ~1 in 10 believes; charlatans everywhere, some JDIT-sponsored; folk beliefs are garbled lineage-memory (accidentally right). Reaction if truth broke: fascinated dread — 007-sixty-thousand-receivers-suspected-world.
  • Frequency: Uncommon, ~60,000 receivers worldwide, rising since the 20th-century bloom.
  • The Undertow (astral plane): see 005-the-world-is-dualistic and GLOSSARY.
  • Potential: inborn (lineages) + one secret exception — an undefined fringe actor holds a crude, dangerous induction method (future arc seed).
  • Forbidden practice & enforcers: JDIT hunts any organization that gets big enough to notice (003-jdit-keeps-the-field-clear); (open: what happens to individual civilian receivers ANSCOM finds?)
  • Power's cost — Corruption (high potency estranges; JDIT monitors its operatives closely, 012-power-estranges; mechanized 2026-08-24 in canons 018-corruption-is-alienation through 026-the-end-of-the-line-is-a-seed; all numbers provisional — see the Open Threads calibration chore):
  • Corruption measures alienation from the human world, and alienation is openness to the Undertow. The Gardener is the loudest broadcaster, so the corrupted usually tune to it — usual, not definitional; "whose signal?" stays a live question. Chassis: GURPS Horror Corruption near-RAW, converting into a curated, era-weighted disadvantage table that drains from hot violence toward cold calculation as the entity cools (018-corruption-is-alienation).
  • Loudness sets a floor: minimum Corruption = 5 × max(manifest Berger band, highest Undertow-linked Talent) — uncleansable, so every receiver carries at least 5. Never-manifested high-Talent latents roll against a real floor: civilians going quietly mad is a canonical case-genesis JDIT misfiles (019-loudness-sets-a-floor).
  • The door opens from inside: any psi can voluntarily open to the Undertow — skill boosts and above-ceiling level-borrowing, priced in dice of Corruption; every receiver instinctively knows how. Crit-fails on psi use accrue Corruption by default. JDIT flags repeated above-assay performance as a going-bad tell (020-the-door-opens-from-inside).
  • The roads in (involuntary): hot zones (per week), the Undertow itself (a day = a week), vile encounters, cursed artifacts — the body-salvage amplifier corrupts its operators. Wounds and ordinary investigation are clean (021-the-roads-in).
  • Alienation can recede — above the floor only, through months of deliberate mundane humanity; any psi use resets the cycle ("retire or rot"). Exotic Undertow mercies exist, unkeyed — deliberate ambiguity (022-alienation-can-recede).
  • Retirement is a needle: the mandatory decommissioning injection kills the liminosome — 1d6 IQ and 1d6-3 DX gone, all psi lost, converted disadvantages remain ("you're this, now"). Recruits know the policy, not the price (023-retirement-is-a-needle). It fails on the very rare astral corpus (astral-side liminosomes), a manifestation mode JDIT does not know exists (024-the-astral-corpus).
  • Corruption is openness: reception and emission couple. JDIT monitors LER drift (fMRI + liminon emitter) — an indicator, never a diagnosis; a growing telepath and a corrupting one look alike. The Gardener reads the same emission through its seed network; rare receive-only psis leave no footprint — its natural sleepers (025-corruption-is-openness, The Blight).
  • The end of the line is a seed: past the (uncalibrated) point of no return — catatonia, mutation, and, if the body keeps enough energy, a new seed. The network is self-propagating; the needle at this stage usually kills, and everyone administering it knows (026-the-end-of-the-line-is-a-seed).
  • Countermeasures: anti-psi humans far rarer than receivers; working psychotronics live in JDIT/ANSCOM inventory, Cold War leftover stock, and independents' jury-rigs.
  • Crowd noise: every human broadcasts thoughts psychically, louder with emotion — Easy to Read (Psychic Only) on the standard template; ANSCOM trains all its personnel silent, free — and the first quiet is a loyalty instrument. Screens slip when cognition degrades; trained silence is itself a hole in the noise that experienced rogues read as a tell. Human chatter rides a short wavelength: corruption tunes the deep band, so the corrupted hear no more of the crowd — and the crowd never reaches the Gardener (027-everyone-is-broadcasting).
  • Mechanics frame (provisional budgets): PP as written, curated closed list; "150-pt heroes + 50 psi"; Talent uncapped at creation but locked after start; UB (Psi) free; precog = likely × hazy; restraint = Duty + Secret; key-facts method — see S000-worldbuilding-mechanics. Closed list ruled 2026-08-24: eleven powers, splits/folds/vetoes and Talent gates per 028-the-closed-list; full Undertow-linkage taxonomy per 029-the-undertow-linkage-table (linked: Telepathy, Astral Projection, ESP, Probability Alteration, Psychic Vampirism, Teleportation).
  • The Berger scale: per-power manifest/ceiling ratings; telepathy-first is universal biology; crutches carry the Psionic Crutch [-5] stigma — 013-the-berger-scale.

Canon glossary

Signal to Noise — canon glossary

Modern-world secret-psionics campaign. The language splits in two registers: ANSCOM's clipped signals-bureaucracy jargon (what gets said aloud), and the GM-side truth vocabulary (what the notes mean). Keep them apart.

Every term below is a heading, so uses elsewhere in the vault can link straight to the definition: [[GLOSSARY#Receiver|receiver]].

This file is the sole registry of sanctioned vocabulary. A word or phrase without an entry here is just random word choice by whoever wrote that note — even if bolded, quoted, or repeated, and even in a numbered canon record. Accord it no special treatment: never infer terminology from it, never propagate it as jargon, and never add an entry except at the GM's explicit direction.

Register pattern (ruled 2026-08-24, two-register sitting): the in-world vocabulary splits into a formal/technical register and a field register, related by age-keyed erosion — field forms arise by corruption of formal terms or by native replacement, and older terms are more worn. Every formal term carries two coordinates, tradition × minting era, from the canonical stratigraphy: Occult inheritance (pre-program, –1940s) · Berger-German (1930s–40s loanwords; provisional, adopted on trial) · US program (Cold War, mystical flavor) · Soviet program (Cold War, dry-descriptive) · JDIT bureaucratese (post-1987). Provenance is domain-keyed (ruled 2026-08-25): the US program was results-focused — practical application in warfare and espionage — so it yielded the ops, tradecraft, and doctrine vocabulary; the Soviet program was scientific, after the whys and hows, so it yielded the physics, biology, and clinical vocabulary; compartments and administration are JDIT's. A term's subject matter predicts its stratum — pick tradition by domain when coining. The field register is not a stratum — it is the living second register eroding all of them; its coinages date by when agents started chewing on a term. The lexicon is deliberately a fossil bed: origin × wear locates every term in the program's history.

Language ruling (2026-08-24): terms and documentation stay English; the game is played in Portuguese, so every term must also pass a table-speech check — a settled Portuguese spoken form. As of 2026-08-24 every existing term is tentative pending the full two-register pass (coordinates + Portuguese check); entries are confirmed one by one.

Program and clearance

JDIT

The Joint Directorate for Irregular Threats — the international organization in charge of psionic phenomena, sold to the public as a boring, do-nothing minor defense initiative. See JDIT. Coordinates: post-'87 acronym, formal register; the field personifies it as "Judith" — cover-speech friendly ("Judith wants the report" passes in a café). PT (table speech): "Judite" — "a Judite tá cobrando"; the spelled acronym lives on paper only. Dependency: if JDIT's acronym ever changes, Judith changes with it. Confirmed 2026-08-25 (two-register pass). Avoid: "the Agency", "the Bureau"

ANSCOM

Anomalous Signals Command — JDIT's operations arm; the PCs' employer. See ANSCOM. Coordinates: post-'87 acronym, spoken as a word. In-house nickname: "the House" / "a Casa" — warm, insular, sinister in hindsight ("a Casa sempre recolhe os seus" — the House always collects its own; canon 014's body doctrine hiding in a proverb). PT (table speech): "Ânscom"; "a Casa". Confirmed 2026-08-25 (two-register pass). Avoid: "the program" (in dialogue; fine as GM shorthand)

CANOPY

The compartment designation for the psionics secret — a container for information, never a name for any organization. "Read into CANOPY" = knows psi is real; all JDIT employees are CANOPY-read. Written in caps, always. Avoid: "the secret", "clearance" alone, and especially using CANOPY as if it were the org (the org is JDIT; its ops arm is ANSCOM) Coordinates: post-'87 JDIT compartment codeword, kept English everywhere (NATO-style, caps on paper). PT (table speech): "Canópi"; the read-in idiom is the formal "credenciado" — "ela é credenciada?" No slang layer, by ruling: clearance talk stays stiff even in field mouths. Confirmed 2026-08-25 (two-register pass). Series ruling (2026-08-25): JDIT compartment codewords draw from a themed arboreal series — CANOPY, DEADWOOD, and any future compartments must fit the scheme. (Real tradecraft forbids indicative codewords; the half-indicative themed series is JDIT's institutional vanity — and a standing GM wink the org cannot see.)

DEADWOOD

Compartment designation for ANSCOM's secret internal investigation and elimination squad — the unit that brings down dangerous or rogue agents (ANSCOM, canon 028). Most of JDIT does not know the compartment or the squad exists. Written in caps, always. Where the field knows of it at all — as rumor — it whispers "the Janitors." Coordinates: post-'87 JDIT compartment codeword, arboreal series (see CANOPY): dead wood is what a healthy forest sheds — and corporate idiom for personnel to be cut. The whisper extends "a Casa": every house has its cleaners. PT (table speech): codeword kept English; whisper "os Faxineiros" — "faxina" already carries the cleanup-detail sense. Confirmed 2026-08-25 (two-register pass). Avoid: "internal affairs", "the hit squad"

The Baltic Incident

The 1987 event in the Baltic that forced the founding of JDIT (008-the-baltic-incident-founded-jdit). See Baltic Hotspot. Main name confirmed 2026-08-25 (un-glossary'd sweep). Alternative names — official euphemisms, field nicknames — to be coined later for flavor (queued, Open Threads).

Field jargon (ANSCOM register)

Decommissioning

The mandatory exit injection — the only retirement ANSCOM allows (023-retirement-is-a-needle). Disclosed at signing as a routine, classified, safe-sounding medical procedure; the price is not. Coordinates: formal = decommissioning — post-'87 JDIT bureaucratese, equipment language for people. Field: "getting dimmed" — lifted from the org's own assets-too-valuable-to-dim idiom. PT (table speech): formal "descomissionamento" — the oil-rig/power-plant word; field "ser apagado" — "apagar" doubles as BR slang for killing, and the field word refuses to distinguish. Confirmed 2026-08-25 (two-register pass). Field synonym (sanctioned 2026-08-25): "the needle" — the injection standing for the whole exit. PT: "a agulha" (provisional table-speech form). Avoid: retirement (bare — in-world, retirement IS this)

Receiver

ANSCOM's term for a psionically active person. Reflects the (incorrect) working theory that psis pick up an external signal. Coordinates: both registers (standard vocabulary) — Soviet-program stratum: minted mid-century as "priëmnik" ("receiver") in the weak era, when psis could only pick up stray thoughts; translated into English at the '87 merger. Old and semi-worn — the bloom has since made psis emitters too, so the word is quietly outdated as well as wrong: datable evidence for attentive players. PT (table speech): receptor. Confirmed 2026-08-25 (two-register pass). Avoid: psychic, esper, mutant

Noise

Field term for any psionic incident, from a bent spoon to a burning precinct — the unit of ANSCOM casework. "We've got a noise in Graz." Strictly field: no document says "noise." Coordinates: field register — native replacement (signals imagery: something audible where there should be silence); bloom-era ANSCOM, young. Formal parents, a three-layer stack: liminic event (CANOPY-compartmented — coined from the liminon; internal memos) and anomalous event (bland any-clearance bureaucratese — external-facing documents, plus some internal use, ANSCOM more than other JDIT divisions). PT (table speech): field "um barulho" — "deu barulho em Graz"; formal "evento limínico" / "evento anômalo". Confirmed 2026-08-25 (two-register pass). Avoid: manifestation, haunting, outbreak, and "noise event" — the hybrid does not exist in-world (GM prose swept 2026-08-25, sitting notes included)

Hot

Field slang: a site is "hot" / "running hot" when it reads elevated liminic flux — or when it is corrupted; the field cannot tell and does not care (formal splits, field lumps). A corrupted site is a "hot zone" in agent speech — and by ruling (2026-08-25) "hot" serves all cases at all levels until an official JDIT technical term is coined (queued, Open Threads); the report behind it says liminic flux, elevated. Coordinates: field register — native replacement, Geiger-counter culture riding the Soviet instrument lineage; bloom-era. PT (table speech): "quente" — "o lugar tá quente", "área quente". Confirmed 2026-08-25 (two-register pass). Avoid: haunted, cursed (witnesses' words, not agents'); Bad Place (struck 2026-08-25 — GURPS-RAW citations only)

The hum

Field lingo for the ambient civilian thought-broadcast — the psychic murmur of a crowd (027-everyone-is-broadcasting): "the hum in Shinjuku is unbearable." Alternate: "the babble." Deep-corruption veterans hear it worst. Sanctioned 2026-08-25 (GM triage). The formal-register term is still queued (Open Threads). PT (table speech): "o zumbido"; alternate "o burburinho" (provisional forms, pending table check).

Going loud

Informal term for a receiver — or a whole group — risking CANOPY reveal, voluntarily or involuntarily: public manifestation, a case boiling toward witnesses, an agent losing control. "Manage receivers before they go loud" (ANSCOM). Sanctioned 2026-08-25 (GM triage), informal register. Distinct from loudness (019-loudness-sets-a-floor) — Undertow audibility; same imagery, different channel. PT (table speech): "estourar" (provisional form, pending table check).

Classified science (CANOPY-compartmented vocabulary)

Liminon

The subatomic particle existing simultaneously in the material and astral planes; the interface between them. Neutrino-like and near-non-interactive except when organized by psionic operation. Discovered by JDIT's research effort. Coordinates: formal register only — post-'87 JDIT science wing, coined in the Soviet-descended clinical idiom. The particle name has no field erosion ("limmies" struck 2026-08-25 — agents never handle the noun). Field contact is via readings: formal liminic flux ("elevated flux at the site"); the field names the result, not the particle — see Hot. PT (table speech): "limínon" (penult stress, elétron pattern); "fluxo limínico". Confirmed 2026-08-25 (two-register pass). Avoid: psion, psi particle, "the carrier"

Berger scale

JDIT's rating for receivers: manifest power over ceiling (potential), per power — "a Berger 3, ceiling 5 telepath" — with an overall figure in dossier headlines. Written B3/C5, spoken "a three over five." Named for Hans Berger. Assays estimate ceiling imperfectly. Coordinates: formal register, used by both — Soviet-program stratum (science domain): the Soviets built the scale from Berger's inherited late work; the US program ran a cruder rival rating that lost at the '87 standardization — a dead fossil term old US-descended hands still slip into (unkeyed; coin only when an NPC needs it). Field erosion: spoken "a three over five"; bare "a six" for B6. PT (table speech): "escala Berger"; spoken "um três sobre cinco"; ceiling = "teto" ("um telepata Berger 3, teto 5"); field "um seis". Confirmed 2026-08-25 (two-register pass). Avoid: psi level, power level (bare)

Assay

The measurement procedure behind a Berger rating — how JDIT estimates a receiver's ceiling (013-the-berger-scale). Assays are imperfect: a measured ceiling can be a band off (standing ambiguity). Provisional (2026-08-25): serviceable, but a more technical formal term is wanted — queued (Open Threads). Coordinates and PT deferred until the real term is coined.

Crutch

A verbal and/or gestural routine (mudra, mantra, sketched sign) a receiver uses to make manifestation deliberate and repeatable. Reliable, and stigmatized — ANSCOM trains agents to push past them, not always successfully. Coordinates: "crutch" is field register — native imagery, minted in bloom-era ANSCOM training culture (the academy sneer, canon 013). Formal parent: induction routine — Soviet-program stratum, the Cold War programs' bloodless clinical term for the phenomenon, inherited at the merger; the field sneer exists precisely because the formal word refuses to judge. PT (table speech): field "muleta" (native Brazilian idiom, stigma intact); formal "rotina de indução". Confirmed 2026-08-25 (two-register pass). Avoid: focus, ritual (those mean other things)

Liminosome

The minor neural organelle that emits liminons as a side effect of its operation. High concentration of liminosome-bearing neurons = psionic aptitude. Discovered; inner workings poorly understood. Coordinates: formal register only. Known to the Soviet bio-psi program pre-'87 under a dead Russian name (unkeyed — surfaces in old files; adjacent to 011-the-substance-came-from-a-liquidator); renamed liminosome post-'87 when the liminon unified the theory. No field form of the organelle name — the field touches it only through the assay number, the "count" (count ≈ ceiling assay, canon 013), the boast idiom of the ambitious. PT (table speech): "liminossomo" (cromossomo pattern); the count boast = "grau" — "grau alto, teto seis". Confirmed 2026-08-25 (two-register pass). Avoid: psi gland, "the mutation"

LER

Liminon-evoked response — JDIT's clinical measure of a receiver's sensitivity to Undertow signals: a liminon-emitting device fires a stimulus and fMRI grades which brain areas respond — a hearing test with a different signal. Baselined for every operative; LER drift (marked increase from baseline) is treated as a corruption indicator. Post-'87 JDIT coinage. GM-side truth: LER is measuring openness, i.e. Corruption (025-corruption-is-openness). Coordinates: formal register — post-'87 JDIT bureaucratese, science domain (Soviet-descended idiom). Field slang, bloom-era: the exam is "the hearing test" — deadpan, from the procedure's own metaphor; LER drift is "going deaf" — deaf to the real world, so a drifting agent is "failing the hearing test." The inversion is the two-register gap at its sharpest: the clinic records rising sensitivity, the field sees a colleague tuning out of the world — both are true (018-corruption-is-alienation). Canon decision 001

Canon 001: Table agreements

Date: 2026-08-22 Status: Accepted Revealed to players: yes — agreed at session zero; restated in the session-0 brief.

Context

Campaign genesis for Signal to Noise, a modern-world (TL8, edges of TL9) GURPS psionics campaign. The premise — secret-world containment operatives working monster-of-the-week cases while a larger source stirs — can be played anywhere from pulp action to grinding horror, so the table's dials had to be set before any material was written.

Decision

  • Tone: creeping cosmic dread. Cases open mundane and curdle; the Flowering's source bleeds wrongness into everything. Humor happens between operatives, not in the fiction's framing.
  • Lethality: full, rules as written. TL8 firearms and psi both kill; no GM softening of death rolls. Death is always on the table and caution is a survival skill.
  • Lines and veils: none declared. The table is comfortable with dark content on-screen; issues are handled ad hoc if they arise.
  • Cadence and shape: biweekly sessions, open-ended campaign. No planned endpoint; the metaplot escalates at the pace play sets.

Consequences at the table

  • Full lethality plus cosmic dread means PC turnover is expected — the program frame makes replacements natural (a new operative is assigned), and prep should keep that pipeline cheap.
  • Investigation-forward pacing: dread needs room to build, so cases should not be pure combat encounters; combat, when it comes, is decisive and dangerous.
  • Open-ended shape means the source's Flowering must be pace-flexible — escalation beats keyed to play, not to a calendar.

Rules basis

  • Injury, death rolls, and recovery run as written: 04-injury-recovery-hazards.
  • Firearm damage and TL8 equipment from the Basic Set KB: 25-b1-equipment. (Note: GURPS High-Tech is not extracted into the KB; gear beyond Basic Set is currently uncovered.)

Retcon cost

Low for cadence; high for lethality — once a PC has died to the dice, quietly softening the rule retroactively devalues that death. Change it openly at the table if at all.

Canon decision 002

Canon 002: Psi is the Gardener's cultivation

Date: 2026-08-22 Status: Accepted Revealed to players: no — the campaign opens with ANSCOM (and the players) knowing none of this.

Context

Campaign genesis. The premise needs one load-bearing truth: where psi comes from and why incidents are escalating. Without it, monster-of-the-week cases would have no pattern to converge on, and the "source stirs" stakes would be improvised later at retcon cost.

Decision

Human psionics is not native. A vast alien intelligence — GM-side name the Gardener — is dormant but stirring, and psionic-enabled humanity is its goal: it is cultivating humans as vessels for itself and its kind (X-COM Ethereals crossed with a Lovecraftian semi-awakened sleeper — see 006-the-gardener-fell-in-deep-time). Its presence on Earth is multiple seeds — fragments buried or hidden worldwide; the whole is one mind, greater than its parts.

The cultivation is artificial selection on human lineages: psionic aptitude is strongly inborn, and receivers have been getting stronger over generations as the Gardener selects for potency. Seeds are its breeding instruments — within their reach they nudge pairings, dreams, and migrations of carrier lineages. (Refined in 015-the-gardener-is-primal: the "nudging" is instinctual whisper-and-seep fitness amplification, not deliberate matchmaking — selection is emergent, and all the cruder for it.) Psi itself is not location-bound: an awakened receiver works at full strength anywhere. Incidents therefore follow lineages, not places — any geographic clustering is demographic, potent lineages still living near their ancestral seed. Receivers (psis) are the crop coming in.

The PCs' employer knows almost none of this: JDIT/ANSCOM knows psi is real and contains incidents under the CANOPY compartment, but has not discovered the seeds, the Gardener, or the vessel project. The PCs can be the first to see the pattern.

Consequences at the table

  • Every case is secretly a data point: incidents map to lineages, and lineages trace back to ancestral seed grounds. Long-term play rewards players who keep their own files — genealogy is the real map.
  • Rogue receivers are victims of cultivation, not freaks of nature — a tragic undertone under full-lethality containment work.
  • PC psis are themselves part of the crop. What the Gardener wants with strong receivers is a personal threat to the party, not just a global one.
  • The campaign's long question is seeded from day one: the colonization is ongoing, not awaited — can it be seen in time, and can it be stopped?
  • Depends on: 001-table-agreements (tone: creeping cosmic dread).

Alternatives rejected

  • A broadcast/transmission source — rejected as the literal truth, but survives as flavor: ANSCOM's "signals" framing is wrong in an interesting way. (Amended 2026-08-24: the corruption channel is now genuinely a transmission — see the two-layer "Signal" ruling in GLOSSARY §Flagged ambiguities and 025-corruption-is-openness; ability genesis stays cultivation, so this rejection stands where it matters.)
  • Psi use stirs the sleeper (use-coupled waking) — rejected; the Gardener's program advances by its own design, not by PC power use. Keeps players free to use psi without meta-guilt.
  • Program secretly knows / is compromised — rejected for campaign start; ANSCOM's ignorance is real. May emerge later in play, but is not canon now.
  • Geographic clustering (noises pool physically around seeds) — adopted at genesis, retconned same day during worldbuilding Stage 1 (S000-worldbuilding-stage-1): psi is not location-bound; incidents follow lineages. Retcon was free — nothing had reached the table.

Rules basis

  • Psi as a power with source "psionic", per 10-powers — power modifiers, Antipsi countermeasures, and psi-tech (antipsi shielding/serum sidebars) all apply.
  • Powers notes that origin (alien cultivation) and source (psionic) are independent: the powers work the same regardless of how they came to exist (10-powers, "Source vs. origin"). This decision is therefore pure setting canon, no house rules needed.
  • Not covered by the KB: GURPS Psionic Powers and Psi-Tech are not extracted (listed but unextracted in 00-library); detailed psi ability catalogs will need extraction or table rulings.

How the players can learn it

Case files that keep featuring the same family names; genealogy work revealing selected lineages and their ancestral grounds; a seed physically uncovered during a containment; a strong receiver's dreams during a noise event; eventually, contact.

Retcon cost

This is the campaign's spine. After the first seed is found on-screen, reversing the Gardener's existence breaks every case that pointed at it. Safe to adjust before then; near-impossible after.

Canon decision 005

Canon 005: The world is dualistic

Date: 2026-08-22 Status: Accepted Revealed to players: no — though PC receivers read into CANOPY will know the sanctioned-science parts.

Context

PSC's defining axes (54-psionic-campaigns §1.3) forced the question of what psi physically is. The answer turned out to be the setting's metaphysics, so it is recorded as one load-bearing decision.

Decision

Reality is dualistic: the material plane and the astral plane — called the Undertow in ANSCOM field slang, the setting's standard term (GLOSSARY) — coexist. The Undertow is shaped by people's feelings, and it occasionally affects — or seeps into — the material world.

The interface between the planes is the liminon: a subatomic particle existing simultaneously in both. Normally it is neutrino-like, near-non-interactive; only when organized by psionic operation does it become interactive with matter. Because of this non-interactivity, human liminon research is biological and empirical, not theoretical physics.

Some human neurons carry a minor organelle — the liminosome — that emits liminons as a side effect of its operation. A high concentration of liminosome-bearing neurons is what makes a person psionic. This is the mechanism behind inborn, lineage-bred aptitude (002-psi-is-the-gardeners-cultivation).

On PSC's axes: psi is scientific (detectable, measurable, ultimately blockable) and not strictly mental — the body is fair game. Advanced receivers can manifest physical changes; body horror is a signature of late-stage cultivation.

JDIT's research effort has discovered both the liminon and the liminosome, though the organelle's inner workings remain poorly understood. Receiver screening is possible; detection and early psi-tech exist. This is where the setting's TL9 edge lives: JDIT's classified, CANOPY-compartmented instruments, not the public economy.

Residue (004-ancient-cultivation-modern-bloom) is astral seepage: haunted places and relic objects are feeling-soaked astral structures bleeding through.

Consequences at the table

  • ANSCOM casework has real forensics: liminon detectors, liminosome assays, screening programs. "Is this person a receiver" is answerable with a lab.
  • The empirical-not-theoretical stance keeps mystery alive despite the scientific framing — JDIT can measure psi without understanding it.
  • Astral scenes are playable space (projection, seepage sites, echo dives), and the astral's feeling-shaped nature ties horror tone to mechanics.
  • Body-horror manifestations telegraph advanced cultivation — a visual escalation ladder for the metaplot.
  • Psychotronics (anti-psi tech) is physically possible; its availability is a separate dial (PSC questionnaire E28, still open).

Alternatives rejected

  • Strict mental line (levitation yes, wings no) — rejected; vessels are bodies, so cultivation must be able to reshape them.
  • Mystical/unmeasurable psi — rejected; a half-blind tech-heavy program is a good story, but the chosen empirical-but-shallow science is better: measurement without comprehension.
  • Monist world (no astral) — rejected with the "psi + residue" choice in 004-ancient-cultivation-modern-bloom; the astral gives residue and projection a home.

Rules basis

  • Axes: 54-psionic-campaigns §1.3; astral-plane canvas §2.5; countermeasures/psychotronics §3.3.
  • Astral projection and psi ability mechanics: pending the Psionic Powers extraction (53-psionic-powers, in progress).
  • House cosmology (liminon/liminosome) is setting canon with no KB equivalent — flagged per vault convention.

How the players can learn it

The CANOPY read-in briefing covers the sanctioned science (liminon, screening, detectors). The astral's true nature, seepage, and the body-horror ceiling are field discoveries.

Retcon cost

Extreme after play begins — this is the physics every case's forensics will lean on. Amend only before session 1.

Canon decision 006

Canon 006: The Gardener fell in deep time

Date: 2026-08-22 Status: Accepted Revealed to players: no — this is the campaign's deepest secret.

Context

Deciding the liminosome's origin (005-the-world-is-dualistic) forced the question of what the Gardener actually is and when its program began.

Decision

The Gardener is a Lovecraftian entity — alien metaphorically and literally — cast to Earth in the prehistoric past, millions of years ago. Its mind is semi-awakened, Cthulhu-like: not scheming in real time but pressing on the world through deep, slow intent — intent that is real but erratic, unfocused, primal (015-the-gardener-is-primal).

Since arriving it has been subtly and quietly shaping the world and human history. It engineered the liminosome into hominid lineages — the breeding program predates Homo sapiens. The seeds are its instruments; the selected lineages its crop; vessel-capable receivers its goal (002-psi-is-the-gardeners-cultivation).

Consequences at the table

  • "Cast to Earth" implies an elsewhere it came from — and possibly others of its kind, wherever they remain. Left deliberately unresolved.
  • Human history is quietly authored: any historical turning point (Stage 3 work) may bear its fingerprints. Use sparingly — the horror is that you can never be sure which events were its.
  • A semi-awakened mind cannot be negotiated with or meaningfully fought at TL8/9 — campaigns against it are about its instruments, crop, and timetable, never the entity itself.
  • The 20th-century bloom (004-ancient-cultivation-modern-bloom) reads as the program entering its final phase — or the sleeper's sleep growing shallow. Both may be true.

Alternatives rejected

  • Natural organelle, alien exploitation — rejected; psi is truly not native, preserving 002-psi-is-the-gardeners-cultivation as written.
  • Seed-stuff infection (lineages part-Gardener at cellular level) — rejected as the mechanism, though the engineered liminosome keeps most of its flavor without making every receiver literally alien tissue.
  • Millennia-scale program — superseded: 004-ancient-cultivation-modern-bloom's "seeds on Earth for millennia" was written before this decision; deep time (millions of years) is now canon and 004 has been amended to match. Human lineage cultivation is still the millennia-scale, historically visible layer.

How the players can learn it

Geological context of an excavated seed (impossibly old strata); the Gardener's fingerprints converging across historical research; direct astral contact with the semi-awakened mind — a late-campaign event.

Low until a seed's age or the entity itself reaches the table; the spine of 002-psi-is-the-gardeners-cultivation does not depend on the timescale, but the horror register does.

Canon decision 007

Canon 007: Sixty thousand receivers, a suspecting world

Date: 2026-08-22 Status: Accepted Revealed to players: partial — operatives know the caseload reality; exact global figures are compartmented.

Context

PSC questionnaire C15/C17 (54-psionic-campaigns §2.2–2.3): how many psis, and what the public knows. The two must be set together — frequency drives how much secrecy is even possible.

Decision

Receiver frequency is Uncommon: about 1 per 100,000 — roughly 60,000 worldwide, up to a hundred in the largest cities, at least one in every significant nation. The 20th-century bloom (004-ancient-cultivation-modern-bloom) means this number is rising.

Public awareness is Suspected: the world officially agrees psi does not exist — no conclusive proof, no clean media capture — but roughly 1 in 10 people wholeheartedly believes, and a quarter of those say so openly. Charlatans abound, some quietly sponsored by JDIT specifically to discredit the idea. Receivers need to keep what they are secret.

Public reaction, if the truth broke (PSC §2.4): fascinated dread. Not uniform persecution — real fear laced with celebrity worship, cults, and recruitment frenzies. Exposure outcomes are unpredictable, which is precisely what JDIT dreads: chaos, not pogroms. An outed receiver faces a lottery — mob, media circus, cult adoption, or a foreign recruiter's van, sometimes all in one week.

Consequences at the table

  • PSC warns that Uncommon frequency naturally degrades secrecy toward "suspected" and defeats all-encompassing factions — which is exactly the world as chosen: JDIT's monopoly (003-jdit-keeps-the-field-clear) is policy, not omniscience. Free receivers, believer networks, and small underground groups exist everywhere ANSCOM isn't looking.
  • The disbelief shield: most people write off psi effects as coincidence, so discreet receivers get away with a great deal — but a true believer may recognize the real thing (PSC §2.3's "compensating advantage").
  • Word-of-mouth makes receivers organize for self-protection — the minor organizations JDIT mows down are a permanent crop, not a finite list.
  • PC receivers carry a Secret; its point value is set at character creation via PSC §2.4's costing procedure (PSC questionnaire C20, with B10–14) — fascinated dread means the "what you gain if outed" list is volatile rather than uniformly negative, which argues for a modest Secret value.
  • Debunker NPCs (Quester / Skeptic / Gainsayer, PSC §2.3) are a standing cast resource; some are JDIT assets.

Alternatives rejected

  • Rare (~2,000) — cleaner for the monopoly, but too sparse for monster-of-the-week casework density and the bloom's visible pressure.
  • Unknown awareness — at 60k receivers PSC prices this as a massive conspiracy under terminal strain; the table preferred a world already murmuring.
  • Denied awareness — one rung too loud: half-open operation by receivers would undercut the containment premise.

Rules basis

  • Frequency tiers and consequences: 54-psionic-campaigns §2.2.
  • Awareness states, charlatan ecology, Secret requirement: 54-psionic-campaigns §2.3.

Retcon cost

Rises fast: caseload density, NPC believer ratios, and JDIT's info-ops posture all encode these numbers from session 1.

Canon decision 008

Canon 008: The Baltic Incident founded JDIT

Date: 2026-08-22 Status: Accepted Revealed to players: no — this sits in JDIT's founding archive, above the PCs' clearance at start.

Context

Stage 3 history work: the turning point that created the current order (003-jdit-keeps-the-field-clear needed an origin). Core questionnaire asks for the event, its three versions, and its physical remains.

Decision

1987, the Baltic. A Soviet shipment of crude psychotronic tech was targeted for sabotage by a US operations team while in transit on the Baltic Sea. Among the cargo was an experimental biological substance, psionically reactive. The raid breached it; the substance leaked and began self-reproducing by consuming marine biological matter. Containment and cleanup succeeded but took months, and was covered up as an oil spill. That area of the Baltic remains a hotspot for weird phenomena to this day (Baltic Hotspot).

The founding. At the Cold War's end the US pushed to terminate the USSR's psionics research and operations. The negotiated compromise: a covert agency under the UN umbrella — where the US holds much greater control and say — which a weakened USSR judged better than an all-out shadow war between the superpowers. That agency is JDIT. The US (and later China) refused the clause forbidding independent national initiatives (003-jdit-keeps-the-field-clear) — the power that caused the Baltic Incident kept its own options open while closing everyone else's.

Three versions of history (core Stage 3):

  • Commoner: a bad tanker spill in the Baltic in '87; fishing was ruined for a couple of seasons.
  • Ruler (national governments, not CANOPY-read): a US sabotage operation against Soviet weapons tech went wrong; classified environmental damage; the diplomatic fallout quietly midwifed a UN defense initiative.
  • Specialist (CANOPY-read): the substance was psionically reactive and self-replicating; cleanup took months and some phenomena never fully stopped. What the substance was and where the Soviets got it — still restricted, still argued about. (The truth: 011-the-substance-came-from-a-liquidator.)

Physical remains: the hotspot zone itself; sealed cleanup archives; retired cleanup personnel (aging, scattered, some talkative); whatever samples survived containment — officially none.

Consequences at the table

  • JDIT's DNA explained: born of superpower guilt and leverage, tilted toward US influence, allergic to biological psi-materials.
  • The hotspot is a standing case generator and the campaign's first canonical weird-site (Baltic Hotspot).
  • Reopened-file arcs have an anchor: 1987 cleanup veterans, missing samples, and the unanswered question of the substance's origin.
  • Deliberately unresolved: where the Soviets obtained the substance — resolved 2026-08-22: secreted by a Chernobyl liquidator, a tumor-ridden latent psi (011-the-substance-came-from-a-liquidator, The Liquidator).

Rules basis

  • Cold War remote-viewing research as the standard modern hook: 54-psionic-campaigns §1.2.
  • Psychotronics as tech: 54-psionic-campaigns §3.3; detailed gadgets pending need from 53-psionic-powers.

How the players can learn it

Clearance climb; a hotspot case that matches 1987 cleanup signatures; a dying cleanup veteran; Soviet-era archives surfacing through the 003-jdit-keeps-the-field-clear parallel-program rumor mill.

Retcon cost

The founding story underpins JDIT's structure and US tilt; the hotspot will be on-screen early. High once either reaches the table.

Canon decision 009

Canon 009: The Undertow is peopled

Date: 2026-08-22 Status: Accepted Revealed to players: no — field lore reaches PCs as unconfirmed stories, same as it reached JDIT.

Context

Stage 3's Victorian turning point ("some séance circles genuinely touched the Undertow, and a few things looked back") forced the question 005-the-world-is-dualistic left silent: does the Undertow have inhabitants?

Decision

The local (Terran) Undertow is mostly populated by thoughtforms: entities congealed from collective human feeling, or echoes of dead people. This is the common case — what most "spirits" and hauntings are.

Alien intelligences sometimes visit. They range from minor and non-sapient to vastly intelligent and wholly alien. Such contacts are exceedingly rare: JDIT holds no first-hand account — only unconfirmed stories collected across decades of research and fieldwork.

The Gardener is very present in the Undertow — as a looming, diffuse presence. It coalesces into something with agency and consciousness only for brief moments (010-the-gardener-thinks-through-receivers).

Structure sharpened 2026-08-24 (ability-curation pass, S000-worldbuilding-ability-curation): the Undertow comprises both astral layers of the PP model — the near field (overlapping the real world; "the Shallows" in field speech, where dreamers drift) and the far field (field name: "the Depths", confirmed 2026-08-25). The far field is associated with abstract concepts and feelings — deeper resonances and echoes of human minds live there, a manifestation of the collective unconscious: shared ideas, myths, and archetypes coalesce into semi-entities. At the far field's "core" sits the Gardener's core — though topography there is non-linear, non-deterministic, and barely comprehensible. Its signal strengthens with depth (025-corruption-is-openness's "deep band" is literal depth).

The Victorian layer (004-ancient-cultivation-modern-bloom era detail): the séance craze was mass amateur dabbling at the Undertow's edge. Mostly fraud — but some circles genuinely touched it, and a few things looked back. Surviving residue: haunted séance sites, and one or two things that never left.

Consequences at the table

  • Ghost cases have a real taxonomy: echo (replaying imprint), thoughtform (feeling-fed, possibly hostile), or — vanishingly rare — a visitor. ANSCOM can misfile all three as "liminic events."
  • Mass emotion is generative: disasters, cults, and panics can breed thoughtforms. The fascinated-dread exposure scenario (007-sixty-thousand-receivers-suspected-world) would seed the Undertow with new entities — a hidden cost of any public revelation.
  • Visitor stories give the GM a sanctioned "wholly alien one-off" slot without breaking the monist psi cosmology.
  • Victorian séance sites are ready-made residue adventure locations, and "the things that never left" are two unkeyed campaign assets.

Alternatives rejected

  • Echoes only / empty astral — rejected; loses thoughtform horror.
  • Full native ecosystem — rejected; the Undertow's population should track humanity's feelings, keeping people the source of most monsters.

Rules basis

  • Astral plane as setting canvas: 54-psionic-campaigns §2.5.
  • Astral projection mechanics: 53-psionic-powers (Astral Projection power). Thoughtform/echo stat-work is house territory — build via 10-powers ability construction when first needed.

How the players can learn it

Residue cases that answer back; a séance-site case with period documents; the field-lore rumor mill; eventually, meeting something that was never human.

Retcon cost

Moderate now; high after the first thoughtform is fought on-screen.

Canon decision 010

Canon 010: The Gardener thinks through receivers

Date: 2026-08-22 Status: Accepted Revealed to players: no — this is the metaplot's engine; discovering it is a campaign-level payoff.

Context

Describing the Gardener's presence in the Undertow (009-the-undertow-is-peopled) surfaced the mechanism behind "semi-awakened" (006-the-gardener-fell-in-deep-time) and gave the breeding program (002-psi-is-the-gardeners-cultivation) its precise purpose.

Decision

In the Undertow the Gardener exists as a looming, diffuse presence. It coalesces into agency and consciousness only for brief moments. The reason is structural: it needs more psis to act as its "neurons" to fully instantiate its cognition. Receivers are not merely future housing — they are the distributed substrate its full mind will run on.

Therefore:

  • "Semi-awakened" is not injury or sleep alone — it is insufficient hardware. The Fall left it without whatever once carried its mind.
  • The breeding program is the slow manufacture of a neural substrate: every new receiver, every potency gain per generation, adds capacity.
  • Integration is already happening: through its Undertow presence the Gardener psionically infects receivers, colonizing cognition gradually — not a harvest event awaited, but an ongoing wiring-in (015-the-gardener-is-primal).
  • The 20th-century bloom (004-ancient-cultivation-modern-bloom) means the substrate is approaching threshold — its moments of coalesced consciousness are growing longer, more frequent, more deliberate.

Consequences at the table

  • The stakes clock has a mechanism: receiver count × potency ≈ how much mind the Gardener can run — refined by 016-its-mind-grows-with-its-thralls: corrupted psis are the active substrate, and sophistication scales with them. ANSCOM containment (and lethality) literally keeps the enemy stupid — without anyone knowing it.
  • Horrific dilemma baked in: every receiver the PCs save, recruit, or are is capacity for the enemy's cognition. The PCs' own existence feeds the threat.
  • Its interventions are episodic by nature — plans executed in brief coalescences, then left running on instinct and seed automation. This explains both deep patience and occasional blunders.
  • Endgame geometry: prevent threshold (cull/limit receivers — monstrous), break substrate linkage (how?), or let it wake and negotiate with a god. All three are live.

Alternatives rejected

  • Vessels as pure housing (bodies to wear, cognition intact elsewhere) — superseded: wearing bodies may still be part of the end state, but cognition-through-receivers is the primary mechanism and the reason the program needs many receivers, not a few perfect ones.

How the players can learn it

Receivers' shared dreams during its coalescence moments; incident timing correlating with receiver-population milestones; a seed's behavior when local receivers die; late-campaign direct contact.

Retcon cost

This is now the deepest load-bearing fact after 002-psi-is-the-gardeners-cultivation. Retconning after any coalescence event is played would orphan the campaign's climax logic.

Psi runs at standard Psionic Powers strength — but growing very powerful changes the receiver: the higher the power, the more alien the person becomes, mentally and even physically.   • Strength dials: 54-psionic-campaigns §3.1.

Canon decision 013

Canon 013: The Berger scale

Date: 2026-08-23 Status: Accepted Revealed to players: partial — the scale and crutch stigma are everyday ANSCOM culture; the GM-side point mapping is invisible to players.

Context

The GM wanted a StarCraft-Ghost / WH40K-style power ladder distinguishing potential (what you could become) from manifest power (what you can do), grounded in the liminosome biology of 005-the-world-is-dualistic and the potential-vs-training spectrum ruled in S000-worldbuilding-mechanics (B12: Talent uncapped).

Decision

The fiction

JDIT rates every receiver on the Berger scale: manifest rating and ceiling, written B3/C7, spoken "a three over seven." The rating is properly per power — "a Berger 3, ceiling 5 telepath" — with an overall figure quoted in dossier headlines. Ceiling is estimated by assay — the percentage of liminosome-bearing neurons — but the correlation is imperfect: more organelle usually means more potential, not always. A measured ceiling can be wrong by a band either way.

Telepathy comes first. Every receiver manifests some telepathic aptitude before anything else: minds are liminon-active matter (liminosome-bearing neurons), so affecting them is cheap. Manifesting kinetic effects means organizing vastly more liminons against their native non-interactivity (005-the-world-is-dualistic) — it is far easier to control someone's mind than to move a coin.

Converting potential into manifest power is training the nervous system to fire in specific patterns — muscle memory for the mind. During training, some receivers develop crutches: verbal and/or gestural routines (mudras, mantras, sketched signs) that make manifestation deliberate and reliably repeatable. ANSCOM trains its agents to push past crutches; it doesn't always succeed, especially with receivers who self-trained before recruitment. Crutch-use carries real stigma inside the psionic community.

The scale is named for Hans Berger — historically the inventor of the EEG, who built it trying to prove telepathy existed. In this setting his late work went further than the journals record; the Cold War programs that inherited it named the scale for him. (Lore to develop: Hans Berger.) (Sharpened 2026-08-25, two-register pass: the Soviet program minted the scale — Berger's late work survived chiefly on the Soviet side; the US program ran a cruder rival rating that lost at the '87 standardization, a fossil old US-descended hands still slip into. Dead term unkeyed — see Berger scale.)

The mechanics (GM-side)

  • Manifest band = total points in psionic abilities only (the advantages themselves). Skills and techniques are excluded — they are finesse, not raw power, and skill points don't map linearly anyway.

  • Ceiling-caps-manifest, Talent-1 minimum, technique-to-buyoff point conversion, and Psionic Crutch [-5] are house rules.

Retcon cost

Low until character creation; effectively fixed once sheets are built and the first dossier quotes a Berger rating.

Canon decision 014

The GM's ethos ruling: JDIT/ANSCOM must not read as a bastion against evil — the organization needs genuinely shady practices. This is the first one made canon.

ANSCOM always collects the bodies of dead operatives. Everyone in the organization knows this. Few know what happens next: if the fallen operative was strong enough and death was recent enough, surviving brain matter is salvaged and integrated into a gestalt of several other such brains.

This grisly assembly is used as a very powerful psi-amplifier for wide-ranging or long-range telepathy work — the canonical example: editing the memories of an entire small town's population. Canon decision 015

Canon 015: The Gardener is primal

Date: 2026-08-24 Status: Accepted Revealed to players: no.

Context

Freeform refinement of the Gardener's cognition and methods (006-the-gardener-fell-in-deep-time, 010-the-gardener-thinks-through-receivers) — how deliberate is the cultivation, really?

Decision

The Gardener is a half-awake, half-asleep alien cognition. Its cultivation is instinctual rather than thought-out: intent exists, but it is erratic and unfocused. It is a very primal entity.

Growing receivers is how it expands cognitively. Through its presence in the Undertow it psionically "infects" psis — integration into its substrate (010-the-gardener-thinks-through-receivers) is not a future harvest event but an ongoing colonization, receiver by receiver.

The cultivation is crude. The Gardener senses psis far more clearly than regular people — receivers are distinctly present in the Undertow, while mundane minds are dim. Its selection mechanism is simply fitness amplification of what it can see: it whispers to sensitive psis' subconscious and seeps into their physiology, making them more alert to danger, more alluring to others — and so, over generations, more reproductively successful. No matchmaking, no design: the potent get blessed, and the blessing compounds.

Consequences at the table

  • The seeds' "nudging" (002-psi-is-the-gardeners-cultivation) is this whispering-and-seeping, not deliberate pairing management — selection is emergent, which is why it took megayears and why the pattern is hard to prove: there is no plan to uncover, only a gradient.
  • Erratic intent explains inconsistent opposition: some cases feel orchestrated, others random — both are true. The Gardener's attention flickers.
  • Being a strong receiver feels good: sharper instincts, magnetism, luck-adjacent survival. The blessing is real, which is the horror — PCs benefit from the thing that is colonizing them.
  • Its clear sensing of receivers vs. dim sensing of mundanes has tactical weight: mundane operatives are near-invisible to it; strong psis are beacons. ANSCOM would invert its own doctrine if it knew.
  • Design direction (NOT yet canon, GM's "maybe"): Corruption (GURPS Horror mechanics, pending design pass) may be thralldom — each point a fraction of the psi's cognition taken over by the Gardener. If adopted, 012-power-estranges's alienation drift and weird-experience breaks become the same colonization through different doors — retroactively explaining why JDIT cannot distinguish them, and giving the power-based Corruption floor its mechanism (the powerful are louder in the Undertow, hence more whispered-at). Resolved 2026-08-24 (018-corruption-is-alienation): thralldom-as-identity declined — Corruption became alienation plus the openness model. The unification survives, one substance through many doors, with the Gardener as the usual, not definitional, occupant.

Alternatives rejected

  • Chess-master Gardener (deliberate breeding plans, orchestrated history) — rejected as the current state: too knowable, too fightable. A gradient cannot be out-plotted. But the rejection is era-scoped: 016-its-mind-grows-with-its-thralls makes the cunning mastermind the explicit endgame trajectory if corruption proceeds.

How the players can learn it

The blessing's statistics (strong receivers are uncannily lucky, pretty, hard to ambush); dream-content convergence among potent psis; the flicker — cases where the opposition's competence varies wildly for no tactical reason.

Retcon cost

Moderate: this sets the campaign's texture of opposition. After a few cases establish the flicker and the blessing, a deliberate-mastermind retcon breaks the pattern players learned to read.

Canon decision 016

Canon 016: Its mind grows with its thralls

Date: 2026-08-24 Status: Accepted Revealed to players: no.

Context

Follow-on to 015-the-gardener-is-primal: is the primal state permanent, and what does the Gardener feel?

Decision

The more the Gardener corrupts, the more "brain power" it gains — and the more aware and sophisticated its actions become. Its primal state (015-the-gardener-is-primal) is a stage, not a nature: given sufficient psis to corrupt, it might become a cunning alien intelligence shaping the world from the shadows — or openly.

Its emotional register evolves with its cognition. Right now it is driven by anger and revenge; as it strengthens it may become colder and more calculating. The anger shows: corrupted individuals tend to become more violent and aggressive than they were before — not always, but normally. The entity's current mood bleeds through its thralls.

Consequences at the table

  • The metaplot's stakes are a dial, not a threshold: almost every corrupted psi raises the opposition's intelligence (softened 2026-08-24 — the rare non-Gardener wavelength is the exception, 018-corruption-is-alienation). Campaign eras can be paced by the Gardener's visible sophistication — instinctive flicker → tactical cunning → strategy → (if unchecked) open mastery.
  • 010-the-gardener-thinks-through-receivers sharpened: corrupted psis are the active substrate. ANSCOM's lethal containment of violent rogues is — unknowingly — enemy lobotomy, which partially explains why the Flowering has taken this long despite the bloom.
  • The violence signature gives Berger-syndrome cases (012-power-estranges, Berger syndrome) a diagnostic texture JDIT misreads: aggression looks like madness but is mood — the entity is angry, and its thralls inherit it. If the anger cools as it grows, late-campaign corrupted will turn eerily calm — a dreadful tell that things are getting worse, not better.
  • Per 009-the-undertow-is-peopled, the Undertow is feeling-shaped: the Gardener's rage is weather there. Undertow travelers may read its emotional state directly — the campaign's strangest intelligence source.
  • GM discipline: run early-campaign opposition angry and crude; let cold competence arrive gradually and unannounced. Players noticing "it's getting smarter" is the intended dread beat.

Alternatives rejected

  • Fixed primal nature — rejected; a static antagonist wastes the bloom's escalation.
  • Cold from the start — rejected; anger-first gives the violence signature and makes the later cooling legible as escalation.

How the players can learn it

The violence pattern across Berger-syndrome case files (rising, then — worse — falling); Undertow weather; the opposition's competence curve across seasons.

Retcon cost

Low now; rises with each case that establishes the violence signature or the competence curve. The emotional-evolution arc is the campaign's long-term pacing spine — treat as fixed once eras begin turning.

Canon decision 017

Context

Drawing the institutional consequences of 015-the-gardener-is-primal and 016-its-mind-grows-with-its-thralls: what does the corruption ecology mean for ANSCOM's mission?

Decision

Three linked facts:

1. ANSCOM's rogue-hunting doctrine is correct on its own terms. The doctrine needs no conspiracy to justify it — the case files keep proving it right. 2. The hunting genuinely slows the Gardener. But ANSCOM's growth feeds the enemy.   • The PCs themselves are inside the bind: getting stronger makes them better hunters and better hosts.

  • None needed — pure setting structure. Mechanical face arrives with the Corruption design pass (Open Threads).

How the players can learn it

The math of their own case history (kills correlate with quiet periods); a corrupted colleague; eventually, the unbearable brief: "our recruitment program is their agriculture."

High — this is the campaign's thesis. Fixed once internal corruption or the supply-line irony reaches the table.

Canon decision 018

Canon 018: Corruption is alienation

Date: 2026-08-24 Status: Accepted Revealed to players: no.

Context

The Corruption design pass (Open Threads §Corruption mechanics), unblocked by the Horror KB extraction (55-horror §Power Corrupts). The candidate meaning on the table was Corruption = thralldom (the GM's "maybe" in 015-the-gardener-is-primal).

Decision

Corruption measures alienation — the degree to which a psyche has become alien to the mundane world and to human experience. It is not inherently Gardener colonization: colonization is one road in, but any door — power growth, Undertow exposure, weird events — deposits the same substance.

The openness model. Alienation opens the mind to the Undertow. Corruption is the opening; what comes through depends on who is broadcasting. The Gardener is by far the strongest and closest broadcaster, so the corrupted usually pick up its proto-mind and tune to its primal desires — usually, but not necessarily. Other presences (009-the-undertow-is-peopled's residents, Visitors) can capture a corrupted mind that strays into their reach.

The tuning evolves with the Gardener (016-its-mind-grows-with-its-thralls): today the corrupted are merely tuned to its erratic primal desires; as it grows more sophisticated, its influence over corrupted psis becomes stronger and more calculated, trending toward direct control.

Mechanical chassis: GURPS Horror Corruption, close to RAW (55-horror §Power Corrupts). Corruption converts into mental disadvantages drawn from a curated, weighted list aligned with the Gardener's ethos: disadvantages matching its current register are more probable (e.g. Bloodlust while it is angry), misaligned ones less. The weights are era-dependent — as the entity's emotional register cools (canon 016), the table drains from hot violence (Bloodlust, Bad Temper, Berserk) toward cold calculation — so the "eerily calm late-campaign corrupted" tell emerges from the dice. List curation, thresholds, and accrual sources: this design pass, continued.

Consequences at the table

  • 012-power-estranges gets its mechanical vehicle: estrangement drift is the Corruption total. One substance through many doors — JDIT's diagnostic blindness is preserved because there is genuinely nothing to split.
  • The blessing's trap is now a loop: the Gardener's whispering is a source of Corruption, and Corruption is what makes a mind exploitable. Contact corrupts; corruption opens.
  • A corrupted psi is not automatically an enemy asset — "whose signal is he picking up?" is a live question per case, and the rare non-Gardener wavelength is premium plot inventory.
  • The Gardener's flickering attention (015-the-gardener-is-primal) has a mechanical face: the tuning waxes and wanes with its focus, not with the victim's total alone.

Alternatives rejected

  • Corruption = thralldom identity — rejected: makes every high-Corruption psi Gardener-aligned by definition, wastes the Undertow's other broadcasters, and closes the "whose signal?" question.
  • Split substances (power-floor Corruption as colonization, weird-experience Corruption as mere damage) — rejected: mechanically distinguishable etiologies contradict 012-power-estranges's load-bearing rule that JDIT cannot tell them apart.

Rules basis

  • Corruption framework: 55-horror §Power Corrupts; disadvantage conversion: §Effects of Corruption; GM-secret totals: §Many Roads to Hell.
  • The curated weighted disadvantage table and era-shifting weights are house rules layered on the RAW conversion.

How the players can learn it

The pattern in Berger-syndrome case files; a corrupted contact who is manifestly not running the Gardener's errands; the day a PC's own disadvantage roll lands and the GM describes what it felt like to receive.

Retcon cost

Low today — nothing player-facing exists. Rises sharply once the first corrupted case is played or PC sheets carry the mechanics.

Canon decision 019

Canon 019: Loudness sets a floor

Date: 2026-08-24 Status: Accepted Revealed to players: no.

Context

The Corruption design pass, continuing from 018-corruption-is-alienation. The Open Threads ledger's original question: a minimum Corruption floor set by power level, as the mechanical vehicle for 012-power-estranges.

Decision

Every psi has a Corruption floor — a minimum total that cannot be bought off or cleansed below. The rationale is the openness model: power and potential make a mind loud in the Undertow, and the loud are whispered-at whether or not they ever act. Alienation is the standing cost of being audible.

Potential counts — but only Undertow-linked potential. Talent in powers that work through the Undertow (Telepathy, Astral Projection, ESP; full taxonomy pending the ability-curation pass) contributes to loudness even if never manifested. Talent in powers that merely organize liminons locally (Psychokinesis — Pyrokinesis and kin are abilities within PK, not separate powers) does not — an unmanifested kinetic is silent; an unmanifested telepath is already an open ear.

Formula (TEMPORARY calibration, revisit before session 0):

> Floor = 5 × max(manifest Berger band, highest Undertow-linked > Talent level)

  • Since every receiver has Telepathy Talent ≥ 1 (013-the-berger-scale), every receiver has a floor of at least 5 — there are no clean receivers, only quiet ones.
  • A never-manifested high-Talent latent carries a real floor and rolls monthly against it: civilians who go quietly mad, hearing voices, without ever manifesting — a canonical case-genesis JDIT will misfile, since there is no manifest power to flag.
  • The rolls machinery (monthly Will at -1 per full 10, conversion per 55-horror §Effects of Corruption, 25-point single-gain surge rolls) grinds on the floor forever: estrangement is a machine, not a narration.
  • Conversion clamps at the floor (ruled 2026-08-24): the RAW rule that converting Corruption reduces the running total applies only to the above-floor margin. A psi who keeps using their powers is never done being subject to corruption.

Consequences at the table

  • 012-power-estranges's "growing very powerful changes the receiver" is now an is, not a risk — no discipline keeps a strong psi clean.
  • Canon 013's monitoring thresholds (clinically notable ~B6, career review ~B8) should fall out of the floor math once calibrated — tune the formula so they do.
  • Advancement decisions price alienation in: raising your band raises your floor, permanently.
  • The latent-madness genesis quietly seeds cases for JDIT files and ties the civilian world to the metaplot without any manifest event.

Alternatives rejected

  • No floor — rejected: a careful psi could stay clean forever, contradicting canon 012.
  • Fold Talent into the band table (count Undertow Talent as ability points, floor = 5 × resulting band) — declined for now in favor of simplicity; revisit at calibration.
  • Two-term sum (5 × B + k × Talent) — declined for now; most tunable, but a second dial to maintain.

Rules basis

  • Corruption effects machinery: 55-horror §Effects of Corruption (monthly cadence and 25-point surge trigger are house adjustments to the RAW weekly roll).
  • The floor itself, its formula, and the Undertow-linked Talent rule are house rules.

How the players can learn it

The monitoring regime's actuarial tables; a latent case file with no manifest history and a familiar decline curve; their own sheets, slowly.

Retcon cost

Low until character creation; the formula is explicitly provisional. The existence of the floor becomes expensive to retract the moment any latent-madness case is played.

Canon decision 020

Canon 020: The door opens from inside

Date: 2026-08-24 Status: Accepted Revealed to players: partial — the instinct itself is universal knowledge among receivers; the pricing and JDIT's flagging practice are not.

Context

The Corruption design pass (018-corruption-is-alienation, 019-loudness-sets-a-floor): can psis voluntarily draw on Corruption for power?

Decision

Any psi can open themselves to the Undertow for power — and almost everyone instinctively knows how. The maneuver takes one extra second (an additional Concentrate maneuver), then the power simply arrives: no FP cost, no extra-effort roll. It is mechanically a dark variant of psi extra effort (53-psionic-powers §Getting tricky) and deliberately far more effective than the legitimate version — that is what makes it tempting.

Two modes, both priced in dice of Corruption, not fixed points — the gamble is the point; deterministic costs would read as a fee, not a risk:

  • Skill boost — bonuses to the psionic skill roll (RAW: +1 per point, 55-horror §Extending Corruption to other abilities).
  • Level-borrowing — one-time use of ability levels not owned (RAW: 1/5 the CP cost of the additional levels, rounded up).

Conversion (TEMPORARY): every ~3.5 points of RAW Corruption cost becomes 1d6 — pure statistical equivalence; find a more idiomatic GURPS expression at calibration.

Borrowed power ignores the Berger ceiling (013-the-berger-scale). A documented C3 can perform C6 work — for a price.

Critical failures on any psi use (all powers, Undertow-linked or not) are the involuntary version: the channel flaps open with nobody holding the door. Corruption gain — a flat die (size TBD at calibration) — is the new default critical-failure special effect, replacing GM improvisation as the baseline; strange manifestations and other punishments may stack on top, but Corruption always accrues.

Consequences at the table

  • Every desperate moment is a devil's bargain the player strikes with themselves — the oppression is interactive, not just ambient.
  • JDIT tracks above-ceiling manifestation as a standing red flag for all agents: receivers repeatedly performing above assay are presumed to be going bad. Assay error (canon 013) is bounded at one band, so mismeasurement innocently explains only modest overshoots — repeated multi-band performances have no innocent explanation JDIT knows of. The dossier ambiguity lives in the one-band margin (and, unknown to everyone, in the astral corpus — 024-the-astral-corpus).
  • ANSCOM's institutional stance on the maneuver (banned? trained against? quietly tolerated in the field?) is deliberately open — it belongs with the "more JDIT shade" thread.
  • Added 2026-08-24: door-opened (and borrowed/stolen) power is what reaches the -30 uber-technique tier no unaided human touches — 030-the-impossible-tier-is-real.

Alternatives rejected

  • Gated access (learned/forbidden technique) — rejected: instinct universalizes the temptation and fits the openness model; the door is part of being a receiver, not a secret.
  • Deterministic costs — rejected: a known price is a budget line, not a gamble.
  • Ceiling-capped borrowing — rejected: exceeding your own biology is the temptation's sharpest edge and JDIT's cleanest tell.

Rules basis

  • Boost/borrow pricing: 55-horror §Extending Corruption to other abilities (psionic rows).
  • Legitimate extra effort, for contrast: 53-psionic-powers §Getting tricky.
  • The opening maneuver, dice conversion, ceiling exemption, and crit-fail default are house rules.

How the players can learn it

They already know — their characters grew up with the instinct. What they learn at the table is the price curve, and what the monitoring regime does to agents who get flagged.

Retcon cost

Low until a PC opens the door on camera; the JDIT flagging practice becomes fixed once any dossier or NPC references it.

Context

The Corruption design pass: does Corruption cleanse (55-horror §Cleansing Corruption), given the "hopeless in the long run" tone anchor and the uncleansable floor (019-loudness-sets-a-floor)?

Decision

Above-floor Corruption recedes only through sustained, deliberate, mundane humanity — ordinary life, human connection, no exposure. The penance-shadow, secularized. Cleansing runs in cycles (length and rate TBD at calibration), and any use of psionics resets the current cycle's progress to zero. Not psi failure — psi use. The door must stay shut.

Alternatives rejected

  • Nothing cleanses — rejected: removes recovery arcs and the desperate search for a way back; hopelessness reads strongest when hope exists and is expensive.
  • RAW Meritorious Act / Penance as written — theology doesn't port; their secular shadows (sustained humanity, mandated leave) do.

Rules basis

  • Cleansing framework: 55-horror §Cleansing Corruption (heavily adapted; the reset trap and cycle structure are house rules).

How the players can learn it

Mandated-leave doctrine; a veteran who got better and won't say how; a veteran who tried, slipped once, and stopped trying.

Canon decision 023

Canon 023: Retirement is a needle

> Cross-ref (added 2026-08-24, S000-worldbuilding-options): the > needle has an uncontrolled natural analogue — astral cord > severance, which violently halts the liminosomes and damages the > nervous system; the injection is the engineered version of a > catastrophe the Undertow can inflict on its own.

The Corruption design pass, cleansing branch — the surgical shadow of 55-horror's "cut it off" ritual, crossed with canon 012's dangling question: ANSCOM careers "end at thresholds, quietly." This is how quietly.

Decision

JDIT possesses a drug (or retrovirus) that disables the liminosome — and kills a substantial fraction of the neurons carrying it. An injection is mandatory for anyone leaving ANSCOM. It is the only form of retirement ANSCOM allows.

Mechanical effects, all permanent and unrecoverable:

  • All psionic abilities and Talents are lost outright. No refund, no recovery. Beyond crippling.
  • The Corruption floor drops to zero — the openness is destroyed.
  • No further Corruption rolls, ever. The running total goes inert: carried, but no longer working.

Ex-agents sleep dark.

Injected retirees are no longer viable as PCs. If they appear in play at all, it is to remind the players of the grim cost of doing the work. Those who leave ANSCOM alive always leave it as less than what they were when they came in.

  • Joining ANSCOM is a one-way door, and every operative is walking toward the needle by one road or another: convert, cross the line, or retire diminished. The org's greyness (Open Threads "more JDIT shade") is now structural, not incidental.
  • The monitoring thresholds (013-the-berger-scale: B6 notable, B8 career review; canon 012's "careers end at thresholds, quietly") get their endpoint: career review at B8 is not a euphemism for a desk.
  • Ex-agents are a distinctive NPC class: dimmed, marked by their old disadvantages, deaf to the broadcast — and possibly bitter about all three.
  • Recruits know the policy, not the price (ruled 2026-08-24): "decommissioning" is disclosed at signing as a mandatory, classified, safe-sounding medical procedure. The IQ/DX damage, the diminishment, the death sentence for advanced cases — none of it. You learn what it costs the first time you meet a decommissioned veteran. Field slang ruled 2026-08-25 (two-register pass): "getting dimmed" / PT "ser apagado" (Decommissioning).

Alternatives rejected

  • Corruption-targeted psychosurgery (excise the taint, keep the psi) — rejected: a return trip would make the horror negotiable. The needle removes the capacity, not the stain; the stain stays.
  • Retirement by surveillance (let them go, watch them) — rejected as the only policy; nothing prevents it existing informally for assets too valuable to dim, which is more JDIT shade to mine.

Rules basis

  • Attribute damage and trait loss are house rules; the "cut it off" precedent: 55-horror §Cleansing Corruption.
  • Liminosome biology: 005-the-world-is-dualistic, 013-the-berger-scale (assay = liminosome-bearing neuron count).

How the players can learn it

An ex-agent NPC who used to be brilliant; the exit-interview room with a medical chair in it; the moment a PC first asks "so how do I quit?"

Retcon cost

Low until revealed — but this is a tentpole of the setting's horror; treat as fixed once any character contemplates leaving on screen.

  • The retiree who still dreams (ruled 2026-08-24, ability-curation pass): the needle ends dreaming (023-retirement-is-a-needle, amended) — but an astral-corpus human's liminosomes are on the other side, so they keep dreaming after injection. A quiet, clean tell for the nightmare file — if anyone thinks to ask a retiree about their sleep.

A second, deeper source of "impossible performance" alongside assay error and the open door (020-the-door-opens-from-inside). Unlike canon 013's one-band error bound, this failure mode is unbounded — and nobody knows it exists.
  • Extends 005-the-world-is-dualistic: the dualism runs through biology itself — some anatomy is in the Undertow.
  • How an astral corpus arises in a human (birth? Undertow trauma? the Gardener's doing?) is deliberately undefined — plot inventory.

Alternatives rejected

  • Infallible injection — rejected: an airtight institutional control leaves no crack for horror to get in through.
  • Known-to-JDIT rare resistance — rejected: JDIT ignorance is the sharper edge; their confidence in the needle is part of the institutional lie.

Rules basis

They almost certainly shouldn't, until the case file that proves it.

Canon decision 025

Canon 025: Corruption is openness

Date: 2026-08-24 Status: Accepted Revealed to players: partial — operatives know the LER procedure as routine monitoring; what it actually measures is not disclosed.

Context

The Corruption design pass, detection branch: who — and what — can see Corruption (018-corruption-is-alienation)?

Decision

Corruption is openness. The more corrupted a psi, the more sensitive they become to Undertow signals — and, almost always, the more they emit on the same wavelength. Reception and emission are coupled.

Instruments: the openness assay

("Openness" is GM-side shorthand; the in-world clinical term is the LER — liminon-evoked response (GLOSSARY), and drift from baseline is "LER drift.")

JDIT measures openness with fMRI coupled to a liminon-emitting device — a hearing test with a different signal: emit, watch which brain areas light up, grade the sensitivity. (In theory you could just ask the psi "did you feel that?" — in practice JDIT and ANSCOM do not trust their agents enough to run it on self-report.) This is the concrete procedure behind canon 012's "liminon-signature baselining."

JDIT knows openness can indicate growing corruption — especially marked increases. But the LER is an indicator, never a diagnosis. The corruption signal is a marked change in profile — especially toward certain wavelengths — and it is confounded: growing skill as a telepath or astral projector also yields better LER results. A drifting profile is therefore one data point among several (psych evals, physicals, time in service) that ANSCOM weighs when deciding between mandatory leave (022-alienation-can-recede) and permanent decommissioning (023-retirement-is-a-needle). A naturally sensitive psi and a corrupting one look identical in absolute terms (019-loudness-sets-a-floor — loudness is baseline openness); etiology never shows; totals stay GM-secret per 55-horror. The diagnostic fog of 012-power-estranges survives measurement.

Perception is embodied and idiosyncratic

Each psi feels the Undertow's influence differently, set by where their liminon-sensitive neurons concentrate: one smells emotions, another sees them, another knows them abstractly. Powers should usually feel embodied — presentation rule for narrating psi at the table.

Living psis

Almost never perceivable. Very sensitive, very skilled telepaths or astral projectors MIGHT read a specific receiver as unusually open to signals that feel wrong — rare and unformalized: the felt read is not institutional knowledge anywhere in JDIT. (Instrumental wavelength detection is another matter — see below.)

The Undertow side

The emission is how the Gardener senses the corrupted: its seeds are very powerful sensors spread around the world (002-psi-is-the-gardeners-cultivation, sharpened). It reads wavelength alignment easily; for a human psi the same read takes extraordinary capability.

What JDIT knows on this channel: certain broadcasts echo through the Undertow; it suspects some of them are malign; and it can sometimes detect a psi emitting on those wavelengths — unreliably, and more easily the more powerful and less controlled the psi. This instrumental capability is as far as institutional knowledge goes.

Some very rare psis only receive — corrupted, tuned to it, and leaving no Undertow footprint. The emission channel cannot find them; their entire risk surface is behavioral. These are the Enemy's natural sleeper agents (The Blight) — not a coordinated cell (the Gardener cannot yet run one, 015-the-gardener-is-primal): its will simply becomes theirs, unconsciously, and their actions converge without any of them knowing the others exist.

Consequences at the table

  • Monitoring scenes get a physical procedure: the tube, the emitter, the tech reading the bloom on the scan. An operative hiding growth (012-power-estranges) is now hiding it from a machine.
  • The Blight's sleepers are not caught, they are churned: an erratic sleeper gets removed as a misbehaver or a Berger-syndrome case, never identified as enemy influence. The Gardener needs no roster — it made them, and its broadcast is one-way. From outside, convergence is indistinguishable from conspiracy.
  • Deep-corruption veterans are walking sensor arrays: they hear more than clean psis, which makes the corrupted genuinely better at parts of the job. The blessing again (015-the-gardener-is-primal).
  • Embodied qualia give every psi NPC a sensory signature worth one line in their note.

Alternatives rejected

  • Corruption invisible to instruments — rejected: monitoring needs teeth for the paranoia to bite.
  • A true corruption-meter — rejected: cited against the Open Threads ruling that the formal register's confidence is an institutional lie; a real meter would make JDIT actually able to tell.

Rules basis

  • Aura-visibility precedent adapted: 55-horror §Effects of Corruption. Reaction penalties from the RAW aura rule are not used; perception is per the rules above.

How the players can learn it

Their own monitoring sessions; a veteran's "something's wrong about him" that turns out true; the case where a screen came back clean and shouldn't have.

Retcon cost

Moderate once monitoring scenes are played — the assay procedure becomes table texture fast.

Canon decision 026

Canon 026: The end of the line is a seed

Date: 2026-08-24 Status: Accepted (threshold number deferred to calibration) Revealed to players: no.

Context

The Corruption design pass, terminal branch: what full corruption is (55-horror's 125-point forced conversion), converging with the crossed-threshold-operatives thread and canon 016's substrate rule.

Decision

A point of no return exists; its number does not yet — pinned at calibration with the rest of the provisional values.

The terminal trajectory: the person goes catatonic for a time, then slowly begins to mutate. If the body retains enough energy, the mutations pull it toward a seed — a fleshy growth of tumorous appearance. The Gardener's seed network is self-propagating: its terminal victims become its new sensing organs (025-corruption-is-openness).

Era-gated conservation: when the Gardener grows sentient — even sapient (016-its-mind-grows-with-its-thralls) — it becomes able to conserve taken bodies un-mutated and run them as discreet agents. Today it cannot; its finished works are lumps, not people. (GM-side inference, not ruled: this may be what "vessel" readiness means — the GLOSSARY's vessel goal requires the conservation capability, so the current era's terminal cases are all failures by the Gardener's own standard.)

What JDIT knows: advanced Undertow openness sometimes triggers physical mutations — cancer-like at first, then taking on a subtly different signature. They know the mutations are the end of the line. If they decide on mercy, the decommissioning injection (023-retirement-is-a-needle) is usually a death sentence at this stage: severed from the Undertow's proto-regulation, the body crumbles under the weight of its abnormal growths. Jargon ruled 2026-08-25 (two-register pass): the clinical term is liminic neoplasia; there is deliberately no field word (Liminic neoplasia).

Consequences at the table

  • Every terminal case ANSCOM fails to contain is future enemy infrastructure — the case queue's stakes quietly compound.
  • Body-collection doctrine (014-anscom-salvages-its-dead) acquires a second, unintended meaning: ANSCOM is either interrupting seed-formation or concentrating it in a vault — feeding the standing amplifier-convergence ambiguity (Open Threads). Do not resolve casually.
  • The Berger-syndrome clinical picture gets its final stage: rage or eerie calm, then silence, then growth. A hospice ward ANSCOM doesn't put in writing.
  • "Mercy" is now a genuinely grey verb: the needle at this stage kills, and everyone administering it knows.
  • Late-era discreet agents are a second infiltration class alongside The Blight's receive-only corrupted — one the campaign should meet only after the players have learned the old rules.

Alternatives rejected

  • Hard RAW 125-point line now — deferred, not rejected: players who count conversions can reverse-engineer a known number; the calibration pass decides where and how visible the line is.
  • Terminal state as death or simple NPC-villainy — rejected: the setting's horror is transformation with a function; the enemy wastes nothing.

Rules basis

  • Forced-conversion precedent: 55-horror §Effects of Corruption (125+). Everything else is house canon.

How the players can learn it

A containment that arrives too late and finds the growth; the medical annex where the merciful injections happen; an old seed that, examined closely, has dental work.

Retcon cost

Low until a terminal case is shown; the seed-propagation rule is load-bearing for the metaplot's escalation math — treat as fixed once any seed's origin is revealed.

Canon decision 027

Canon 027: Everyone is broadcasting

Date: 2026-08-24 Status: Accepted Revealed to players: partial — operatives live the training and the silence; the wavelength structure underneath is not disclosed.

Context

Adopting the PSC "world broadcasts loudly" insert (54-psionic-campaigns §Telepaths and mental screens, the on-the-world variant) after the Corruption pass settled the wavelength model (025-corruption-is-openness).

Decision

Every civilian broadcasts their thoughts, all the time — more intensely the stronger the emotion. Mechanically: Easy to Read (Psychic Only, +0%) is part of the standard human template; assume every NPC has it unless bought off. It counts against no disadvantage limit, per the PSC guidance.

The buy-off is ANSCOM training, granted free. Part of the training ANSCOM gives prospective agents is learning to shut off the noise of the crowds — and non-psi ANSCOM personnel get the same training: in an agency whose enemies include telepaths, being unreadable is basic opsec. PCs have the buy-off at no point cost. Civilians at large are stuck with the trait — they don't know there is anything to train.

Screens slip. Keeping the screen up is an acquired habit, not a switch: temporary degradation of cognitive capacity — especially prefrontal function, from any source (exhaustion, alcohol, sedation, concussion, terror) — lowers the screen's effectiveness or drops it entirely. Some very experienced people have the habit so ingrained it cannot drop.

Wavelength separation. Human minds broadcast on a much "shorter wavelength" (metaphorically) than the deep-Undertow signals that openness tunes to. Consequences:

  • Corruption does not amplify crowd noise — the corrupted are more sensitive to signals from deeper in the Undertow, not to the surface chatter of minds.
  • This is why mundane minds are dim to the Gardener (015-the-gardener-is-primal): it listens on the deep band, and the crowd's noise never carries there. Receivers are loud where it lives (019-loudness-sets-a-floor); civilians are loud only in the room.

The relief is part of the hook. Recruits whose telepathy manifested before recruitment lived immersed in the murmur; ANSCOM's training is the first silence of their adult lives. The org knows this — the gift of quiet helps it sell itself as genuinely concerned with its operatives' well-being, and the gratitude is a loyalty instrument it doesn't have to fake.

Consequences at the table

  • Untrained and rogue telepaths are people drowning: crowds are torment, riots and panics are deafening. Sympathy and horror in the same case file — and a texture for why self-taught receivers (013-the-berger-scale's crutch class) are strange before they are dangerous.
  • Emotion scales volume: a telepath reads a poker table poorly and a panicking mob overwhelmingly. Crowd scenes have a psychic weather report.
  • Trained silence is itself a signature (ruled): to a psi who knows what to listen for, an ANSCOM-trained mind in a civilian crowd is a hole in the noise — and very experienced rogue psis know it, using the silence as a tell to spot and avoid ANSCOM and its agents. The hunters' opsec is the prey's tripwire.
  • A slipped screen is a security breach: whatever degrades cognition makes an operative readable again — and an adversary who knows that can arrange the degradation. Hospitals, bars, and interrogation rooms are all soft points.
  • Session-0 texture for the pre-mades: every PC remembers the day the noise stopped.

Alternatives rejected

  • On-the-telepath implementation (Reflexive +40% / Uncontrollable -10% passive reception, PSC's other variant) — declined as the world-rule; may still model untrained NPC telepaths' misery mechanically when needed.
  • Buy-off priced in points — originally rejected; superseded 2026-08-24 (S000-worldbuilding-options): the structure is now UB (trained silence) [5] = permission to buy off Easy to Read (Psychic Only) [-10], with the buy-off itself point-priced. The spirit stands — every PC receives the UB and the 10 buyback points free in the starting package. A PC may legally refuse the buyback and keep the 10 points; in-fiction this earns ridicule for the lack of discipline. Rival psi-agency training grants equivalent UBs under other names._
  • Corruption-scaled crowd noise (cities unbearable for the deeply corrupted) — rejected: wrong wavelength; openness points down, not around.

Rules basis

  • Easy to Read + Psychic Only (+0%), template placement, buy-off and no-limit guidance: 54-psionic-campaigns §Telepaths and mental screens (PSC p.27).
  • Free buy-off via ANSCOM training (including mundane personnel) is a house rule.

How the players can learn it

They lived it: the training montage, the first quiet crowd. The other side arrives with the first rogue telepath who never got the gift — or the first time a target's panic gets loud enough to hurt.

Retcon cost

Low until played; the silence texture and the drowning-rogue trope become table furniture fast once cases establish them.

Context

The ability-curation chore (Open Threads): the campaign uses a curated closed list (S000-worldbuilding-mechanics A7). This pass walked the full Psionic Powers catalog and the PSC p.11 structural checklist, power by power, veto/approve. The complete per-ability tally lives in S000-worldbuilding-ability-curation — that note is the authoritative appendix to this record.

Eleven powers exist: Anti-Psi (NPC-only), Astral Projection, Biokinesis, Ergokinesis, ESP, Probability Alteration, Psychic Vampirism, Psychokinesis, Psychometabolism, Telepathy, Teleportation.

Three PP powers do not exist as such:

  • Psychic Healing — split into Biokinesis (others' bodies) and Psychometabolism (own body). The gruesome-healing mandate governs the Cure family.
  • Dream Control — folded into Astral Projection (dreams touch the Undertow; 009-the-undertow-is-peopled as amended).
  • Animal Telepathy — world-ruled away: almost no animals carry liminosome-bearing neurons, so fauna are simply not valid Telepathy targets. Exception: seed-changed animals (a local subspecies, occasionally an entire very local species). JDIT knows a little and spends nearly nothing on it.

Player-exclusive choices: Telekinetic Control or the split TK abilities, never both; Telespeak or Telesend/Telereceive, never both.

Talent gates (thresholds pinned at calibration): Levitation, Autoteleport (significant distance = incredible feat), Regeneration, Regrowth — all meant to be very rare and impressive.

Machines: computers are not minds — no liminosome, no Undertow presence. Cyberpsi reads/steers electrical states at a Complexity penalty; PP's digital-minds rule is void. Useful digital I/O (storing, decoding, complex control, digital taps, Phreaker) requires the cyberpsi implant, a neural digital-I/O translator (JDIT psi-tech; provenance and access open).

Anti-Psi stays as written but is NPC-only. ANSCOM runs two tiers of anti-psi personnel: rare unattached agents assigned against powerful psionic threats, and a secret internal investigation/elimination squad most of JDIT does not know exists.

Additional-build frameworks (Biokinesis/Psychometabolism advantage builds): allowed in principle, every specific build needs GM approval.

Consequences at the table

  • Char-gen has a definitive menu; the closed-list tracker chore is satisfied except for calibration numbers.
  • The vetoes are world-facts, not just balance calls: no Netrunning and the implant gate keep human minds and machines ontologically separate; the no-life-energy ruling keeps the horror mental.
  • The elimination squad is standing JDIT shade and a late-campaign landmine (The Blight adjacency).

Rules basis

  • Catalog and options: 53-psionic-powers; checklist: 54-psionic-campaigns §1.8 (PSC p.11).
  • Gates, the implant, the ontology ruling, and NPC-only Anti-Psi are house rules.

How the players can learn it

The menu itself at char-gen; the world-reasons only through play (a seed-changed dog, a decommissioned netrunner-rumor that goes nowhere, the colleague who is never assigned twice).

Retcon cost

Moderate at char-gen, high after: every sheet built on this list is a commitment. The ontology rulings (no life energy, mindless machines) are load-bearing for future canon — treat as fixed.

Canon decision 030

Canon 030: The impossible tier is real

Date: 2026-08-24 Status: Accepted Revealed to players: no — the tier is GM-side dread inventory; no PC has seen an uber-technique effect or been told the class exists.

Context

The PSC p.11 options sitting (S000-worldbuilding-options) reached item #12: PP's "uber-techniques" — Permanent Effect, Lethal Attack, and Omnicast, each defaulting at psi skill -30 and written for cinematic campaigns expecting skill 25–30 (53-psionic-powers §Cinematic psis and uber-techniques). A 150+50-point lethal procedural never reaches that tier, so "off" looked free — except the campaign had already minted one such technique (the anti-corruption Mental Surgery variant at ~-30, "godlike-feat territory", S000-worldbuilding-ability-curation §Telepathy), and the setting already contains performance beyond human reach (020-the-door-opens-from-inside).

Decision

The -30 technique tier exists in the world, and no unaided human reaches it. Baseline psis — PCs included, at any realistic skill — cannot buy or attempt uber-techniques. But power that does not come from the psi alone can get there: door-opened performance (020-the-door-opens-from-inside), borrowed power, and stolen power (the Psychic Vampirism adjudication note, S000-worldbuilding-ability-curation). A Gardener-fed thrall or deeply tuned receiver may exhibit uber-technique effects on screen.

The anti-corruption Mental Surgery technique (~-30 default, any failure treated as critical) is the first member of this class, not a one-off house number.

Held in reserve: Omnicast beats Anti-Psi. RAW, Omnicast affects subjects with Immunity to Psi and Psi Static — meaning something at this tier can reach through the elimination squad's screens. That beat is spent deliberately or not at all.

Consequences at the table

  • The tier is a legibility horizon: JDIT's models of what psi can do are calibrated on human performance. An uber-technique effect on a case is evidence of the door, not of skill — the players can learn to read it that way (013-the-berger-scale's assay worldview breaks down exactly here).
  • Permanent Effect gives the Gardener's side a signature atrocity: edits that never heal, curses with no recovery roll.
  • Anti-Psi personnel are safe from everything except the things this canon exists to describe — the elimination squad's confidence is well-founded right up until it isn't.
  • ANSCOM cleanup doctrine does not depend on this tier: RAW Mindwipe permanence (an hour at -4) covers wipes.
  • Depends on / props up: 020-the-door-opens-from-inside, 022-alienation-can-recede (the Mental Surgery ruling), 028-the-closed-list (the tier is outside the list's economy).

Alternatives rejected

  • Off entirely — rejected: it would orphan the Mental Surgery ~-30 ruling and discard a clean mechanical expression of what door-opened power looks like on screen.
  • On as written (available to anyone with the skill) — rejected: flatly misfits the campaign's power level and lethality (001-table-agreements).

Rules basis

  • Uber-technique write-ups: 53-psionic-powers §Cinematic psis and uber-techniques (PP p.76).
  • Restricting the tier to door-opened/borrowed/stolen power is a house rule; the KB offers only "extremely optional" as guidance.

How the players can learn it

The hard way: a case where the damage does not heal, the wipe does not fade, or the anti-psi escort goes down mid-sentence. Formal disclosure never happens — JDIT does not have this tier in its manuals.

Retcon cost

Low today (nothing revealed). Rises sharply the first time an uber-technique effect appears on screen — after that, the tier is table furniture and "unaided humans can't" is the load-bearing half.

They are independent sleepers whose tuning makes the entity's primal wants their own, unconsciously (025-corruption-is-openness); their actions converge without coordination, and from outside, convergence is indistinguishable from conspiracy. The PCs must uproot the rot — inside the one organization whose job is to notice exactly this, and which therefore cannot admit it happened.

Stakes

Left alone, the sleepers steer cases: containments that fail on schedule, strong rogues who slip away, salvage-eligible bodies that reach the amplifier program (014-anscom-salvages-its-dead). The Gardener gains eyes and hands inside its hunter — and every month the rot runs, the 016-its-mind-grows-with-its-thralls clock ticks faster.
